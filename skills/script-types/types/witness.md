# Type: transformation with a witness

Someone else notices the change before the speaker does. Proof carries the script.

- **Opens with:** the witness. Used up: "My sister asked what I'd changed. I hadn't told her anything." Kept: "The man at the next pitch asked what pan that was."
- **Person and tense:** 1st person, past. Or 3rd person about a described customer.
- **Beat order:** the witness moment (hook and proof), back to how things were (problem, the accommodation), what changed and why (promise, then mechanism), what they'd tried before (objection), wider proof (a count, a third party), offer, risk reversal, call to action, identity line.
- **Carried by:** proof. The witness is a named kind of person (a sister, a colleague, a stranger at a campsite) and what they said or did is specific.
- **The product arrives:** in the middle, as what changed between then and now.
- **Not the same as:** `demonstration`, where the camera is the witness and no account is needed.
- **Watch for:** this type needs a true account. With a real customer's words in the research, build on them, word for word where quoted. With none, write it as a brief for a real customer to film: placeholders for who noticed and what they said, listed, never filled in. A generated presenter can't claim this. The outcome described must be one the brand's guardrails allow.
