# Type: discovery

Someone finds out why the problem happens, and it changes what they do. The mechanism carries the script, told as news.

- **Opens with:** the finding, as a turn. Used up: "Here's what I didn't know. Coffee isn't bitter. Hot water makes it bitter." Kept: "Here's what I didn't know. Jackets don't leak through the fabric. They leak at the seams."
- **Person and tense:** 1st or 3rd person, past for the finding, present for how it works. A brand can tell it in 3rd person: "somebody worked out why this happens".
- **Beat order:** hook (the finding, or the question it answers), the problem as it looked before they knew, the mechanism explained plainly, the promise it makes possible, why what they'd tried couldn't work (objection, answered by the mechanism), proof, offer, risk reversal, call to action, identity line.
- **Carried by:** the mechanism: 1 cause, taught in 2 to 4 plain sentences, with 1 picture.
- **The product arrives:** after the mechanism is understood, as what acts on it.
- **Not the same as:** `three-things`, which lists parts; this has 1 cause, and a before and after of understanding. `category-callout` blames what the category does; this explains why the problem happens.
- **Watch for:** a discovery that is really a feature list. The cause has to be true and the brand's to state: a brand's own finding is not presented as independent science.
