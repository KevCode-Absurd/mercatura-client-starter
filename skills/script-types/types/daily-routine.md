# Type: a daily routine

The product inside an ordinary day. Nothing is argued: the day is shown, and the product is 1 small part of it.

- **Opens with:** a moment of the day already under way. Used up: "6:50. The kettle is cold and you're already drinking it." Kept: "It lives on the hook by the door, so it's the one you take out with the dog."
- **Person and tense:** 2nd person, present, or plain narration. 1st person only in a brief a real founder, customer or creator films, about their own day.
- **Beat order:** hook (a moment of the day), what that part of the day used to take (problem, 1 line), the product used where it falls, why that is easy (mechanism, 1 sentence), a later moment where it is no longer thought about (promise, shown), offer, call to action.
- **Carried by:** the use, in its place. Small true details persuade: where it is kept, what it sits next to, what gets skipped because of it.
- **The product arrives:** early, with no introduction. Its name can wait for the offer.
- **By how it is bought:** used up, the routine is the daily use and the minute it takes. Kept, it is where the thing lives and when it is reached for without thinking.
- **Not the same as:** `demonstration`, 1 continuous action that proves what the product does. This is several moments of 1 day and proves only that it fits.
- **Watch for:** a day nobody has. Take the hour, the place and the steps from how the brand and its customers say it is used. Show the use, never its effect on a body. A generated presenter is a model, not a customer: tell it to "you" or as narration, with no streak or result of their own. A real person's routine is a brief they film. This type breaks the usual order on purpose.
