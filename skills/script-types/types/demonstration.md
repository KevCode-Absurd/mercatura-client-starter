# Type: demonstration

Shows the thing working, first. Proof leads and the argument follows.

- **Opens with:** the demonstration under way, no introduction. Used up: "1 part this, 2 parts milk, ice. 15 seconds." Kept: "10 minutes under a garden hose. Now look inside."
- **Person and tense:** 2nd person or plain narration, present.
- **Beat order:** hook (the demonstration), what you just saw and why it matters (promise), why it does that (mechanism), who this is for (1 line of problem), the doubt answered (objection), a second proof from outside (count or third party), offer, risk reversal, call to action. At 15 seconds: the demonstration, what you saw, the offer, the action. The demonstration is the proof even where the beat table gives proof no room.
- **Carried by:** proof, shown. The top rung of the proof ladder, and available to a brand with no reviews.
- **The product arrives:** immediately. It is on screen from the first frame; its name can still come later.
- **By how it is bought:** kept, the thing is put through something (water, heat, weight, a wash). Used up, with an edge nobody can see, the demonstration is the use: the pour, the texture, the 1 step. Never its effect on a body.
- **Not the same as:** `witness` (a person's account) and `how-its-made` (the making, not the using).
- **Watch for:** only what really happens and can be filmed with the real product, as 1 continuous action. No result the guardrails rule out. This type breaks the usual order on purpose.
