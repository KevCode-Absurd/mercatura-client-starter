# Type: category call-out

Names what the whole category gets wrong, then shows the sharper version. For buyers who already know the kind of solution and are tired of it.

- **Opens with:** the category's flaw, as a fact, seen from the shelf. Used up: "Most cold brew is brewed hot and chilled." Kept: "Most rain jackets are tested as fabric, not as a jacket."
- **Person and tense:** 2nd or 3rd person, present. The brand says "we" only from the offer on.
- **Beat order:** hook (the flaw), why it matters to the buyer (what they bought and what happened), the promise, the same thing done properly (the dose, the form, the material, the test), proof (third party first), the objection "they all say that" answered with a specific, offer, risk reversal, call to action.
- **Carried by:** a sharper mechanism: the one the category claims, with the detail the category leaves out.
- **The product arrives:** after the proper version is explained, as the one that does it.
- **Not the same as:** `how-its-made`, told from inside the workshop about the brand's own decision. This is told from the shelf, about everyone else's.
- **Watch for:** the flaw must be true and the brand able to back it. Call out kinds of product and practices, never a named competitor. The contrast is on a fact, not an adjective.
