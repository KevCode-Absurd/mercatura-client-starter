# Type: offer first

Leads with the terms. For buyers who know the product and need a reason to act.

- **Opens with:** the offer, specific. Used up: "The 2-bottle first box is back." Kept: "The 26 cm is back in stock, and it's the last run this year."
- **Person and tense:** 2nd person, present.
- **Beat order:** hook (the offer), why these terms (a reason tied to the product: "2 bottles because that's 4 weeks", "100 days because that's how long it takes to know"), the price in 1 frame, the reversal with its real terms, 1 line of proof, the reason for now, call to action. Problem and mechanism get 1 line at most.
- **Carried by:** the offer.
- **The product arrives:** in line 1. This type names it early on purpose.
- **By how it is bought:** used up: the first order, what it costs after, how to pause. Kept: 1 price against what it replaces or how long it lasts, and the returns.
- **Not the same as:** `price-of-the-problem`, which opens on what the buyer has already spent and is for people who don't know the product yet.
- **Watch for:** every number is the brand's real number. Urgency is real (a date, a quantity) or it is left out. No discount the offer doesn't state. Shown to a cold audience this type fails: it assumes the argument has already been made.
