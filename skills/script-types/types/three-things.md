# Type: three things

The mechanism broken into a short numbered list, with a question left open so people stay for the answer.

- **Opens with:** the count and the open question. Used up: "2 things make coffee bitter. The second is the one nobody fixes." Kept: "3 things decide whether a jacket keeps you dry. The label tells you 1."
- **Person and tense:** 2nd person, present.
- **Beat order:** hook (the count), 1 line of problem so they know it's for them, the promise, thing 1 (and why most miss it), thing 2, thing 3, the objection answered by one of the things ("which is why the last one half worked"), proof, the product as all of them in 1 (offer), risk reversal, call to action.
- **Carried by:** the mechanism, in parts. Each thing is 1 feature with its "which means". 2 or 3 things, never more.
- **The product arrives:** after the list, as the one that does all of them.
- **Not the same as:** `discovery` (1 cause, told as a story) and `timeline` (ordered by time; this is ordered by what matters most).
- **Watch for:** a list of features with no mechanism behind each. If any 2 items could swap, order them so they build. Each thing is true of this product. Pay off the open question.
