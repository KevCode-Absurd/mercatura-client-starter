# Paper world as a still image: 1 scene inside the paper

Judged on 1 thing: at the size of a thumb, a stranger sees a world made of real paper with 1 thing in it plainly wrong or plainly put right, and reads the headline once.

## The frame

- **1 moment.** For an unaware or problem-aware buyer: the problem as a paper scene at its worst. For a solution-aware buyer: the mechanism, 1 paper thing acting on another. For a buyer who knows the product: the product as hero of the changed scene.
- **The viewer stands inside the scene,** which runs past every edge of the frame. Choose 1 viewpoint: at a paper character's eye level, low and looking up, or straight down.
- **Depth:** a near layer, the subject, a far layer. The subject is the largest or brightest thing in frame.
- **1 look,** the job's preset. 1 paper character at most, a fictional adult by its proportions and dress.
- **The product:** where the ad shows it, front to the viewer and large enough to read. Where the ad leaves it out, the scene holds only the problem.

## Words on the picture

- 1 headline of 8 words or fewer, as clean type in the brand's own typeface on a quiet plane of the scene (a paper sky, a plain wall), in the top third. It says what the picture cannot: whose problem this is, or what just changed.
- 1 smaller line or a row of 2 or 3 chips at most, and a button only where there is an offer. 20 words in all.
- Type is laid flat over the picture. No letter is cut or folded from paper, and no sign in the scene carries words.
- Nobody in the scene speaks: no speech bubble.

## For the image prompt

- Order: the look's wording, the scene, the closing line.
- `papercraft` (default): "Layered papercraft world: everything built from stacked cardstock with visible paper thickness, deep layered depth between foreground, midground and background paper planes, layered cast shadows, paper quilling details, the camera inside the scene and the paper world extending past every edge of the frame."
- `cutout`: flat construction-paper cutout scene, simple flat card shapes, snipped edges, jointed paper puppets, soft drop shadows, bold flat colours.
- `handmade`: handmade paper stop-motion scene, torn edges, visible glue seams, hand-placed imperfection, soft practical light with falloff.
- `collage`: mixed-media collage composed as 1 deep scene: photo-cutout faces on hand-drawn paper bodies, patterned-paper clothes, cardboard and newsprint structures. The background is scenery, never a board.
- `origami`: everything folded with clean geometric creases, pleated details, soft pastel papers, soft diffused light that shows the folds.
- Closing line: "Everything in frame is real physical paper, photographed: visible paper grain and fibres, cut edges showing paper thickness, real cast shadows, soft studio lighting, the paper shot filling the entire frame."
- Name the paper on each main object ("a kettle snipped from blue card") and the camera's position inside the world.
- Colour: saturated, high in contrast, the palette named. Origami alone is soft pastel.
- Say "papercraft", never "paper cut", near a face or the product. Never "diorama", "shadow box", "tabletop" or "model": they show the craft from outside.
- The product is paper at its true shape and colours, each line of label copy given exactly. In `collage` it is its real photo, a cutout with rough scissor edges.
- Not a flat vector illustration, a framed box or a board on a table.

## What fails in a still

- A craft object photographed from outside, with a table edge or a frame in view.
- Several moments in 1 frame, with arrows between them.
- A change the brand's claims do not state.
