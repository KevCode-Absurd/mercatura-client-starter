# D2C page design system

Brand and brief win. Shopper pages persuade through the real product and buyer argument; the studio is a tool and must remain predictable. Preserve the supplied fonts, colors, products, claims, terms and approved scope. Design recommendations are hypotheses, not measured conversion gains.

Before rendering, commit to four to six EXISTING palette tokens and their roles; heading, body and action font roles; one dominant focal move; two to five layout principles; a 45–75ch long-prose measure; restrained motion and accessibility rules. Resolve colors/fonts from Brand Brain. Do not swap brand typography because a generic style guide dislikes it. No new global fonts, palette, scripts or dependencies are required.

Three directions differ in composition, image function and buyer-question sequence, not just color or headline wording. Carry the selected system through every section. Give each section a distinct question, clear grouping and purposeful spacing. Repeat grids for genuinely equivalent catalog items, not unrelated arguments. Use numbering only for real steps/reasons. Spend visual emphasis on one focal idea; keep supporting content quiet and readable.

Show the actual product/variant with accurate proportions. Use imagery to explain identity, use, scale or mechanism. Keep the ad's promise recognizable and early purchase/discovery access clear. Group actual price, essential variants and terms near the buying choice. Customer proof must be supplied and relevant; generated imagery and repeated packshots cannot manufacture proof.

Body text starts at 16px with comfortable leading; long prose uses the declared reading measure via .cro-prose. Tune type roles rather than making every heading huge. On mobile preserve the primary argument and useful image; on desktop deliberately reflow. Preserve logical DOM/focus order, readable terms, image detail, table access and zoom. Do not hide overflow to disguise a broken layout.

Technical floor: inspect actual text/surface contrast (4.5:1 ordinary text, 3:1 large text), visible keyboard focus, named controls, usable targets, clipping and responsive behavior. Aim for 44px purchase/disclosure controls. Respect reduced motion; no perpetual decorative animation or identical entrance effect on every section. Never assert a full accessibility pass from screenshots or limited browser checks.

Visual review separately judges hierarchy, product specificity, argument rhythm, imagery, reading density and brand fidelity. Cite the supplied frames and owning section. Contrast on photographs/gradients needs visual inspection; unavailable evidence stays unverified. Keep useful work, recommend localized repairs and preserve approved content. A technically clean page is still subject to design critique.
