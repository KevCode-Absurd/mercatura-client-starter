# Anime reference and video templates

Use these for manual prompting or to describe the same roles in the app's existing plan fields. Replace placeholders with real decisions; bind only attached files using the chosen route's actual tags. These are authored templates distilled from the supplied manga/anime prompter, video prompter, anime lesson and GHOST HAND breakdown, not provider capability claims. For state handoffs and a worked sequence, see [multi-clip continuity](multi-clip-continuity.md).

For scene-scoped reference exclusions, ownership, timed triggers and conditional edit/extension prompts, use [Video prompt architecture](../../production-principles/references/video-prompt-architecture.md). Retain the drawn style, current single-lead route and selected audio choices.

## Identity sheet

```text
Create an identity sheet for one original adult anime lead: [silhouette, face proportions, hair shape/colour, outfit, build, distinguishing accessory]. Use [selected line weight, palette, cel shadows and texture] consistently in six views: full figure front, three-quarter, side, back, face close-up and hands/prop detail. Neutral background, no captions. This sheet defines identity; its pose and backdrop do not define the film.
```

## Environment and starting composition

```text
Create a starting frame for an original 2D anime brand story. [IDENTITY TAG] controls the lead's design only. [PRODUCT TAG] controls accurate product shape, scale, colour and label artwork. The scene is [place]; [spatial anchors] establish foreground, midground and background. The lead is [position/gaze], the product is [position/state], and the obstacle is [position/distance]. Camera: [size, height, angle]. Light: [source/direction and cel-shadow shapes]. Use [selected visual language]. No unrequested panels, paper scan, captions or SFX lettering.
```

## Difficult action key frame

```text
Keep identity from [IDENTITY TAG], geography and palette from [SCENE TAG], product geometry from [PRODUCT TAG]. Previsualize the instant [specific grip, interaction, impact or reveal] occurs. State contact points, hand orientation, support, gaze and relative scale. [Camera and visible expression]. Keep the selected 2D line/shadow treatment. This is [an action target / a timed accent hold / the ending pose], not a pose to hold throughout the video.
```

## Timed video direction

```text
Duration: [accepted clip length]. Mode: [actually supported input mode]. Original 2D anime, [visual language and identity anchors]. References: [actual tags, separate roles, relevant exclusions and beat scope]. Initial geography and object states: [map, count and holder/support].
[start–end seconds] Camera: [one dominant behaviour, including locked]. Action: [trigger, anticipation, ordered contact/path and visible completion]. Light: [source/shadows]. Sound: [according to enabled sound/BGM controls]. VFX: [one motivated effect or none].
[start–end seconds] Camera: [setup]. Action: [consequence/reaction and product-led payoff]. Light: [same scene source]. Sound: [allowed cue or silence].
Approved dialogue: [exact words, narrator or hero]. Voice signature: [register, timbre, accent/rhythm]. This clip's delivery: [emotion and pacing]. Keep design, line weight, palette, cel shadows and product artwork stable. Deliberate pose-to-pose rhythm with secondary motion; no unrequested photoreal/3D treatment. End state: [objects, contact/support, pose, travel direction and persistent effects for the next opening]. No generated captions or SFX lettering.
```

Fill the accepted length with purposeful beats, not mandatory evenly divided shots. Use a clean cut between clips when continuation is unavailable. A supplied starting frame controls composition; a design reference alone does not.

## Optional Simplified Chinese handoff

If requested, translate production directions and field labels: 标题, 模式, 时长, 参考处理, 风格, 主体与场景, 镜头列表; each beat has 镜头, 动作, 光线, 声音, 特效. Preserve actual reference tags, brand/product names and exact approved dialogue in its intended language. Give an English explanation separately if wanted. Do not translate spoken copy silently or claim Chinese improves generation quality by itself.

## Local repair

```text
Preserve the selected style, identity, scene, framing, product and approved words. Correct only [visible defect] in [affected beat]. [One instruction controlling the defect: contact geometry, travel direction, shadow treatment or timing]. Keep unrelated choices fixed while comparing the same moment.
```
