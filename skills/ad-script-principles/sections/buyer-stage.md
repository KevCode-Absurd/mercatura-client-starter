# Buyer stage: awareness and sophistication

A script meets the buyer where they are. 2 things place them: what they know (awareness) and how tired they are of hearing it (sophistication). Both come from Schwartz. They are independent of each other.

## Awareness: what they know

| Stage | What they know | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Not that they have a problem | "You cook everything on medium. There's a reason." |
| Problem aware | The problem, not that answers exist | "If your face peeled the last time you tried retinol, this is for you." |
| Solution aware | That answers exist, not this product | "A coated pan can't be taken to full heat. That's the whole problem." |
| Product aware | This product, not yet convinced | Answers the 1 doubt left: the first week, the weight, the size |
| Most aware | The product, and close to buying | "The 2-tube starter is back." |

- Most people are unaware or problem aware; few are in market now. The colder the stage, the more the ad has to do and the lower the immediate return. It is also where the scale is, and why colder ads run longer.
- How the product is bought moves the weight. Something bought once is considered for longer: the same person stays product aware for weeks, comparing, so more of its ads answer 1 remaining doubt. Something bought again is tried sooner and judged in use, so more of its ads are about the first order.

## Sophistication: how tired they are

| Stage | The market | What still works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First to make the claim | The plain claim: "a jacket that keeps you dry" |
| 2 | Claims are common | A bigger or more exact claim: "dry through 8 hours of rain" |
| 3 | Tired of claims | A mechanism: "jackets leak at the seams first, so every seam is taped" |
| 4 | Everyone has a mechanism | A sharper mechanism: "taped by hand, and each one is hosed for 10 minutes before it ships" |
| 5 | Tired of mechanisms | Identity: "for people who stopped checking the forecast" |

A market sits mostly in 1 stage. An ad written at a higher stage than the market needs still does better than one written lower. Stage 2 claims are where compliance trouble starts.

## Message to market match

The stage is a property of the whole ad. A problem-aware script with a product-aware visual gives the buyer 2 starting points and the platform no clear person to find: the overlay, the hooks and the page sit at the script's stage. A set of ads spread across stages opens more of the market than many ads at one.
