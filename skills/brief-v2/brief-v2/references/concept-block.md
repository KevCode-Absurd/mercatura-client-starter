# Concept block: the template

The shape of every concept in a brief. Fill every field. The thinking block comes first; headlines come last. A field that is not known is written "inferred" or "not known", never filled with a guess that reads as a fact.

---

## The header of a brief

```markdown
# Creative brief: {Brand}, {YYYY-MM-DD}

**Rests on:** {measured: ad account, {dates} / reported by the founder / research / ad library only}
**Mode:** {PIVOT / ITERATE / SCALE / PROMO / NOT MEASURED}: {1 or 2 sentences, from the diagnosis, not from 1 number}
**Diagnosis:** the ads speak to {who}; the buyers are {who}. Stages with nothing running: {stages}. The belief in the way: {kind}.
**Product:** {used up / bought once}
**Stages in this brief:** {n} Trigger, {n} Exploration, {n} Evaluation, {n} Purchase
```

In NOT MEASURED mode add, as the next line: `Not measured: no concept here is proven.`

## A concept

```markdown
### Concept {N}: "{Name}"

> **Thinking block**
> - **Stage:** Trigger / Exploration / Evaluation / Purchase
> - **Awareness:** unaware / problem-aware / solution-aware / product-aware / most-aware
> - **The belief in the way:** "{what this buyer believes that stops the sale, in their words}" ({source: a quote, What almost stops them, or "inferred"})
> - **The belief to install:** "{what they believe after the ad}" (held up by: {the claim, the demonstration, the guarantee or the review that makes it true})
> - **Kind of belief:** outcome / identity / problem / solution / product / credibility
> - **The moment:** {where they are, what time it is, what they are doing when the problem shows} ({source, or "inferred"})
> - **Evidence tier:** {1 to 8}, {measured / reported / from research / inferred from an ad library}: {the fact it rests on}
> - **Opener (video only):** {1 of the 12}

**Product:** {name}, {price}
**Angle:** {1 or 2 sentences, from the buyer's side}
**Buyer:** {the avatar, and the situation they are in}
**Format:** {a format from the app's list: a still image, a person to camera, a demonstration, a carousel, a long story, and so on}

**3 variants. They differ on: {axis} and {axis}.**

**Variant A**
- Sub-persona: {who exactly}
- Feeling: {curiosity / relief / pride / belonging / fear of loss / ...}
- Awareness: {level}
- Opens with: {the opener, and the first thing seen}
- Layout: {headline only / headline and sub-headline / headline and points / all 3 / price / a customer's words / a number}
- Headline length: {short 3 to 5 / medium 5 to 7 / long 8 to 10}
- Tone: {direct / empathetic / doubt turned to belief / teaching / identity}
- Differs from B on: {2 axes}. Differs from C on: {2 axes}.
- Headline: {the exact words}
- Sub-headline: {only if the layout has one}
- Points: {up to 3, each what the buyer gets, 6 words or fewer}
- Customer's words: "{word for word}" ({first name or "verified buyer"}, {source}) or "quote needed"
- What is seen: {1 line: the product as its Reference photos show it, the place, the person, the light}
- Photo: `{file in assets/}`, or "to be made"
- Why this shape: {1 line}

**Variant B** {same fields}

**Variant C** {same fields}
```

## The footer

```markdown
**Copy check:** {what was rewritten, or "nothing"}
**Inferred in this brief:** {every field marked inferred, so a person can confirm or correct it}
**To ask the founder:** {question ids for the blanks, for example F5.9, F2.7}
```

## The fields

### The belief in the way, and the belief to install

The first is what the buyer already believes. The second is what a true thing about the product lets them believe instead. If nothing the brand can show makes the second one true, the concept is not ready.

| Product | The belief in the way | The belief to install | Held up by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A face cream, used up | "My skin just reacts to everything. Nothing will change that." | "It is the barrier that is worn down, and that can be rebuilt." | The ingredient list, a 28-day review |
| A snack bar, used up | "Anything with this much protein tastes like chalk." | "This one is eaten because it is good, and the protein is a bonus." | A taste reaction, the review count |
| A carbon-steel pan, bought once | "Good pans are fussy. I'll ruin it in a month." | "It takes 1 minute of care, and it gets better for 20 years." | A care demonstration, the warranty |
| A rain jacket, bought once | "At this price I can't risk the wrong size." | "If it does not fit, it goes back free and the right one comes." | The exchange terms, word for word |
| Sunglasses, bought once | "Sunglasses bought online feel cheap after 6 months." | "The frame material is why some last, and this is that material." | The material, a 2-year review |

### The moment

Name the scene, not the person.

- Yes: "7.10 on a school morning. She is at the hob with 1 hand, the eggs have stuck again, and she is scraping the pan with the spatula she was told not to use."
- Yes: "The bathroom mirror at 11 at night. Her cheeks are red again after the new cleanser, and she is reading the back of the bottle."
- Yes: "The dog is at the door, it is raining, and the jacket on the hook is the one that soaks through at the shoulders."
- No: "Home cooks who want a better pan." That is a person, not a moment.
- No: "Women with sensitive skin." That could be anyone, anywhere.

### Evidence tier

See `research-frameworks.md`. Always written with its basis. "Tier 6, inferred: 3 competitors each run 4 or more versions of a care demonstration, the oldest for 200 days" is honest. "Tier 6, proven by competitors" is not.

### 3 variants that are 3 ads

A set that passes (a baby blanket):
- A: a gift buyer; relief from not knowing what to give; opens on a number of buyers.
- B: a new mother buying for herself; pride in choosing well; opens by naming her.
- C: a grandparent; closeness; opens on a small story.

A set that fails:
- A: "The gift they keep", a gift buyer, relief.
- B: "The gift she won't forget", a gift buyer, relief.
- C: "Give something she'll use", a gift buyer, relief.

That is 1 ad written 3 times. Rewrite B and C so they reach different people.

## A note for a video

Add this under a concept whose best form is a video:

```markdown
**For a video:** opener {n}: "{the line}". First 3 seconds: {what is seen happening, already in motion}.
**Who is on camera:** {a real person the brand has the right to show (who, and what right), or a fictional adult who is never presented as a customer, the founder or an expert}
```
