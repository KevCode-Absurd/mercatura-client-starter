# Audit log: the entry template

A running record of what the account confirmed, what tired and what was underfunded is the most useful thing to read before a brief. It belongs to the brand, so it is kept in the brand's brain and never in this skill's folder, which every brand shares.

- In the app: the Coverage screen is the current read of the account, and `brain.md` holds the record (What we know works, What to avoid, Ideas to explore).
- By hand: add an entry in this shape to `brain.md`, newest first, after every review of the account.

```markdown
## {YYYY-MM-DD} ({measured: account, {dates} / reported / ad library only})

**Top issue:** {the 1 thing that would move the account most this week: the number and the fix}
**Underfunded:** {an ad doing well on little spend: name it, with its numbers}
**Spending without return:** {an ad or ad set: its cost per sale, and what to do}
**Missing:** {a stage, a buyer, a format or an offer with nothing running}
**For the next brief:**
- CONFIRMED: {angles or formats the numbers support: brief fresh versions}
- DEAD: {angles or formats that failed, and how many times: stop briefing them}
- TEST: {the untested format or angle that matters most now}
```

With no account, only "Missing" and "TEST" can be filled, and the entry says "ad library only".
