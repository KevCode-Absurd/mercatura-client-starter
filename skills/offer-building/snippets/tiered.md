Mechanic: spend more, get more.
- A reward at a spend level. Say the level and the reward in 1 short line: spend this, get that.
- The reward is the extra as given. Do not describe it as money off unless it is.
- A ladder of levels is 1 short line a level, lowest first.
- Say what a buyer would add to reach the level, if the facts make that plain.
