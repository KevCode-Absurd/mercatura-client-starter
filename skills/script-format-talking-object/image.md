# Talking object as a still image: the object, its face, its 1 line

Judged on 1 thing: at the size of a thumb, a stranger reads the object's feeling from its face, then reads its line, and the 2 together make the point.

## The frame

- **1 object, in its real place, with 1 expression.** The expression is the hook: fed up, worried, guilty, deadpan or lost, by the tone chosen. It is caught mid-feeling, looking at the product or away from it. Never looking at the viewer with a smile.
- **Its state tells the back story** a video would narrate: the dust on it, the dent, the half-used level, the unopened seal.
- **Composition:** the object fills about half the frame, its face at the centre of attention, where such an object really sits (the shelf, the hook, the back of the cupboard). The product stands at the edge at true size, sharp, its front to the lens, where the object's eyes can go to it. For an unaware buyer the product may be left out, and the object looks at the empty place.
- No people in the picture. A hand reaching past the object for the product is the most a person appears.

## Words on the picture

- **1 speech bubble: the line that lands.** 12 words or fewer, first person, in the object's tone, pointing at the object's mouth. It is the 1 line a video would end on, and it must make sense without anything said before it ("She hasn't opened me since March.").
- Outside the bubble, 1 line of plain type at most: what the product is, in 6 words or fewer, set by the product. A button only where there is an offer. The object never speaks the offer.
- 20 words on the whole image.

## For the image prompt

- Say what it is: a photograph of the real room, with the object as a physical thing in it and a face that belongs to its surface. The face is 2 eyes, eyebrows and a mouth on a clear area of the object, in the object's own material (moulded, stitched, dented in), with small arms only if the pose needs them. Give the expression as what the features do: brows dropped, eyes slid to the left, mouth pressed flat.
- Name the object's material, colour, wear and exact place, and the room's light and where it comes from.
- The product has its real shape, colours and printing from its photo, and no face.
- The bubble is a clean white speech bubble with a thin dark outline and a tail to the object; its words are given exactly, in quotes, once.
- Not a cartoon drawing and not a sticker of a face laid on a photo.

## What fails in a still

- A bubble that needs 2 more sentences to be understood.
- A cute face with no feeling in it: a mascot, not a witness.
- The object telling the viewer to buy, or saying "you".
- The product with a face, or the object drawn so like the product that the 2 are confused.
