# Format: talking object

Where this and the task skill differ, this wins.

It is judged on 2 things: the face and its feeling are read in the first second, and its last line is one a stranger could repeat.

## Choosing the object

The object is the narrator, and the choice decides the ad. It is never the product: a face on the product spoils its label, and a product that praises itself is an ad at first sight. The product is what the object talks about. 3 kinds of object can talk:

| The object | Choose it when | What it can say |
|---|---|---|
| **A witness**: something in the room when the problem shows (the hook by the door, the bedside lamp, the wooden spoon by the hob) | The buyer does not yet name the problem, or the product's result can't be seen | What it saw the person do, and how often |
| **The thing it replaces**: the old way, kept and failing (the scratched non-stick pan, the half-used tub at the back of the cupboard, the suitcase with the wobbly wheel) | The buyer owns the old way or has tried other fixes | Its own faults, the ones anyone can see or the brand's facts state; that it has been left alone since |
| **The problem itself**: a thing outside the body that the product removes or prevents (the burnt-on egg, the ring of limescale, the puddle under the coat) | The product's result is visible and the brand's claims state it | That it lost, and to what, in the claim's own terms |

Then 5 tests. An object that fails 1 is replaced.

1. **It was there:** in the room at the moment the research describes. When customers name an object themselves ("the graveyard of tubs under my sink"), use theirs.
2. **It is 1 simple shape,** known with a face on it in under a second. Not a pile, a routine or an idea.
3. **It has something to lose or to confess:** it is being replaced, blamed or ignored. An object with nothing at stake only describes.
4. **It can stand beside the product** in 1 frame, in a place where both would really be, at true size, and can't be taken for the product.
5. **It is nobody's and nothing's:** generic, never a competitor's product by shape or colour. Not a body part, an organ or a symptom: that tells the viewer they have a condition. Not a mirror or a screen: a reflection can't be rendered.

Say what it is, what it is made of and what state it is in (dusty, dented, half empty). Damage is shown, not mentioned.

## The 5 tones. Pick 1.

| Tone | How it sounds | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Rant | Fed up, venting about what it has had to watch | A witness |
| Warning | Urgent: it has seen where this goes | A witness |
| Confession | Quiet: it admits its own part | The thing it replaces |
| Fact-drop | Deadpan: 1 plain fact, said as if obvious | Any |
| Existential | Searching: what was it even for | The thing it replaces, the problem |

The brand's voice sets how far the joke goes. A brand that never jokes gets a confession or a fact-drop, not a rant.

## What the object says

- **First person, always.** "I", "me", "my". The person it lives with is "she", "he" or "they". It never says "you": it is not talking to the viewer.
- **A specific feeling.** Not "I'm sad". What exactly: passed over, blamed, kept as a guilt object.
- **1 detail only this object would know.** How long it has sat there, what it hears at 6 in the morning.
- **It only knows what it saw.** What the person reaches for, how often, what stopped. Never a result inside a body ("her bloating went"), never how the product works, never a number that is not in the brand's facts.
- **No call to action in its mouth.** It does not sell, name a price or tell anyone to buy. The offer is separate type, beside the product.
- **The product** stands beside the object, never part of it, with its real label and no face.

## What it is good for

It says what the buyer never says out loud, it is funny or tender at first sight, and nobody mistakes it for a testimonial. It carries 1 feeling well and 1 fact at most.

So it carries the **problem**, told by a witness: the accommodation, the failed fix, the thing the buyer has stopped noticing. It is weak for mechanism and for proof.

By stage: a witness for an unaware or problem-aware buyer; the thing it replaces for a solution-aware buyer who has tried the fixes. It is not an offer ad.

## What to avoid

- "You" anywhere in the object's words.
- A happy object from the start: there is nothing to turn.
- A second talking object in the same ad. A set of related objects is a set of separate ads.
