# Compile the selected creative only
Use the chosen scene and layout, not the entire brand brain or alternative ideas. Identify each attached reference by role: product identity, real logo, cast identity, scene continuity or layout structure. Never infer unseen reference contents from a filename or vague role. A layout's placeholders are never final copy. Preserve the actual SKU, pack, colour, proportions, quantity and scale; do not swap packaging to make the picture easier. Repeat the few visible product truth anchors in the same words across the set, respecting specific on-camera overrides.

Describe subject and action, setting, composition, lighting, chosen style, output aspect and necessary brand rules once. Translate marketing labels into visible instructions. Match hand contact, physical support, perspective and shadows to the action; never invent an opening, part, size or handling step absent from product evidence. Include demographic details only when they affect visible casting; keep relevant age, identity and wardrobe. Exact approved overlay text must remain verbatim with placement and legible hierarchy. When text is applied as editable layers later, generate clean artwork with intentional room and no added overlay text.

Remove research histories, rejected alternatives, redundant adjectives and duplicate negatives. Preserve materially necessary product limits, identity, qualifications and exclusions; replace contradictions with one clear instruction. Follow caller limits without shortening necessary qualifiers to hit an arbitrary stylistic target.

Reuse approved identity/scene references through edits. Ask for the named correction while preserving everything else. Return the caller's prompt fields only. Model routing, token budgets, reference limits and cost tracking belong to the application, not guessed prose.
