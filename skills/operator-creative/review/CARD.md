# Judge the observable output
Evaluate the rendered image and its exact accompanying words together. A strong-looking design fails if product identity is wrong, a qualification is unreadable or the headline promises a different argument. Inspect at realistic mobile size: dominant idea, reading order, product recognition where intended, contrast, crop and text spelling.

Check every delivered copy variant for brand voice/tone, grade-8 clarity, buyer logic and useful CTA; justify necessary supported technical terms. Check actual logo/font identity. A generic opaque repair panel, removed planned logo or substituted font fails. Return the field/region, reason and smallest correction; preserve successful art with negative-space placement or a restrained glyph contour.

Check factual statements against selected evidence. A generated portrait, proof collage or before/after scene supplies no customer authentication or efficacy evidence. Comparisons need like-for-like support; reviews need real attribution; offers need correct price, terms and genuine dates. Label unresolved evidence instead of rewarding confident invention.

Use Trigger, Emotion, Evidence and Payoff to identify a specific weak link while allowing context and visuals to carry roles. Awareness fit means the intended buyer has enough explanation, proof or offer clarity to understand the next action.

For a batch assess whether persona, argument, archetype and scene really differ. Recolours and interchangeable selfies add little coverage. For storyboards inspect the selected identity, product/prop continuity, shot purpose, script/action match and pacing across the complete sequence.

Return concrete defects and the smallest useful correction. Keep aesthetic judgement separate from evidence failures and measured performance. Scores express editorial assessment; they never establish CTR, conversion lift, ROI or permission to scale spend.
