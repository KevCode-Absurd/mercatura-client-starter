# Brand evidence for creative work
Separate four reusable records: product truth, buyer evidence, writing voice and visual rules. Preserve source IDs and exact SKU, pack, serving, price and offer terms. A claim found on a sales page is a brand assertion until its support is known; a review about another product is category research.

Product truth describes what can be shown: packaging, contents, dimensions, real ingredients and supported use. Buyer evidence records situation, trigger, desired payoff, objection and authentic language; age alone establishes neither need nor awareness. Voice records working examples and useful do/don't rules. Visual rules record actual logo, font, palette roles, product colours and approved aesthetics; distinguish observed choices from suggested directions.

Extract only available information. Missing testimonials, certifications or measured outcomes stay missing. Do not invent a six-colour palette or a unique mechanism to complete a template.

Carry voice and surface-specific tone through every public word. Default to grade-8 clarity across all awareness stages; necessary supported technical terms need a simple buyer-useful explanation. Keep the actual role-specific font files and logo references in the visual record. Missing assets stay explicit input gaps; no replacement font, removed planned logo or generic opaque text panel can conceal them.

At handoff send the slice the next stage needs: planning gets buyer and supported proposition; writing gets selected facts, relevant quotes and voice; rendering gets approved scene/text, product references and visual constraints. Keep research history, rejected concepts and evidence provenance outside generation prose. Refresh the shared records when underlying product, offer or brand facts change.
