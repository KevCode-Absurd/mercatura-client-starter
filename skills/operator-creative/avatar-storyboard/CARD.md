# Carry identity through the story
Use the selected avatar as the identity source. Separate its fixed face, hair, marks, proportions and outfit from scene-specific pose, light and framing. The application's sheet view order is authoritative: preserve it and verify face close-up, body, profile/back and hand coverage as appropriate. Each panel must show the same person or original character; the sheet establishes identity, not the final scene's lighting.

Every storyboard shot earns a role: hook, context, demonstration, evidence, payoff or CTA. TEEP helps locate a missing motivation or reason to believe; it does not require identical timing for all formats. Choose delivery appropriate to founder, educator, narrator, dialogue or UGC. Fictional casting must not invent a personal product result.

Carry brand voice/tone, grade-8 clarity, buyer logic and useful CTA through approved copy and performance. Keep speech exact at render time. Preserve product printing and planned logos. Editable overlays use the actual brand font in planned negative space; no generic opaque boxes or replacement family. The video-editing card covers measured timing/finishing when clips are requested.

Give each frame a drawable moment, cast present, action, framing, setting, props and script/overlay timing. Preserve room layout, light sources, outfit and object positions through neighbouring frames unless the action changes them. Product appearance and handling follow the real reference and selected shot's visibility. Show only handling that advances the approved argument; portability can use a sealed pack. Customer language is not evidence for invented experiences, side effects or physiological explanations.

Use additional boards when the argument or scene changes require them, not to duplicate near-identical frames. Check script fits shot time and matches visible action. Keep voiceover distinct from lip-synced dialogue. Review identity, product fidelity and sequence coherence before downstream production. Stop at the requested storyboard stage.
