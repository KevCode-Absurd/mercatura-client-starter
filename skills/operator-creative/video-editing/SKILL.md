# Brand-preserving video editing

Use the approved script, buyer/stage, selected cast, actual product references, brand voice/look and measured clips. Carry the buyer's argument and useful CTA through reference, character sheet, storyboard, clip plan and edit. Every shot earns attention, explanation, proof or action. Fictional casting is not a testimonial. Plain grade-8 language is the default across TOFU, MOFU and BOFU; keep necessary supported technical terms with a simple explanation.

Represent each source clip and its associated captions/overlays as one timed group. Keep local caption times with that clip; translate once into the final timeline when trimming or moving it. Measure source duration and final duration. Preserve original speed unless a deliberate change is approved, and never clip a spoken word to force a target length. Transcriptions stay verbatim; creative copy repairs happen upstream before rendering.

Load the real role-specific brand font before measuring or drawing text, and pass its file on each draw. Verify the rendered face against the font's own glyph widths. Fit exact approved words using actual measurements. Wrap without truncation; if readable text does not fit, split the timed phrase or repair its placement. Missing fonts are an input failure. Never replace them with Arial or another family.

Use transparent brand glyphs in planned negative space, with a restrained glyph contour for contrast. Generic opaque caption/title plates are forbidden. Preserve authentic product printing and planned real logos. Authored in-world elements may remain when the approved format calls for them; a no-brand scene receives no invented logo. A readability repair cannot erase successful imagery, branding or qualifications.

Preview first/last frames, key cuts and each caption at phone size; listen to hook joins, pronunciation, speech and music levels. Export clean footage plus optional captioned footage and a subtitle file. Check actual dimensions, duration, audio and visible typography. Report the precise failing field/region and smallest correction; an editorial verdict does not establish conversion lift.
