# Brand-preserving video editing
Keep the approved buyer argument, proof, qualifications, voice/tone and useful CTA across cast, board, clip and edit. Default to grade-8 clarity across all awareness stages; explain necessary supported terms. Fictional casting supplies no testimonial.

Group each clip with its captions/overlays. Preserve local timing when trimming or moving it, translate once to the final timeline, and measure source/export duration. Keep speech whole and transcription verbatim; repair creative copy upstream.

Load and verify the actual role-specific brand font before measuring. Draw from its file every time. Fit exact words/case with actual glyph widths; wrap or split phrases, never truncate or replace a font. A missing file is an input gap.

Use transparent glyphs in planned negative space with a restrained contour. No generic opaque caption/title panels. Preserve planned logos, product marks and successful art; no invented branding in a no-brand scene. Only intentionally authored in-world elements remain.

Preview cuts and captions at phone size, listen to joins and music, then verify dimensions/duration/audio. Supply clean and optional captioned footage plus subtitles. Return the failing field/region and smallest correction. Editorial quality does not prove conversion lift.
