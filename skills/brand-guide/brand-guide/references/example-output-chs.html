04 — Voice

How CHS Sounds

CHS talks like a friend who happens to know a lot about dog health. Direct, specific, a little blunt. Never like a press release.

Voice Principles

PrincipleWhat It Means
DirectSay what it does. "Slippery elm coats the gut lining." Not "Known for its soothing properties, slippery elm helps to coat the digestive tract."
SpecificConcrete details over vague claims. "Firms up loose stools" beats "supports digestive health." Names, numbers, timelines.
Has EdgesBe against something. "No spray-dried bacteria that die before they reach the gut." Not just positive. Real opinions.
ShortOne idea per sentence. If a sentence has a comma and a present participle, it's probably doing too much. Split it.

Do / Don't

Do

"Coats the gut lining. Soothes the kind of irritation that turns into runny stools."

Don't

"Known for its soothing properties, slippery elm helps to coat the digestive tract, providing relief from irritation and promoting gut health."

Do

"Energy and stamina. The 'old dog who suddenly acts young again' ingredient."

Don't

"This powerful mushroom supports energy levels and overall vitality, helping your dog stay active and healthy."

Do

"No fillers, no spray-dried bacteria, no vague proprietary blend. Twelve real ingredients you can pronounce."

Don't

"Our commitment to quality ensures that every ingredient is carefully selected to support your dog's overall wellness journey."

Words to Avoid

These words flag copy as AI-written. Use sparingly or not at all:

enhance foster showcase crucial journey vibrant renowned delve pivotal testament holistic empower seamless meticulous

Sentence Patterns to Avoid

PatternWhy It's Bad
"Known for its..."Vague attribution. Who knows? Say what it does instead.
"...ensuring/promoting/contributing to..."Present-participle filler. The sentence said nothing after the comma.
"Not just X, but Y"LLM crutch. Just say Y.
"supports overall wellness"The single most overused phrase in supplement copy. Be specific or delete.
"This [adj] [noun]...""This powerful mushroom..." "This unique formula..." — empty adjective + category noun. Name the thing.

Voice in Five Words
