# Format: UGC (creator or AI presenter)

Where this and the task skill differ, this wins.

## What makes it UGC

- **It reads as a person, not an ad.** 1 adult, their own phone, a real room, the room's own light. The product is in their hand or within reach, the way it is really held and used.
- **Register:** UGC casual. Low reading level, contractions everywhere, fragments are fine, the customer's own nouns. It should sound like something a person would post.
- **Exactly 1 specific number or anchor** carries the ad: a time ("week 3"), a moment ("6 in the morning"), a count, a dose. 1 is remembered; 3 sound like a brochure. It comes from the brand's facts, in the same terms. The price and the offer's own terms do not count towards the 1.
- **It underclaims.** "Not dramatic. Just consistent." beats "I'm obsessed". The bigger the claim, the more it reads as an advert.
- **Never:** "game-changer", "revolutionary", "obsessed", "life-changing", or any line that would also work as the voice-over of a polished advert.

## Who the person is

- **A real creator** speaks as themselves. A testimony is only compliant from someone who has used the product: their true detail goes where the result is, and none is scripted for them.
- **A generated person** is a fictional character, not a customer. They can't say "this worked for me", give a personal result or tell a personal story as fact. They speak in the 2nd person ("you've stopped..."), make an observation, or pass on what customers say, quoted word for word. Proof comes from counts, third parties and demonstration. Always an adult.

## What it is good for

A person with a phone has a believed human face and can read as a friend. It cannot show what a camera can't see.

So it carries the **problem** (a shared experience) or **proof** (a demonstration in hand). It is the wrong format for an ad that rests on an invisible mechanism.

By stage: problem-aware and solution-aware buyers first (the doubt named, then answered by what is shown); product-aware buyers with the product in use. For an unaware buyer the product stays out of the opening.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`: the avatar's `What almost stops them` (the doubt), `The moment` (where they are), `Customer language bank` (their nouns; a quoted line, word for word). No quote on file: an observation, and nothing is put in quotation marks.
- `products-and-positioning.md`, the product: `How it is used` and `Visible result` (the demonstration and what may be said of it). Blank `How it is used`: the product held and shown, not opened or used. Blank `Visible result`: no claim about what is seen.
- `brand.json` and `offers.json`: the 1 number, every claim, the offer. Not there: not said.
- `brand-guidelines.md`: `Brand voice and tone` and `Brand guardrails` (Never say).
