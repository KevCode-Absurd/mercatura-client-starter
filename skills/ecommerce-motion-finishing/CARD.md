# Motion finishing
Emphasise only a useful approved product description, proof, demo step or closing offer/action. Take exact contiguous phrases from the approved script; retain qualifications and never invent claims. No repeated caption, unnecessary overlay or covered product/face/hand.

Place sparse labels/badges in stable negative space using the brand's verified headline font and palette. Draw transparent glyphs with a restrained contour; no generic opaque plates or replacement faces. Preserve existing logos and product printing. An underline is only a purposeful accent under a stable visible object.

Keep events inside the final timeline, non-overlapping and long enough to read. Match timing to the approved scene, explain sample coverage limits and flag untracked movement for review. Do not alter footage, narration, sound or duration. Preview at phone size with sound off; the last action must remain understandable. A missing font is an input repair, not permission to hide or restyle the words.
