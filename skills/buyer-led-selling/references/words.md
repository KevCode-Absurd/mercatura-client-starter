### Select the reader and deliver the promise

A headline or hook attracts the intended buyer by their recognisable moment, useful benefit or real news. Put the main stage-appropriate appeal in the opening; curiosity must resolve into that same argument, not an unrelated reveal. For an unaware buyer, the situation can be the appeal and the product can arrive later. Match the destination's promise and the actual next step.

Replace a vague quality adjective with a relevant, sourced detail when one is available. Exact numbers are informative only with their scope and conditions; precision itself is not proof. Explain a verified process in plain words and connect it only to supported value. A process shared by competitors can be worth explaining without claiming exclusivity.

Write enough useful information to resolve this buyer's doubt within the requested length and space. More words do not automatically sell more. Present an existing trial, sample or guarantee only with its actual eligibility and terms. Never invent a gift, discount, urgency or membership restriction to make the offer persuasive. Keep a clear, appropriate CTA.
