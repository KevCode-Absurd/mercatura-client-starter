### Show the approved service

Make the scene or action help the viewer understand the same buyer benefit as the approved copy. When a demonstration is relevant, show the verified use and the observable task or outcome it can support. Keep the product, its handling and any comparison conditions accurate. A decorative hero shot can be appropriate; no compulsory demonstration or new claim is added to an approved concept.

A generated demonstration, process scene, reaction, before/after or result is an illustration, not test evidence. Do not fabricate measurements, manufacturing practices, customer experiences or efficacy. If proof footage or a verified process reference is missing, use an accurate product/use illustration and note the gap. Image and words must agree; style, camera movement and motion cannot enlarge the promise.
