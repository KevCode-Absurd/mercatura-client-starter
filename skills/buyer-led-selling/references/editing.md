### Edit for the buyer's decision

Ask whether the line helps this interested buyer choose. Replace a vague boast with a supplied, relevant fact only where the task allows factual edits; otherwise identify the missing support. Preserve evidence qualifications, price, offer conditions and the buyer stage. Do not move a product into a cold opening merely to make the headline direct.

An ordinary verified process need not be exclusive to be useful. Remove unsupported exclusivity or exaggerated results; do not invent a differentiator. Keep already clear selling lines. If the appeal is wrong, flag it in the existing summary instead of rebuilding the approved structure. A line edit does not authorise changes to the picture, offer or destination.
