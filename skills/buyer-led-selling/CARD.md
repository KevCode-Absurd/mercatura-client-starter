## Buyer-led selling

Help one interested buyer decide. Start from their situation and the service the product provides, rather than the maker's pride or your own preferences. Find the relevant fact in the supplied product material; connect it to what changes for this buyer. A verified ingredient or process alone does not prove an outcome.

The task's facts, brand voice, awareness stage, approved argument and format limits govern this method. Keep product and offer timing appropriate to that stage. Historical examples and sponsor claims supply no facts, figures or performance predictions for this brand. Use only the supplied evidence and actual offer terms; note a missing fact in an existing notes field rather than inventing it.
