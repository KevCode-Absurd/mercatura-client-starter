# Satisfying as a still image: the motion, frozen at its best instant

Judged on 1 thing: a stranger can feel what happens in the next half second, and looks twice.

## The frame

- **Freeze it just before it resolves, or the instant it has.** The pour with the stream still joined and the glass 1 finger from full. The seal two thirds peeled, in 1 piece. The scoop levelled, the edge of the blade just leaving it. The lid a quarter turn from shut. For "order from disorder": the last thing about to drop into its place. A still of the finished state alone is a product photo.
- **1 motion, 1 thing in focus,** very close, filling the frame. The viewer is nearer than they would stand: at the rim of the glass, level with the shelf.
- **Order makes it satisfying when nothing moves:** exact spacing, a clean edge, a surface with nothing on it but this, 1 line of light along it.
- **The product** is in the frame and sharp: it is the thing in motion, or it stands just behind it with its front to the lens. Where the thing it replaces is in focus, the product is what it is being cleared for.
- A hand only if the motion needs one: 1 hand, entering from 1 edge, cropped at the wrist.

## Words on the picture

- 1 line of 6 words or fewer that turns the feeling into the point: what this motion means for the buyer ("1 scoop. That's the whole routine."). It does not describe the picture.
- Set small, on the quietest part of the frame, away from the motion. The picture is 80% of this ad.
- An offer line and a button only where there is an offer. 14 words on the whole image.
- The picture may carry no words; the feed copy then makes the point in its first sentence.

## For the image prompt

- Say what it is: a real close-up photograph taken at the instant named, at a fast shutter, so the moving thing is sharp: the stream, the droplets, the curl of the seal.
- Name the material and how it behaves: its weight, thickness, sheen, how it folds, beads or settles; a count that matters ("3 drops in the air, no splash on the counter").
- The place and its light: 1 real surface the buyer owns, soft daylight from 1 named side, a clean background that falls a little out of focus.
- The product is the real one from its photo, its printing sharp, opened where it really opens.
- No slow-motion blur, no splash crown, no floating ingredients, nothing levitating, no studio sweep.

## What fails in a still

- The finished state with nothing about to happen.
- A burst or a splash: drama is not the same as satisfying.
- Something that could not happen in a kitchen or a bathroom.
- A headline large enough to compete with the motion.
