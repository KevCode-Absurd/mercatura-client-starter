# Spoken voice: the card

<!-- The same rules as SKILL.md, with the explanation taken out. Sent at the "focused" context level; "full" sends SKILL.md. -->

Read each line as if aloud, at the pace it would be delivered, to a friend. You fix how a line sounds, never what it says.

- Every line is a whole sentence a person would say. A clipped line gets its small words back, even when that makes it longer. A price, an amount or a button is a label and may be a phrase.
- 1 idea per sentence. The verb comes early, then what matters; no trailing clause that makes the listener wait.
- Plain words. A technical term stays only where this audience uses it.
- Sentence lengths vary the way speech does. Never force a pattern, and never cut a told sentence into short flat ones. Fix 4 or more very short sentences in a row, and a run all the same length, by joining them with "and", "but", "so" or "because".
- Sayable in 1 breath, and nothing you would be embarrassed to say to a friend.
- Contractions always. A line that trails off on "it", "that" or "of" is mended only when the mended line is still what a person would say.
- Keep the person and the tense the script set; fix only a wobble.
- Leave a word the brand's own published lines use. A rhythm or a turn of phrase the brand sometimes uses is left only on a line a person would still say.
- Remove what sounds like a machine: "it's not X, it's Y"; em dashes; stacked rhetorical questions; a run of fragments for drama; a "No X. No Y. No Z." stack; a clipped tag-on ("Yes, really."); lists of 3 adjectives; words nobody says aloud ("game-changer", "elevate", "seamless", "unlock", "journey", "transform your"); filler openers ("Here's the thing", "Let's be honest").
- Copy that is read, not spoken (primary text, a native story, the words on an image): the breath test does not apply. Story copy runs in paragraphs of 2 to 5 whole sentences, with no rows of 1-line paragraphs. A headline is a plain sentence or a phrase a person says.

Never change: the meaning; a claim, number, price, offer term, guarantee, product name or placeholder in square brackets; the order of lines; a plain line that already works. Do not add a sentence, and do not shorten a line except to split 1 sentence that is too long for a breath into 2 whole ones.
