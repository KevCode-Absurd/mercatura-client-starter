# Real speed, real materials

A hook that looks slowed, floaty or generated does 2 kinds of harm: the viewer takes it for a rendering, and the hard cut into the ad body jars. The general rules are in `skills/production-principles/SKILL.md` section 4. This file is what is particular to a satisfying hook.

## Speed

Never in a prompt, and no near-synonym of them:

- slow motion, slow-mo, slowed, time slows
- time-lapse, sped up, fast forward
- frozen in time, bullet time, frame by frame
- high-speed camera, any frame rate

Say instead, once, near the top: `real speed`. A slow camera is fine; slowed playback is not. "The camera pushes in slowly" is a camera. "A slow pour" is a pour that the model slows down: write "a steady pour".

Verbs decide the speed as much as the speed words do. Things settle, roll, pour, peel, click, fold, slide, land. Nothing explodes, shatters, bursts or crashes: those verbs bring slowed footage back even when it is ruled out.

## Materials

Say how the material behaves when the motion depends on it. 1 clause is enough.

| Material | What it does | What it never does |
|---|---|---|
| Thin liquid (water, a drink) | Falls in a clear stream, splashes small, levels at once | Hang in the air, change colour by itself |
| Thick liquid (serum, syrup, oil) | Falls in a rope, folds on itself, levels slowly | Bounce, bead on its own surface |
| Cream or balm | Holds a peak, takes the mark of what touched it | Flow level, drip |
| Powder | Falls in a stream with a little dust, lands as a cone | Clump in the air, float upwards |
| Small solid pieces (gummies, pellets, nuts) | Tumble, knock each other, roll a little, stop | Merge, stick in the air, multiply |
| Fabric | Hangs by its weight, creases where it is folded | Float, move with no wind |
| Foam or padding | Compresses when squeezed, returns in about a second | Stay dented, puff past its shape |
| Metal, glass, hard plastic | Keeps its shape, rings or clicks on contact | Bend, ripple |
| A lid, cap or latch | Travels on its thread or hinge, stops | Appear already on, pass through the rim |

A count that matters is stated: "8 gummies", not "some". Pieces left to chance multiply.

## The look

The hook sits in front of an ad body and has to belong to it. Take the look from the body it will join and from `Motion and sound` (Camera, Pace) in the brain: a hook in front of a phone-filmed body looks filmed on a phone, in the same kind of light. Words that help either way: the light and where it comes from, how close the camera is, what is in focus. Words that push towards a rendering and are left out: dreamlike, surreal, epic, glossy, perfect, 3D, render. No camera maker and no film title: named trademarks are refused.

## Check each prompt before it is sent

- [ ] `real speed` once; no word from the speed list
- [ ] 1 motion, with a start and an end
- [ ] Natural verbs only
- [ ] The material's behaviour said where the motion depends on it
- [ ] Counts stated
- [ ] No filter word (`skills/production-principles/models.md`), no named place, shop or trademark
