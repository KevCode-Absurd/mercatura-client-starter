# The 2 prompts: the opening still, then the clip

A hook is made in 2 steps, each from this page. The opening still is made and approved first; the clip starts on it. The reasons are in `skills/production-principles/SKILL.md` (sections 1 and 2). How a named model wants timing, references and sound written is in `skills/production-principles/models.md`: use its form, not the form shown here, where they differ.

Inside the app the clip prompt is written by code from the shot plan. This page is for a hook made by hand.

## Before writing either

Have these in front of you, copied from the brain, not reworded:

- the product's `Looks like` and `Pack` lines (the 2 or 3 visible facts that matter most)
- the step of `How it is used` that the motion shows, and any lesson about how the product opens
- the motion's line from `Moves and sounds`, with its sound
- the file for each job under `Reference photos`
- the row of the approved line-up: object in focus, mechanic, place, camera, what "done" looks like, the last frame

## 1. The opening still

It shows the motion already under way: the instant about half a second in. A still of an empty set gives a clip that spends its first second on nothing.

```
[ASPECT]. A photograph. [CAMERA: where it is, how close, what it looks at].
[THE OBJECT IN FOCUS by its 1 name, with its 2 or 3 visible facts],
[THE MOTION CAUGHT PART-WAY: where the moving thing is at this instant],
on [SURFACE] in [PLACE]. [LIGHT and where it comes from].
@Image1 is the pack, front on: copy its shape, colours and printing exactly.
@Image2 is the product out of its pack: copy its shape, colour and finish exactly.
[@Image3 is a close view of its surface: copy the texture.]
The only objects in the frame: [each by its name]. Nothing else.
No words except the pack's own printing. The top and bottom sixth of the frame are plain.
```

Attach only the photos the frame needs, 1 per job. If the pack is not in the frame, do not attach it.

Look at the still against the check in `SKILL.md` step 4 before it goes for approval. Anything wrong in it will be wrong in the clip.

## 2. The clip

```
[ASPECT] video, [N] seconds, one continuous take, real speed.
References:
@Image1 is the opening frame: the clip starts on it exactly.
@Image2 is the pack, front on: its shape, colours and printing stay exactly as shown.
@Image3 is the product out of its pack: its shape, colour and finish stay exactly as shown.
The only objects in the scene: [name (2 or 3 visible facts)]; [name (...)].
Camera: [position]. [Locked / pushes in slowly / moves in a small arc from left to right].
[First part]: [the motion continues from the opening frame: what moves, from where, to where].
[Last third]: [the end: what "done" looks like]. [How the material behaves, if it matters].
[Last half second]: nothing moves. The frame holds on [the last frame: the product in view, its printing uncovered].
Sound: [the sound of the motion], then [the sound of the end], the room. No music. No voice.
No on-screen text.
```

Where the model takes a first frame directly, the still is given as the first frame and the first reference line is dropped.

Under 1,500 characters. Each object has 1 name, used the same way at every mention. The colour words are the brain's colour words.

## Worked example: a consumable (made-up brand)

Brain, `products-and-positioning.md`, Calm Body Butter. Looks like: thick pale-lilac whipped cream with soft peaks. Pack: squat clear glass jar, matt white screw lid, round white label with the name in dark purple. How it is used: unscrew the lid, peel the silver foil seal from its tab, scoop a little out. Moves and sounds: the foil seal peels off in 1 piece (a soft tearing breath); the cream holds a peak.

Line-up row: object in focus, the product itself; mechanic 8, peel; place, the edge of a bath on pale stone; camera, top down, locked; done, the seal is off and the untouched cream is whole; last frame, the open jar, the label readable, the seal lying beside it.

Opening still:

```
9:16. A photograph. The camera looks straight down from 30 cm.
The glass jar (squat clear glass, round white label with dark purple name) stands open
on pale stone at the edge of a bath, its matt white lid beside it. A hand holds the jar's
silver foil seal by its tab, a third of the way peeled back, showing pale-lilac whipped
cream with soft peaks underneath. Soft daylight from a window on the left.
@Image1 is the pack, front on: copy its shape, colours and printing exactly.
@Image2 is the product out of its pack: copy its colour and soft peaks exactly.
The only objects in the frame: the glass jar, the white lid, the foil seal, 1 hand.
Nothing else. No words except the jar's own label. The top and bottom sixth are plain stone.
```

Clip, 4 seconds:

```
9:16 video, 4 seconds, one continuous take, real speed.
References:
@Image1 is the opening frame: the clip starts on it exactly.
@Image2 is the pack, front on: its shape, colours and printing stay exactly as shown.
@Image3 is the product out of its pack: its colour and soft peaks stay exactly as shown.
The only objects in the scene: the glass jar (squat, clear, white label); the white lid;
the foil seal (silver, round, 1 tab); 1 hand.
Camera: straight down, locked.
First 3 seconds: the hand keeps peeling the foil seal back across the jar in 1 steady
pull. The seal stays in 1 piece and curls as it comes. The cream under it is untouched.
At second 3: the last edge of the foil seal lets go of the rim.
Last second: the hand lays the foil seal on the stone to the right of the jar and leaves
the frame. Nothing moves. The frame holds on the open jar, its label readable.
Sound: foil peeling, a small pop as the last edge lets go, a quiet room. No music. No voice.
No on-screen text.
```

## Worked example: a durable (made-up brand)

Brain, Ferro 26 cm pan. Looks like: dark blue-black carbon steel, a satin sheen, a long flat steel handle with 3 rivets. Pack: none in use (a card sleeve). How it is used: heat, a little oil, cook, wipe. Visible result: food slides and does not stick. Moves and sounds: a fried egg slides across the pan when it is tilted (a faint hiss); the pan rings when set down on a hob.

Line-up row: object in focus, the product itself; mechanic 18, release; place, a hob beside a white plate; camera, locked, close, from the side at pan height; done, the egg is whole on the plate and the pan is clean; last frame, the tilted pan above the plate, the handle in view.

Opening still:

```
9:16. A photograph. The camera is level with the rim of the pan, 40 cm away, side on.
The pan (dark blue-black carbon steel, satin sheen, long flat steel handle with 3 rivets)
is held by its handle and already tilted towards a white plate on the worktop. 1 fried egg,
its white set and its yolk whole, is halfway across the pan, sliding towards the lower rim.
Warm light from a window behind the hob.
@Image1 is the product: copy its shape, colour, sheen and handle exactly.
The only objects in the frame: the pan, 1 fried egg, the white plate, 1 hand on the handle.
Nothing else. No words. The top and bottom sixth of the frame are plain.
```

Clip, 4 seconds:

```
9:16 video, 4 seconds, one continuous take, real speed.
References:
@Image1 is the opening frame: the clip starts on it exactly.
@Image2 is the product: its shape, colour, sheen and handle stay exactly as shown.
The only objects in the scene: the pan (dark blue-black steel, long flat handle, 3 rivets);
the fried egg (white set, yolk whole); the white plate; 1 hand on the handle.
Camera: side on, at the height of the pan's rim, locked.
First 2.5 seconds: the fried egg keeps sliding across the tilted pan, whole, leaving
nothing behind it, and reaches the lower rim.
At second 3: the fried egg slides off the rim and lands flat on the white plate.
Last second: the pan stays tilted above the plate, clean and dry. Nothing moves.
Sound: a faint hiss as the egg slides, a soft landing on the plate, a quiet kitchen.
No music. No voice.
No on-screen text.
```

## Check each prompt before it is sent

- [ ] Every photo attached is named, with its job
- [ ] The words about the product are the brain's words; no colour, shape or size word of your own
- [ ] The opening frame already has the motion in it
- [ ] The end comes in the last third, and the last half second is still
- [ ] The last frame has the product in view with its printing uncovered
- [ ] The sound line says what is heard, and says no music and no voice
- [ ] The speed and material check in `realism-rules.md` passes
- [ ] The camera's position is the still's position, and it does 1 thing
