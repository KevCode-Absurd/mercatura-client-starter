# Satisfying hook: the card

**What it is.** The first 4 to 5 seconds of the ad, wordless: 1 object does 1 motion that resolves. Then the product's demonstration, or an ad body that already exists.

**What it is for and judged on.** It keeps people watching so the rest of the ad is seen. The first frame stops the thumb. The eye stays until the motion ends. The last frame leads into what follows with no change of subject.

## The object in focus

The product itself out of its pack, something it is made of, or the thing it replaces. The pack may be in the frame. Never a sealed pack turning on a stand, and never a scene that has nothing to do with the product.

## Structure in seconds (a 4-second hook; 5 where the motion needs it)

- 0.0: the motion is already part-way (the pour in the air, the seal a third peeled, the lid a quarter turn from shut). The opening frame is described that way.
- 0 to 2.5: the same motion continues. The end is in sight and not yet reached.
- 2.5 to 3.5: the end. What "done" looks like is stated.
- Last half second: nothing moves, the product is in view.

The hook is shot 1 of clip 1, wordless. What follows cuts on in the same place with the same objects.

## Shots this format cuts between

- The hook: 1 held shot, no cut.
- The start state, wide.
- The action, very close.
- The end state, from above.

## Mechanics: pick 1, from a motion the product really makes

What comes out: **pour and settle** (to rest); **fill to the brim** (stops at the rim or line); **scoop and level** (flat); **spread** (edge to edge in 1 pass); **pump out** (1 dose that holds its shape); **dissolve or bloom** (clear, frothed, steeped); **drop and settle**.

How it opens, shuts or fits: **peel** (off in 1 piece); **click shut**; **twist to the stop**; **perfect fit** (flush in its space); **zip or fasten** (the whole length in 1 pull); **open and reveal** (contents in order inside).

How the material behaves: **squeeze and spring back**; **fold or roll** (loose to a neat block); **unroll or drape** (flat, hanging still); **wipe clean** (1 pass, a clean stripe); **release** (lets go whole: off the pan, out of the tin); **bead and run off** (dry); **cut through** (a clean cross-section).

Order from disorder: **sort into order** (a row, a grid, their slots); **stack or nest** (flush); **many become 1** (the things it replaces cleared in 1 move, the product set down in their place).

Only when the product makes no motion: light crossing its surface, or the result 1 second after (still rocking, still steaming).

The end stays in doubt until the last third. A glass that is full by second 1 leaves 3 seconds of nothing.

## The camera and the subject

- The motion does the work. The camera is locked and close, or straight down for anything that fills, sorts, spreads or fits. At most 1 slow push in or 1 small arc, with its start side and end side stated.
- An unusual position (inside the bowl looking up, from the back of a shelf) is only obeyed when the opening frame is already taken from there.
- Wide and aerial moves are for a product used outdoors at that scale.
- 1 motion. Nothing explodes, shatters or bursts. Write "steady", not "slow", for anything that pours or falls: "a slow pour" comes back slowed. No slow motion and nothing sped up.
- Say how the material behaves when the motion depends on it: thick liquid falls in a rope and levels slowly; powder lands as a cone; cream holds a peak; pieces tumble, knock and stop; foam returns to shape in about a second; a lid travels on its thread and stops.
- State a count that matters ("8 gummies"). Pieces left to chance multiply.
- A hand, if there is one, enters from 1 side, does the 1 thing and leaves. A motion that needs no hand is safer. A product that is worn is on a hanger, laid flat, or on an adult cropped below the face.

## The product on screen

- No colour, shape or size word for it that the brand's facts do not have. A model told a colour in other words gets the colour wrong.
- What comes out of it in the hook (its colour, its form, its count) is what comes out of it in the demonstration.
- When the object in focus is an ingredient, a material or the thing it replaces, the product itself is still in the last frame.

## Sound

Name the sound of the motion and the sound of its end, as part of the action ("the lid seats with a click"). The room and nothing else: no music, no voice. The loudest moment is the end of the motion.

## The first and the last frame

- First: 1 subject with clear edges, in the brand's colours, the motion visibly under way, nothing in the top or bottom sixth of the frame.
- Last: a settled still with the product in view and its printing uncovered, in the place and light where the next clip begins. For a hook made for an existing ad, that is the place and light of the ad's first frame.
- The overlay is 1 to 4 words that open a question ("Wait for it", the first words of the buyer's own thought). Never an offer, a price, a claim or a call to action. It never sits over the object in focus.

## Avoid

- An opening on an empty set, or on a hand about to start.
- A camera move with nothing resolving in it.
- 2 motions in the hook, or a second event after it.
- A change that could not happen (a liquid changing colour for no reason).
- A place the ad never returns to.

## After the render, look at

1. Is frame 1 already moving?
2. Does the motion end, and in the last third?
3. Does anything new happen after the end?
4. Does the product match its photo: shape, colour, finish, count?
5. Is the camera where it was asked to be?
6. Is the last frame a clean still that the next clip can start on?
7. Stopped at any frame, is it still a good picture?
8. Has a person listened: the motion's sound, no music, no voice?
