# Founder questionnaire

For the founder (or whoever owns the product and the P&L). About 60 minutes on a recorded call for the questions marked **core**; the rest can be answered in writing afterwards. Have the product, in its packaging, within reach.

We already read your website, reviews, support tickets, surveys, order data and ad account. This list is only the things those can't tell us. Short, plain answers are best. "I don't know" and "we've never checked" are useful answers: they tell us what not to say in an ad.

Each question has an id (F1.1 and so on) so an answer can be traced to the place it is used. Where a question says "your main product", answer for the 1 you most want in ads, then add any other product that will be advertised.

---

## 1. Why this exists

What the data can't show: what happened before the first sale.

- **F1.1 (core)** What was happening in your life the week you decided to make this? Give the scene: where you were, what you'd just tried, what annoyed you.
- **F1.2 (core)** What did you believe about this category that the people already selling in it did not?
- **F1.3** What did you get wrong in the first version, and what did you change? Was there a moment you nearly stopped, and what kept it going?
- **F1.5 (core)** Which parts of your story are you happy to see in an ad, and which are private?

## 2. The product, inside and out

What the data can't show: the decisions behind the label, and what the product is really like in the hand. A picture or a video made without these answers shows a product you don't sell.

- **F2.1 (core)** For each main product: what is the 1 decision that made it more expensive or harder to make, and why did you make it anyway?
- **F2.2 (core)** What did you refuse to put in, or refuse to do, that competitors do?
- **F2.3 (core)** How does it work, in the words you'd use with a friend? Then: which parts of that explanation are proven, and which are your belief?
- **F2.4** The exact figures that matter and are not on the label or the page: amounts of ingredients, materials and their weight or thickness, sizes, capacities, test results. Which of them may we state in an ad?
- **F2.5** Where is it made, by whom, and what about that is worth saying?
- **F2.6 (core)** Where is the product honestly weak? Who should not buy it?
- **F2.7** How long before a customer notices something, what do they notice first, and how do you know? Then: could a camera see it (straight away, after a week, after a month), or is it only felt? "Nothing to see" is a useful answer.
- **F2.8** What is the most common way customers use it wrong? And what do pictures of it usually get wrong: held, opened, worn or served in a way that never happens, the wrong amount, the wrong colour or size?
- **F2.9** What could a competitor copy in 3 months, and what could they not?
- **F2.10 (core)** Take your main product out of its packaging and hold it. What does it look like: shape, colour, shiny or matt, soft or hard, and how big is it next to your hand (or on a person, if it is worn)?
- **F2.11** Now the packaging it arrives in. What is it (box, pouch, bottle, jar, bag, sleeve), what colour is it, and which words are printed largest on the front, from top to bottom?
- **F2.12 (core)** Show us how it is used, 1 step at a time, as if we had never seen it: where it opens, what comes out or goes on, what you do with it, how long that takes, and what is left over or put away afterwards.
- **F2.13** What does it do when it is handled? Name 2 to 4 real movements (it pours, tears open, clicks shut, fizzes, folds, zips, sizzles) and the sound each one makes.

## 3. Claims and proof

What the data can't show: what is actually backed, and by what document.

- **F3.1 (core)** List every claim you make today. For each: what backs it (study, test report, certificate, survey, supplier document), and can you send the file?
- **F3.2 (core)** Every number you use in public (customers, reviews, star rating, percentages): where does it come from, and as of what date?
- **F3.3 (core)** What have lawyers, regulators, a marketplace or an ad platform told you not to say? Include ads that were rejected and why.
- **F3.4** Which claims would you like to make but can't yet? Is anything in progress (a study, a test, a certification) and when does it land?
- **F3.5** Experts, practitioners or press who have really endorsed you: who, what exactly did they say, and what are we allowed to use (name, face, quote, logo)?
- **F3.6** Any required wording: disclaimers, warnings, age limits, safety or care notices, regulated-category rules in each country you sell in.

## 4. Money and offers

What the data can't show: the limits you work inside, and what you're willing to do.

- **F4.1 (core)** Which product or bundle do you most want a new customer to buy first, and why (margin, repeat rate, stock)?
- **F4.2 (core)** What can you afford to pay to get a new customer, and what first-order value do you need? A rough range is fine. If you are willing: what 1 unit costs you to make, and what an order costs to send.
- **F4.3 (core)** Which offers are allowed, and what is the lowest price or deepest discount you will ever show? Which offers are you never willing to run?
- **F4.4 (core)** The guarantee, returns and warranty, exactly: what each covers, the time limit, the conditions, who pays the postage, how a customer claims it. How often is each used?
- **F4.5** What happens after the first order, in plain words. If the product is used up: how long 1 unit lasts, whether there is a subscription, what renews and when, how to pause or cancel. If it is bought once: how long it lasts, and what a customer buys from you next, if anything.
- **F4.6** Offers that worked and offers that flopped, including ones no longer in the ad account. Why do you think so? How many sales or flat discounts did you run in the last 12 months?
- **F4.7** Stock, shipping or seasonal limits in the next 90 days (launches, sell-outs, price changes, sales, last order dates). Which of these are real limits we may mention in an ad? We won't invent any.

## 5. The customer, as you see them

We have what customers wrote. This is your read, which we will check against it.

- **F5.1 (core)** Who do you think your best customer is? Describe 1 real person you've met or spoken to, without their name.
- **F5.2 (core)** Who buys that surprised you?
- **F5.3 (core)** Who do you want more of, and who do you not want as a customer?
- **F5.4** What do customers say to you in person or on calls that they don't write in reviews? Include your honest guess at why people who nearly bought did not, and why people send it back, cancel or never order again.
- **F5.5** What had your best customers tried before you, and why did they give up on it?
- **F5.7** Is the buyer always the user? If not, who is which (a parent, a partner, a gift)?
- **F5.9** Take the customer from F5.1. Where are they, and what time is it, when the problem shows up? Where and when do they use the product? If we filmed each moment, what would we see them doing?

## 6. Competitors and the enemy

What the data can't show: what you are willing to say about others.

- **F6.1 (core)** When you lose a sale, who or what do you lose it to? Include "doing nothing" and "keeping the one they already own".
- **F6.2 (core)** Can we name competitors in ads? Compare prices? Show their product? Where is the line?
- **F6.3** What is the idea, habit or industry practice you're against? Say it the way you'd say it when annoyed.
- **F6.4** Which competitor do you respect, and what do they do better than you? What do competitors say about you, or about products like yours, that is fair, and what is unfair?

## 7. Voice, look and taste

What the data can't show: what you would and wouldn't put your name on, in words, in pictures and in video.

- **F7.1** 3 ads or brands (any category) you wish were yours, and what you like in each. Links are best.
- **F7.2 (core)** 3 ads or styles you never want to look like, and why.
- **F7.3 (core)** Words and phrases you love, and ones you ban. Include anything that sounds like "marketing" to you.
- **F7.4** How far can humour go, and what is off limits: swearing, body talk, fear, guilt, jokes about competitors? If the brand were a person at a dinner, how would they talk?
- **F7.6** Send 3 things written in your own words (an email to customers, a post, a voice note). We learn the voice from these, not from adjectives.
- **F7.7** Where does the product belong in a picture? Which room or place, tidy or lived in, at what time of day and in what light (a morning window, an evening lamp, outdoors)?
- **F7.8** What sits next to it in real life? Name the surface it stands on and 3 to 5 things you'd expect to see near it. And what should never appear in a picture with it?
- **F7.9** Think of a video that felt right for the brand. Is it quick or unhurried? Filmed on a phone in someone's hand, or steady and polished? Close on the product, or on a person?
- **F7.10** What should a video of yours sound like: a person talking, music (what kind, and what never), or only the sound of the product? And words on screen: captions throughout, a few words, or none?

## 8. What has been tried

What the data can't show: tests that ran before the current ad account, and why you think they went the way they did.

- **F8.1 (core)** The 3 best ads you've ever run. For each: what it was, your theory of why it worked, and the number you judge it by (spend, sales, return), if you have it.
- **F8.2 (core)** Things you've tested that failed. For each: are you sure the idea failed, or was it the execution?
- **F8.3** Ideas you believe in but have never managed to test. And anything you are tired of seeing in your own ads.
- **F8.5** Which kinds of ad do you run today (a person talking to camera, a demonstration, a still image, a carousel, a long story), which have you never run, and why (no footage, no confidence, tried once)?
- **F8.6** Agencies, freelancers or creators who made your past ads: what did they get right and wrong about the brand?

## 9. Who appears in the ads

What the data can't show: consent, rights and your comfort with casting.

- **F9.1 (core)** Will you appear on camera? Will you lend your voice? Are you comfortable with an AI-made version of you saying approved lines?
- **F9.2 (core)** Who else can appear: staff, real experts, real customers, paid creators? For each, what rights do we hold (where, how long, paid ads or organic only)?
- **F9.3 (core)** AI-generated presenters: allowed or not? If allowed, any limits, and do you want them labelled?
- **F9.4** Who should the people in the ads be: age range, gender mix, backgrounds, what they wear and what they are doing? Anything that would feel wrong for the brand?
- **F9.5** May we use customer reviews word for word in ads, with first names or without? And the best customer story you know: would that customer let us use it, and in what form?

## 10. Honesty lines for story ads

Long story ads (native ads) can be built in several ways. These are your decisions, per brand.

- **F10.1 (core)** A story told by a "customer" who is a composite of real reviews, not 1 real person: allowed? Must it be labelled?
- **F10.2 (core)** Images styled like a notes app, a chat, a review or a news item (generic, never a real outlet or platform): allowed?
- **F10.4** Topics the brand never touches: health conditions, weight, age, money worries, politics, religion, anything else.

## 11. Assets and rights

- **F11.1 (core)** Where do your photos, videos, logos and fonts live, and which are cleared for paid ads? Include footage that exists but has never been used (the workshop or factory, you, behind the scenes, customer clips).
- **F11.2** Things we must handle with care: licensed characters, music, press logos, retailer logos, certification marks. And trademarks: exact spellings, symbols, and names we must never shorten or change.
- **F11.5 (core)** For each product that will be in ads, send 5 photos, or tell us which of yours they are: the pack from the front (as it is sold), the product out of its pack, the product in a hand (or on a person, if it is worn), the product being used, and a close view of its surface. Say which you don't have.

## 12. Goals and how we work

- **F12.1 (core)** What must be true in 90 days for this to have been worth it? 1 number if you can.
- **F12.2** Which countries and channels, and which product is the priority?
- **F12.3** Who approves ads, how fast, and does anything need a legal check before it goes live? How many new ads a week can you review and launch?
- **F12.5** What would make you stop an ad the same day?
- **F12.6** What haven't we asked that we should know?
