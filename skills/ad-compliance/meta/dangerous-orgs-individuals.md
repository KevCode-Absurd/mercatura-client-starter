# Dangerous Organizations and Individuals
### Policy details
Dangerous Organizations and Individuals
Ads Must Comply with the Community Standard on Dangerous Organizations and Individuals
Ads must not contain praise, support or representation of individuals or groups designated by Meta as Dangerous Organizations and Individuals.
Overview
This policy provides specific additional protections beyond what is prohibited in the Community Standard on Dangerous Organizations and Individuals.
Guidelines
In addition to the requirements in our Community Standards, Ads can’t:
- Praise, Support, or Represent any designated individuals or organization in any tier of the Community Standard.
Enforcement
We have the same policies around the world, for everyone on Facebook.
Review teams
Our global team of over 15,000 reviewers work every day to keep people on Facebook safe.
Stakeholder engagement
Outside experts, academics, NGOs and policymakers help inform the Facebook Community Standards.
