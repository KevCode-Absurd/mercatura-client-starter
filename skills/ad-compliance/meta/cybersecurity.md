# Cybersecurity
### Policy details
Cybersecurity
Ads must comply with the Community Standard on Cybersecurity.
Ads must not use phishing or social engineering techniques to capture others’ sensitive information, and must not promote or link to malicious code - such as malware or spyware.
Overview
This policy provides specific additional protections beyond what is prohibited in the Community Standard on Cybersecurity.
Guidelines
In addition to what our Community Standards prohibit, ads can’t:
- Include links that cause an automatic download upon opening the landing page.
- Advertise software or products designed to disrupt communication or signal sharing, including jammer and descrambling devices that intercept the connection between a phone and mobile phone base station.
Enforcement
We have the same policies around the world, for everyone on Facebook.
Review teams
Our global team of over 15,000 reviewers work every day to keep people on Facebook safe.
Stakeholder engagement
Outside experts, academics, NGOs and policymakers help inform the Facebook Community Standards.
