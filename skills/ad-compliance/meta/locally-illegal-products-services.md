# Locally Illegal Content, Products, or Services
### Policy details
Locally Illegal Content, Products or Services
Ads that are reported by a government, a court order, non-governmental organizations or members of the public as violating local law may be removed.
Overview
This policy supplements our Community Standards on Locally Illegal Content, Products, or Services
Additional Guidelines for Ads
We require that Ads on Facebook and Instagram must not be unlawful. In addition to locally restricting content, we may globally remove Ads that are reported to us as unlawful.
Additionally, we may globally remove content that violates local law when it is determined that the product is subject to a recall.
Enforcement
We have the same policies around the world, for everyone on Facebook.
Review teams
Our global team of over 15,000 reviewers work every day to keep people on Facebook safe.
Stakeholder engagement
Outside experts, academics, NGOs and policymakers help inform the Facebook Community Standards.
