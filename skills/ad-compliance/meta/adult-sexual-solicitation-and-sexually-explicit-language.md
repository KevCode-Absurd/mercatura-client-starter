# Adult Sexual Solicitation and Sexually Explicit Language
### Policy details
Adult Sexual Solicitation and Sexually Explicit Language
Ads Must Comply with the Community Standard on Adult Sexual Solicitation and Sexually Explicit Language
Ads must not contain content that facilitates sexual encounters, commercial sexual services between adults, or content asking for or offering pornographic content. In addition, we also restrict the use of sexually explicit language.
Overview
This policy provides specific additional protections beyond what is prohibited per the Community Standard on Adult Sexual Solicitation and Sexually Explicit Language. Ads must also comply with our Health and Wellness Advertising Standard.
Guidelines
In addition to the requirements in our Community Standards, Ads can’t:
- Contain content discussing sexual practices or experiences, even without sexual solicitation
When targeting people aged 18 or older, advertisers can run ads that:
- Contain content that contains usernames, links to or logos of Adult Subscription Websites
- Contain content with sexually explicit language in satirical or humorous contexts
- Contain content with sexually suggestive language referring to sexual encounters
We require additional information and/or context to enforce the following advertising rules. Ads can’t:
- Include content that would otherwise be prohibited by this policy where its violating elements are being satirized or attributed to something or someone else to mock or criticize them.
Enforcement
We have the same policies around the world, for everyone on Facebook.
Review teams
Our global team of over 15,000 reviewers work every day to keep people on Facebook safe.
Stakeholder engagement
Outside experts, academics, NGOs and policymakers help inform the Facebook Community Standards.
