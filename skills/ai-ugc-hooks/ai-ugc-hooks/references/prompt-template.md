# The 2 prompts: the opening still, then the clip

A talking hook is made in 2 steps. The opening still (the presenter, the product, the room) is made and approved first; the clip starts on it. Why: `skills/production-principles/SKILL.md` sections 1 and 2. How a named model wants speech, timing and references written is in `skills/production-principles/models.md`: use its form where it differs from the form shown here.

Inside the app the clip prompt is written by code from the shot plan. This page is for a hook made by hand.

## Before writing either

Copied from the brain and the approved line-up, not reworded:

- the product's `Looks like` and `Pack` lines, and its `In hand` photo for size
- any lesson or `Never show` rule about how it is held, stood or opened
- the approved presenter: the picture and the words that tell them apart
- the row: lever, the line with its cue word marked, pacing, the room, where the product is at the start

## 1. The opening still

It is the first frame of the clip: mid-thought, mouth just opening, eyes on the lens, the product already in the frame (in the lowered hand, or on the surface in front), not yet the subject.

```
[ASPECT]. A frame from a phone video, held at arm's length [or: phone propped on the surface].
[THE PRESENTER in the fixed words], in [THE ROOM: what is behind them, the light and where it
comes from]. [POSE: where they look, what the face is doing, which hand holds the phone].
[WHERE THE PRODUCT IS: which hand, at what height, or where on the surface], its front to
the lens, its printing uncovered.
@Image1 is the presenter: the same person exactly.
@Image2 is the pack, front on: copy its shape, colours and printing exactly.
@Image3 is the product in a hand: copy its size against the hand.
The only objects in the frame: [each by its name]. Nothing else.
Looks like a phone video, not a photo shoot: ordinary room light, a slightly off-centre
frame, nothing smoothed. No words except the pack's own printing. The top and bottom sixth
of the frame hold nothing that matters.
```

## 2. The clip

```
[ASPECT] video, [N] seconds, one continuous take, real speed. A phone video.
References:
@Image1 is the opening frame: the clip starts on it exactly.
@Image2 is the presenter: the same person from the first frame to the last.
@Image3 is the pack, front on: its shape, colours and printing stay exactly as shown.
The only objects in the scene: [name (2 or 3 visible facts)]; ...
Camera: [arm's length / propped], a little hand movement, the framing does not change.
[THE DELIVERY, as what the face and body do, from the row's pacing].
[THE LINE, in the model's speech form, the whole line, word for word.]
On "[CUE WORD]": [which hand] lifts [the product by its name] to [chest height, beside the
face], front to the lens, printing uncovered. [Any rule on how it is held or stood.]
Last half second: mouth closed, eyes on the lens, the product held still.
Voice: [age, pace, 2 or 3 traits, accent]. Sound: the room[, the product's own sound on the
lift]. No music. No subtitles and no on-screen text.
```

Under 1,500 characters. 1 name per object. Nothing is fetched from off screen: what the presenter lifts was in the opening frame.

## Worked example: a consumable (made-up brand)

Row: lever 8, objection first. Line: "You're going to think this is just another greens powder. *Fair*." (11 words; a 6-second clip, with the lift on "Fair".) Pacing: direct. Room: a kitchen worktop by a window, morning. Product: a matt green tub with a white lid, in the left hand, resting on the worktop.

```
9:16 video, 6 seconds, one continuous take, real speed. A phone video.
References:
@Image1 is the opening frame: the clip starts on it exactly.
@Image2 is the presenter: the same person from the first frame to the last.
@Image3 is the pack, front on: its shape, colours and printing stay exactly as shown.
The only objects in the scene: the green tub (matt green, white lid, the size of a mug);
a white mug; the worktop.
Camera: held at arm's length in her right hand, a little hand movement, the framing
does not change.
She looks straight into the lens, level eyes, a half shrug, a dry tone.
Presenter (dry): "You're going to think this is just another greens powder. Fair."
On "Fair": her left hand lifts the green tub from the worktop to chest height beside her
face, front to the lens, printing uncovered.
Last half second: mouth closed, a small smile, eyes on the lens, the green tub held still.
Voice: a woman in her 30s, even, dry, General American accent.
Sound: a quiet kitchen. No music. No subtitles and no on-screen text.
```

## Worked example: a durable (made-up brand)

Row: lever 5, watch this. Line: "Watch how small *this* jacket folds." (6 words; a 6-second clip in which the fold is the action.) Pacing: direct. Room: a hallway by the front door. Product: a navy padded jacket, already half folded on a bench, its own pocket turned out beside it. The lesson says: it packs into its left pocket, which zips shut.

```
9:16 video, 6 seconds, one continuous take, real speed. A phone video.
References:
@Image1 is the opening frame: the clip starts on it exactly.
@Image2 is the presenter: the same person from the first frame to the last.
@Image3 is the jacket packed into its pocket: copy its size and shape exactly.
The only objects in the scene: the navy jacket (padded, matt, half folded); the bench.
Camera: phone propped on a shelf at chest height, still, the framing does not change.
He glances at the lens, then down at the jacket, and keeps his eyes on it.
Presenter (even): "Watch how small this jacket folds."
On "this": both hands push the rest of the navy jacket into its own left pocket in
1 steady motion and pull the pocket's zip shut. The packed jacket is the size of a loaf.
Last second: he holds the packed jacket up at chest height in his right hand, mouth
closed, eyes on the lens.
Voice: a man in his 40s, even, British accent.
Sound: the hallway, fabric rustling, the zip closing. No music. No subtitles and no
on-screen text.
```

## Check each prompt before it is sent

- [ ] Every photo attached is named, with its job
- [ ] The line is whole, word for word, in the model's speech form, and fits the seconds
- [ ] The cue word is named and the lift is described: which hand, to where
- [ ] The product was in the opening frame; nothing comes from off screen
- [ ] The product's words are the brain's words; any rule on how it is held is in
- [ ] The delivery is written as what the face and body do
- [ ] The last half second is a closed mouth and a still product
- [ ] The voice and the sound are said; no music
- [ ] No filter word, no named place, shop or trademark (`skills/production-principles/models.md`)
