# When a hook comes back wrong

What came back, why, and what the next prompt says. A fault is written down as 1 line (what was asked, what came back) and added to the brand's lessons. Facts about a named model and the words its filter refuses are in `skills/production-principles/models.md`.

| What came back | Why | What the next prompt does |
|---|---|---|
| The product is not the brand's product | No photo attached, or it was described in other words | Attach the pack photo and the in-hand photo, each with its job. Use the brain's words only |
| The product appears from nowhere, or turns into something else | It was not in the opening frame | Put it in the opening still, in the hand or on the surface. The lift starts from there |
| The last words are cut off, or rushed | Too many words for the seconds | Cut words until the line fits at the speaker's pace, which follows their age, or ask for a longer clip |
| The lips do not match the words | 2 speakers, or the line was paraphrased in the prompt | 1 speaker. The line once, word for word, in the model's speech form |
| The lift happens at the wrong moment | No cue word | "On '[word]': ..." with which hand and to where |
| The framing changes, or the phone "turns round" | A reveal was written as a camera move | The camera does not move. The product comes up into the frame |
| It looks like a studio advert | Words like polished, professional, cinematic; or no word on the phone | "A phone video", the room's own light, a slightly off-centre frame |
| The face looks smoothed or waxy | Nothing said about the face, or a beauty word | "Nothing smoothed"; the presenter's 2 or 3 particular features; match the approved picture |
| The presenter is a different person | The picture was not attached, or a long description fought it | Attach the approved picture; say only what tells them apart |
| A hand covers the label, or the fingers are wrong | The hand was left to chance | Say which hand holds what and where the other hand is. "Printing uncovered" |
| Words or captions appear in the picture | The model filled the frame | "No subtitles and no on-screen text." |
| Music under the voice | Sound was not said | "Sound: the room. No music." A person listens before the clip is used |
| The line says something only a customer could say | The lever was a real-person lever | Change the lever (`hook-frameworks.md`), not the wording |
| The job is refused by the filter | A filter word, a medical or violent phrase, a named place, shop or trademark | Rewrite once without it. A second refusal: change the idea. A refusal is charged |

## Joining a hook to an ad body

- The hook is scaled to the body's size and frame rate.
- The hook ends on a closed mouth; the body starts on its own first word. No word runs across the join.
- Look at the hook's last frame beside the body's first: the same product, the same kind of light, and either the same person or plainly a different one. A near match of 2 different people reads as a mistake.
- The raw clip is kept. An overlay goes on a copy.
