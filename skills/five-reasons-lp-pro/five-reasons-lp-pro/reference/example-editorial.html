Still Have Questions?

The most common things groups ask before they book.

How long is the workshop?−
Two to three hours from check-in to clean-up: design, cutting, soldering, stone setting, and finishing. Allow 15 minutes on either end if it's your first time.
Do we need any jewelry-making experience?+
Zero. A working silversmith hosts every group and walks you through every step. We are not a school. The silversmith is your hype-person at the bench, not a teacher with a lesson plan.
What if some of us don't drink?+
This isn't a bar. It's a creative studio. Drinker or not, every guest is making the same piece on the same bench. No one sits out, no one is the odd one out. Ages 16+ welcome (under 18 with a parent).
How big can our group be?+
Smaller groups (2–7) book into mixed public sessions. 8 or more unlocks a private studio buyout with custom packaging for the bride or birthday guest. Bachelorette parties of 12, 16, even 24 are routine. Use the group form for a quote.
What do we actually make?+
Sterling silver ring, bracelet, pendant, bolo, cuff, or stud earrings. You pick the design and the stone (turquoise, larimar, moonstone, sun stone, amber, mother of pearl, mossy agate, and more). Cabochon-style only, no faceted stones.
What if someone bails last minute?+
Cancellations inside 24 hours are $20 per person. Outside that window, reach out and we'll always try to make it work. Friends can swap into open seats anytime, no name change needed.
What about the price?+
Most studios are $130–$165 per person all-in (Waikiki and Irvine premium tiers run a touch higher). Compare it to dinner plus drinks plus an Uber. Same money, except you walk out wearing the night instead of waking up to the receipt.
Where are the studios?+
Nine studios: Boise, Gilbert, Irvine, Nashville, Park City, Provo, SLC, St. George, and Waikiki. Pick whichever is closest at checkout.
