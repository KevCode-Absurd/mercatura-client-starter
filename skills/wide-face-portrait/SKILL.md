# Wide Face portraits

Adapted from Nuova Era's Wide Face package. This is a photographic camera treatment for still images, with deliberately exaggerated perspective and natural skin detail. It changes the framing, not the person's anatomy or the product's claimed effects. An adult subject of any requested appearance can carry it; the original example's woman, wardrobe and room are not universal casting requirements.

## Reference roles

Inspect [the bundled camera reference](assets/canonical-wide-face.png) when using this skill outside the app. Supply its actual pixels to an image renderer that accepts references. It controls overhead viewpoint, close perspective, face-to-frame scale and body recession. It does not supply a new character's identity, skin tone, freckles, makeup, clothing, product or location.

A supplied character or approved avatar controls identity, facial features, natural asymmetry, age, hair and skin. A product photo controls shape, proportions, colour and label. Name these roles separately in the render prompt so the camera reference never replaces the person or product. With no character reference, create an original fictional adult fitting the brief. Do not label that character a customer or invent personal product results.

Inside Creative Engine, selecting this look loads this core plus [image.md](image.md) for the relevant planning, words, prompt and picture-check steps. The render attaches the camera reference separately from product and identity references. Outside the app, use the same files and actual assets in Claude, GPT or another harness; adapt to the available renderer's schema rather than assuming a particular tool or Project setup. If reference input is unavailable, deliver the explicit camera prompt and identify the unexecuted reference step. Do not claim the image was inspected or rendered.

## Direction and controlled edits

Follow the selected subject, scene, aspect ratio, copy and product presence. The reference's vertical composition is a useful starting point for a standalone request without a chosen size; an app set's selected aspect ratio wins. Make the face dominant within that shape and give the product a credible grip and readable placement when shown. This look is suited to beauty, fashion and creator-style campaign portraits; it is not evidence of skin improvement or a before/after claim.

For “same character” keep identity and markings stable. For “change only the hair”, alter only the hair and preserve crop, camera, expression, light, room and other styling. Keep the approved image as an additional continuity reference when supported. Change one visible issue at a time when repairing skin; more texture is not automatically better. [Portrait realism](../production-principles/references/portrait-realism.md) supplies localized texture, makeup and physical-contact repair guidance when needed.

Use [image.md](image.md) for camera and render direction and [the examples](references/examples.md) when a standalone prompt or minimal edit is needed. A request for a prompt receives a usable prompt; image generation uses an actually available tool within the user's requested scope. “8K detail” describes a visual aim, not a guaranteed export size.

## Review the result

Check the overhead angle, dominant upper face and taper toward the chin, sharp eyes and coherent recession of the shoulders. Reject a normal eye-level headshot, circular fisheye, uniformly inflated face or broken anatomy. Judge skin at the same scale: varied fine texture, restrained sheen, no stamped pore grid or sharpening halos. Check identity, product label, hand contact and exact copy independently. The geometry can be strong without copying the example's shiny finish. A checklist does not establish quality until the actual rendered image has been inspected.
