# Testing native ads

For the person running the ads. No writing or planning step is sent this file.

The figures below came with the method this skill was built from. They are not measured on this app or on any brand in it. Treat them as a starting guess and replace them with the brand's own numbers once it has run a set.

- **A set is 5 stories on 1 tension:** the lead story and 4 others, each with a different arc, form and image type.
- **1 campaign, 1 ad set per tension,** budget set at the campaign.
- **The first 3 days show whether a tension has potential, not profit.** No add-to-carts or checkouts in 48 hours: the tension is replaced.
- **Early signs given by the source (unmeasured):** CPM around $30 to $40 and under $60; 20 to 25% of viewers clicking anything; 2.5% clicking through to the page.
- **Reading them:** high CPM with good click rates means the message is crowded. High CPM with poor click rates means the ad is weak.
- **What wins is built on:** more stories on the winning arc and image type, then a new tension or an avatar.
- **Before a set runs,** a person reads each story's notes: the narrator (a real customer, a composite, the founder, an expert or an observer), every placeholder, and anything marked "inferred". A bulletin image is looked at by a person before it is published: platforms restrict formats that look like news.
