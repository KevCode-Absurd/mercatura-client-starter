Product aware: they know this product and are not convinced.
- The doubt that stops them, or the comparison they are making, is where the opening leads. Where the request gives this ad a way in, the opening starts from it.
- The work of this ad is 1 objection, answered completely, with proof. Compare on something concrete: a dose, a material, a size, a price per day or per year of use.
- The product is named from the first lines. Do not explain what it is.
- Ask directly and take the risk away, as the offer states it: the guarantee, the trial, how to pause or cancel for something reordered; returns, exchanges and the warranty for something kept.
- It is shorter than a colder ad: every line that re-explains the product is a line they skip.
- How it fails: it answers 3 objections thinly instead of 1 fully.
