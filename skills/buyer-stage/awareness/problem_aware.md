Problem aware: they feel the problem and do not know there is an answer.
- The opening makes them think "that is me". Where the request gives this ad a way in, the opening starts from it.
- Then make it matter, as a long story of the problem: what it costs, how often, what they tried, where it leads (the request gives the range). Then show that a kind of answer exists, before this product is named.
- No technical term: say what a thing does in their words.
- The product arrives after the way out is understood, not in line 2.
- An image ad's picture is the problem. The product is left out unless asked for.
- Ask softly: try it, see if it fits. The request says how much of the deal is said: usually none here, and where 1 term is given, the one that makes trying safe.
- Openings that fit: their own words, a moment, a callout by what they do. Never by what they have or are.
- How it fails: it jumps to the product while they are still nodding at the problem.
