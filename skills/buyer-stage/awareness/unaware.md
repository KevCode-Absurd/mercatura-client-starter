Unaware: they do not know they have the problem.
- Open on their life, never on the problem's name and never on the product or what is in it. Where the request gives this ad a way in, the opening starts from it.
- The work of this ad is to make them see the problem, told as its story. It is the longest ad: the request gives the range.
- No technical term: they have no word yet for the answer.
- The product arrives late and lightly, after the cause is understood (stated only as the facts hold it). It may be named only in the last lines, and seen earlier, in use, with no word about it.
- An image ad's picture is their moment. The product is left out unless asked for.
- Ask for little: a look, a read. No price, no discount, no deadline.
- Openings that fit: an observation, a story, a surprising fact. A question about the problem fails: they have not thought of it yet.
- How it fails: it sells before they care.
