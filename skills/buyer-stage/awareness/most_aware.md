Most aware: they want it and need a reason to buy today.
- What they already like about it, or the thing that has kept them from ordering, is where the opening leads. Where the request gives this ad a way in, the opening starts from it. Never on a price read out.
- The work of this ad is to make ordering today feel easy and safe. Say the deal the way a friend would tell them about it, in 1 or 2 spoken sentences: what it costs to start and the 1 term that takes the risk away. The other terms are on the page.
- No education and no long story. A short true moment is welcome. This is the shortest ad.
- A deadline or limit only if the offer really has one. Urgency is never invented.
- Ask plainly, once, at the end.
- How it fails: it warms up, or it reads out terms.
