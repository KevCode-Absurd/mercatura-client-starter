# Creative coverage and performance

Apply the smallest stage needed. The source course methods are adapted to this app's existing Coverage, sprint, Brand brain and landing-page capabilities. These files supply a method, not a live ad-account integration.

| Task | Load | Minimum input | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fill a creative portfolio gap | `stages/creative-diversity.md` | Allowed formats, existing tags/counts, approved product/offer, buyer/stage, available proof/assets | Ranked briefs with gap and evidence IDs |
| Compare before/after creative | `stages/paired-evaluation.md` | Matched briefs and rendered assets, approved facts, blinded labels | Pairwise scores, visible reasons, failures, ties |
| Diagnose changed performance | `stages/performance-triage.md` | Comparable dated aggregate rows, definitions, targets, change log | Evidence, competing hypotheses, investigation and proposed action |
| Review Google exports | `stages/google-diagnostics.md` | Only the selected module's typed exports, provenance and business targets | Findings and manual recommendation candidates |
| Review event measurement | `stages/measurement.md` | Aggregate event diagnostics and redacted configuration | Observed faults and verification steps |
| Report a period | `stages/reporting.md` | Validated KPI packet, comparable prior period, verified action log | Changed metrics and a concise prioritized next step |

Use approved existing brand facts and assets. Missing values are unknown; zero is an observed value. Carry source, period, currency, attribution definition and simulation status. Read only the relevant rows or findings; calculate ratios, comparisons and rollups before asking a model to write. Do not send customer identifiers or whole account exports to copywriters, image renderers or narrative writers.

Existing Coverage reads `brands/<brand>/sample-data/performance/account.json`, or labels a simulated account. Optional `performance_evidence` supplies dated aggregate clicks, impressions, purchases, value and reach with definitions. Campaign settings, event diagnostics, search terms and other modules still need actual exports/adapters. Proposals do not change bids, budgets, campaign settings or ad status.

No universal ROAS, CPA, frequency, learning-window or diversity cutoff establishes profitability, statistical significance or platform retrieval behavior. Use explicit business targets and comparable account baselines. Keep source examples separate from brand data. Creative judges assess output quality; live lift requires its own controlled experiment.

## What this reads from the brand brain

Select only the relevant approved product facts and offer conditions, buyer objections and stage, voice rules, visual rules and evidence IDs. Account diagnostics additionally need supplied business goals and contribution margin if available. Never infer targets or claims from demo data. Unknown proof excludes proof-led concepts; missing targets allow descriptive analysis only.
