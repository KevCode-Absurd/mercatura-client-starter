# Native image type: phone reminder

A lock screen with a reminder notification, or a reminders list with the same item repeating.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** Everyone knows what their own ignored reminders look like. It is the workaround, in the phone's own voice.
- **Suits:** Routines people can't keep: a cream twice a day, a supplement, getting up every hour. Good with the self-blamer and exhausted arcs.
- **A product bought once:** Only when the problem makes a chore that comes round again: "re-proof jacket (again)", "soak pan overnight", "return window closes today". With no such chore in the story, plan a sticky note or a worn object.
- **Words on the image:** 1 to 4 reminders of 3 to 8 words each, with times. Each is something the narrator does because of the problem, as the story tells it. Repetition does the work: the same reminder at 10:00, 11:00, 12:00.
- **Headline under the image:** "Read this if you've snoozed this one too."

## Briefing the image

- A generic lock screen or list: the time, a plain wallpaper, notification cards.
- Give each reminder's text and time exactly.
- A real-looking screenshot crop.

## Honesty

- Every reminder is one the narrator set for themselves. None pretends to come from a real app, a bank, a clinic, a shop, a courier or a person.
- The screen is generic: not a copy of any real operating system's interface or icons.
- No reminder is a sales message, and none names the product.
- **If words on the image are off:** plan a candid moment.

## Avoid

- More than 4 reminders.
- A reminder nobody would set.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: The moment, for the times of day; Customer language bank, for how they would word a reminder. Blank: the copy's own lines only.
