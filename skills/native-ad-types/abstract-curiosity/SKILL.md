# Native image type: abstract curiosity

A strange but ordinary image: something out of place, a detail from the story with no explanation. No product, no problem shown.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** Pure curiosity. The only way to resolve the image is to read.
- **Suits:** Crowded markets where every rival shows the same pain. A second-round test once a tension is proven.
- **A product bought once:** The empty hook where the old one hung, the third one in the bin, the dry patch on the shed floor.
- **Words on the image:** None, or 3 to 6 words from the copy that deepen the puzzle.
- **Headline under the image:** A line that links the image to the reader: "This is where I finally worked it out."

## Briefing the image

- Take 1 concrete detail from the story's opening or its first experience and photograph only that: the parking spot they pulled into, the third glass on the counter.
- It must be explained by the story's first lines, or the click feels cheated.
- Phone photo look, plain light.

## Honesty

- Curiosity, not a trick: the story pays the image off in its opening.
- It suggests nothing that didn't happen in the story: no accident, no emergency, no event.
- **If the story has no such detail:** plan a candid moment.

## Avoid

- Shock images.
- Anything unrelated to the story.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: The moment, for the place the detail belongs to. Blank: the detail and the place as the copy gives them.
- `brand-guidelines.md`: Brand world (Places, Surfaces and props, Never in frame). Blank: an ordinary place that fits the buyer.
