# Native image type: curiosity object

One object the problem leaves behind, photographed close, with nothing to explain it. Not the product and not a person.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** The reader cannot tell what it is in the first half second, and it looks like something a friend posted in disgust. The only way to find out is to read.
- **Suits:** Problems that leave something behind you can hold: hair, gut, skin, feet, cleaning, water, a mattress, a filter. Cold traffic.
- **A product bought once:** What the old one left behind: the cracked sole, the rusted blade, the flaked coating in the pan.
- **Words on the image:** None.
- **Headline under the image:** "Why your [problem] [keeps happening]", or "It wasn't [what they blamed]. It was [the real cause]."

## Briefing the image

- Name the one object from the copy's opening and where it was found: on the drain cover, in the sink strainer, on the pillowcase, on the bathroom tile.
- Close, from above or at the angle a hand holds a phone, the object filling a third of the frame. The surface under it is real and a little worn: grout, limescale, a towel that has been washed often.
- On-camera flash or flat bathroom light, slight tilt, a little softness at the edges. Name the imperfections.
- A hand may hold it or point at it. No face.

## Honesty

- The object is what the problem really leaves behind, as the research describes it, in the amount a real one would be. Not an exaggerated amount.
- Nothing in the image says the product removes it. No before-and-after pair.
- Gross, not graphic: no blood, no wound, nothing a platform would hide behind a warning.
- **If the problem leaves no object:** plan a candid moment.

## Avoid

- A clean studio macro shot. It reads as an ad for the object.
- The product, the pack or a logo anywhere in frame.
- Text, arrows or circles drawn by the image model.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: The moment (where and when the problem shows); the buyer's own words for what they find. Blank: the object the copy's opening names, marked "inferred".
- `brand-guidelines.md`: Brand world (Places, Surfaces and props, Never in frame). Blank: an ordinary bathroom, kitchen or bedroom that fits the buyer.
