# Native image type: worn object

A plain, slightly sad photo of a thing. The object is evidence of what they tried before, or of the workaround.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** It is odd enough to stop a thumb and specific enough that the right person knows exactly what it means.
- **Suits:** Stories about what was tried and failed. Strong with the quietly angry and exhausted arcs.
- **A product bought once:** This type's home ground. The old one itself (the pan with the bare patch in the middle, the suitcase with 3 wheels), or the pile of them.
- **A product that is used up:** The half-used tub at the back of the cupboard, the row of nearly full bottles.
- **Words on the image:** None, or 1 handwritten-looking caption of 6 words or fewer, taken from the copy.
- **Headline under the image:** "If you own one of these, read this."

## Briefing the image

- Choose the object the copy names: what the narrator bought, used and gave up on.
- Shot from above or at arm's length, in the place it really lives (the cupboard, the hook by the door, the car boot).
- Signs of use: dents, dust, a worn patch. No readable label.
- Flat natural light. A bit of clutter at the edges.

## Honesty

- The object is generic. Never a rival's recognisable product, pack, shape or label.
- The wear is the kind the research describes. Nothing that suggests a danger or a defect the brand can't support.
- If the object is the brand's own product after use, it comes from its reference photos and shows only what customers report.
- **If the copy names no object:** plan a candid moment.

## Avoid

- A clean product shot.
- Making the object look disgusting; it should look familiar.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: proposed `Tried before` (each kind of thing, what it cost, why it failed); What almost stops them. `competitors.md`: Gaps worth owning, for the kind of thing, never the brand. Blank: only an object the copy names.
- `brand-guidelines.md`: Brand world (Places, Surfaces and props). Blank: the place the copy puts it.
