# Native image type: emotional scene

The room where you can see the problem changed how someone lives. Nobody is in the frame; what they left behind tells it.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** It is a photo from the reader's own house. The empty frame makes them supply the person, and the person they supply is themselves.
- **Suits:** Pain that changes how people live more than how they look: sleep, snoring, relationships, confidence, energy, an ageing pet. Cold traffic.
- **A product bought once:** The workaround left standing: towels along the window sill, the second fan, the chair nobody sits in.
- **Words on the image:** None.
- **Headline under the image:** "Why your [person or thing] ended up [where the picture shows]", or "The reason you [behaviour they are embarrassed by]".

## Briefing the image

- Take the changed room from the research, in the buyer's own words, and name the 2 or 3 objects that show it: the pillow on the couch, the phone charger moved there, the folded blanket that says it is not the first night.
- Name the hour and the light it gives: a hallway lamp at 2am, grey morning through a blind, the television's blue on the wall.
- Phone photo, taken standing in the doorway, slightly tilted, a little noise in the dark areas.
- Real clutter: a mug, a remote, shoes. Nothing styled.

## Honesty

- A scene of a kind the research describes, not a claim about one family. Nothing in the headline says whose house it is.
- No children in frame, and no scene that implies harm, an accident or an emergency.
- The product is not in it, and nothing in the frame is a result of the product.
- **If the research gives no changed room:** plan a candid moment.

## Avoid

- A person looking sad at a window. That is stock photography.
- Hotel-clean rooms, matched cushions, studio daylight.
- A second, happier photo beside it.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: The moment; what changed in how they live; who else is affected. Blank: the scene in the copy's opening, marked "inferred".
- `brand-guidelines.md`: Brand world (Places, Surfaces and props, Light and time of day, Never in frame). Blank: an ordinary home that fits the buyer.
