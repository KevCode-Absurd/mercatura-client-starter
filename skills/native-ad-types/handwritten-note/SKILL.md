# Native image type: handwritten note

A photo of real-looking handwriting on paper: a note left for someone, a page from a notebook, a list on the back of an envelope.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** Handwriting is personal and rare in a feed. People read it.
- **Suits:** Things felt and not said aloud. Strong with the self-blamer and reluctant believer arcs, and with older readers.
- **A product bought once:** Works when the story gives someone a reason to write: what has been spent, added up on an envelope; a note left with a gift. With no such reason in the story, plan a worn object.
- **Words on the image:** 8 to 25 words in handwriting. The first line is the hook. They are a line the narrator wrote in the story, or the story's opening said as a note.
- **Headline under the image:** "Read this if you've written something like this."

## Briefing the image

- Lined or plain paper on a real surface (a kitchen table, a bedside table), a pen nearby.
- Ordinary adult handwriting, legible, slightly uneven, 1 crossing-out at most.
- Give the words exactly. Ask for correct spelling.
- Shot at a slight angle in natural light.

## Honesty

- The note is the narrator's own. It is not a letter from a real, named person, a doctor or an authority, and it is not a customer's letter to the brand.
- No signature with a full name. No claim about the product.
- **If words on the image are off:** plan the same scene with the page blank and the pen beside it.

## Avoid

- Calligraphy, or a font pretending to be handwriting.
- Long paragraphs nobody can read on a phone.

- `customer-avatars.md`: Customer language bank, for the words; per avatar, The moment, for where the paper lies. Blank: the copy's own lines only.
