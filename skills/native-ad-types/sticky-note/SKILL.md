# Native image type: sticky note

A sticky note in the place where the problem shows up: on the fridge, the bathroom mirror, the inside of a cupboard door.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** It is a tiny scene. The place and the few words together tell the workaround in 1 glance.
- **Suits:** Workarounds and habits: reminders to do something, to take something, to not do something. Good with the exhausted and self-blamer arcs.
- **A product bought once:** Strong. The care rules and workarounds people stick on things: "do NOT put in dishwasher", "eggs: use the OTHER pan", "take the old coat if it rains".
- **Words on the image:** 3 to 10 words, in marker or pen. A reminder or an instruction to self that the story mentions or plainly implies.
- **Headline under the image:** "Read this if there's a note like this in your house."

## Briefing the image

- 1 note, or 2 or 3 where repeated failed reminders are the point.
- The surface says where we are: a fridge door with magnets, a mirror with a toothbrush in view, a cupboard door by the hob.
- Give the words exactly; slightly rushed handwriting.
- Close enough to read on a phone.

## Honesty

- The note is the narrator's reminder to themselves. It makes no claim and names no product.
- **If the story has no workaround or habit:** plan a candid moment.
- **If words on the image are off:** plan the same scene with the note blank.

## Avoid

- A wall of notes.
- Neat designer lettering.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: The moment, for where the note is stuck; Customer language bank, for the words. Blank: the copy's own lines and the place the copy names.
- `brand-guidelines.md`: Brand world (Places, Surfaces and props). Blank: an ordinary kitchen or bathroom.
