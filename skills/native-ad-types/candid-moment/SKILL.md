# Native image type: candid moment

A photo that looks like someone took it of themselves or was caught by someone nearby, in the middle of a moment the story describes. The default native image.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** The reader sees their own day. Recognition stops the scroll before they have decided whether it is an ad.
- **Suits:** Any tension with a moment a camera could catch. Pairs with every arc. The safest first test, and the fallback when another type can't be made.
- **A product bought once:** The moment the old one fails, or the workaround: scraping the pan at the sink, standing in the bike shed in a soaked shirt.
- **Words on the image:** None.
- **Headline under the image:** "Read this if [the moment, as something they do, in their words]."

## Briefing the image

- Name the exact moment from the copy's opening or breaking point: where, what time of day, what the hands are doing, what is in reach.
- A fictional adult who fits the buyer's age and look, in ordinary clothes, not posed, not looking at the camera unless it is a selfie. Hands, a shoulder or a view from behind is often stronger than a face.
- Phone camera framing: slightly off-centre, a real room or car, daylight or lamp light.
- Show the strain or the annoyance in posture and hands, not in a face pulled for the camera.
- For a problem on the body (skin, hair, weight), show the routine or the workaround, not the body as the problem.

## Honesty

- The person is made up. Nothing in the picture or the headline says they are a customer, and they are no real person's likeness.
- No children. A story about a child or a pet shows the adult's hands, or the object.
- No before-and-after of a result the product can't show.

## Avoid

- Stock-photo smiles, studio light, theatrical pain.
- The brand's product in frame. If the story's moment needs it there, it is the real one, from its reference photos.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`, per avatar: The moment (where and when the problem shows, what they would be doing); Who they are. Blank: the moment in the copy's opening, marked "inferred".
- `brand-guidelines.md`: Brand world (Places, Surfaces and props, Light and time of day, Never in frame). Blank: an ordinary room that fits the buyer.
