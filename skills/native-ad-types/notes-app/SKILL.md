# Native image type: notes app

A phone screenshot of a note: a list, a count of days, a few lines typed to nobody.

- **Why it stops the scroll:** A note to self is private. Reading one feels like seeing something you weren't meant to, and the text is read by reflex.
- **Suits:** Stories about trying and failing, tracking, restarting. Strong with the self-blamer arc.
- **A product bought once:** A list of the ones bought, with the year and how each ended, or a running total of what they cost.
- **Words on the image:** 12 to 30 words: a title of 2 to 4 words and 3 to 6 short lines, typed as a person types (lower case allowed, a date or a count). They are the narrator's note as the story quotes or describes it.
- **Headline under the image:** "Read this if your notes app looks like this."

## Briefing the image

- A generic notes screen: title line, a few short lines, a system-style font, the time in the status bar.
- The words are the image. Give them exactly, line by line.
- A slightly cropped screenshot, as people share them.

## Honesty

- The note is the narrator's own and agrees with the story. It names no product, states no result and makes no claim.
- The screen is generic: not a copy of any real app's interface, icons or logo.
- **If words on the image are off:** plan a candid moment of the narrator's hands on the phone, screen not readable.

## Avoid

- More than about 30 words.
- A note that reads as written for an audience.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `customer-avatars.md`: Customer language bank, for how this buyer would type it; per avatar, The moment, for the time on the screen. Blank: the copy's own lines only.
