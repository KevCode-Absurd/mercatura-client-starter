# Advertorial: the page an advertorial ad leads to

For a step that plans a static advertorial ad, or outlines the page behind it. The ad's own rules are in `script-format-advertorial`; the picture's rules are the planner's own. This card is only about the page and what the ad hands to it.

## What the ad hands to the page
- **The headline.** The page's headline is the ad's headline, or keeps its main words. No brand or product name in either.
- **The question.** The ad opens 1 question (why this happens, why the usual fix fails). The page's first 2 paragraphs start answering it.
- **The buyer.** 1 buyer, the same one, in their words.
- **The offer.** If the ad names an offer, it is the page's first buying option, same name, same price.
- **The picture.** The page's first image shows the same product, pack and variant as the ad.
Plan the ad so each of these can be repeated: a headline that works as a page headline, 1 question, 1 buyer.

## The page's outline, in order
1. Headline about the problem or what was found. Marked "Advertisement".
2. The problem in the reader's own terms.
3. What people try first, and why each falls short.
4. The cause underneath: where the mechanism is explained.
5. The product, as what follows from the cause.
6. Who it is for and who it is not for.
7. Proof, the offer, the guarantee or returns, the questions.
The button comes after 5.

## By kind of product
- Bought again (a serum, a snack): the cause is what is in the usual product or how it is taken; the offer is a pack, a starter bundle or a subscription.
- Bought once (a pan, a jacket): the cause is how the usual product is made or what wears out; the offer is 1 price with returns and warranty. No "supply", no price per day.

## Honesty
- It is advertising and is marked so. No publication name, masthead, news date or "as seen in".
- Told in the brand's voice. No made-up founder, customer, doctor or journalist, and no author line with a name the brand's facts do not hold.
- No review score, review count, press logo, study or statistic that is not in the brand's facts. A review shown is a real one, word for word.
- Claims, prices and guarantee terms come only from the brand's facts. What is not on file is left out.
