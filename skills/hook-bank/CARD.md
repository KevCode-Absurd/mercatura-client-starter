# The hook bank: the card

<!-- The rules of SKILL.md with the explanation taken out. The hook writer is sent the picked callouts and shapes themselves (src/lib/hooks/bank-info.ts bankBlock); this card is what a step set to "focused" reads about them. -->

- The first 0 to 3 seconds of an ad say who it is for. On-screen words land in under 2 seconds.
- Name the buyer by what they live with, the moment they are in, how they see themselves, or what they want instead: from their own record, never made up. What they do, not their age or sex. No "you" statement about a health condition.
- A script is dealt 3 shapes from the bank by its own number: other scripts are dealt others.
- A shape from the bank is a way in, never a line to fill. Use one only where this ad can hold it, say it wholly in the buyer's own words, and put nothing in a blank that the ad does not state.
- A line in a founder's, a customer's or an expert's own voice is for a real person to say. A generated presenter never says "I tried", "I didn't believe" or "I'm a [profession]".
- A line with a number, a count or a result needs that fact from the brand's material, exact.
- No 2 hooks of a set open the same way.
- A cold buyer's hook holds no offer, no call to action and no feature list. A ready buyer's hook may state the deal.
- A founder's hook is 1 of 5, each only where it is true: the frustration origin, the credential, against the grain, the hard moment, the process shown. A fact the brand does not hold is a prompt for the founder to answer on camera.
- Cut: really, very, just, actually, unique, amazing, incredible, high-quality, game-changing.
