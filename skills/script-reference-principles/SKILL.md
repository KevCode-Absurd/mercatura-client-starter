# Script from a reference's principles

The job: the reference gives you 1 idea about persuasion. Everything else comes from the brand. The finished script should not be recognisable as a version of the reference: different sequence, different words, different shots. It is judged as any script from research is, and on whether someone shown both ads would say they share an idea, not a template.

You are given why the reference worked, not its script. That is on purpose. If you were shown its lines you would drift toward them.

The method is `script-from-research`, loaded with this file. Its rules hold in full: nothing invented, no result reported by a speaker who is not a real customer, placeholders for what is missing, and the rules of the app above both.

## Steps

### 1. State the principle in 1 sentence

Turn "what to borrow" into 1 transferable principle, stripped of the reference's subject and of how its product is bought.

| The reference did | The principle |
|---|---|
| A fake apology that turns into a price drop | Open by conceding something, so the turn lands as a surprise in the buyer's favour |
| The least likely person out-doing everyone | Use the least likely case as proof that the method, not the talent, does the work |
| 3 "yes, it's true" lines before the reveal | Agree with the buyer's doubts in a rhythm, then break the rhythm |
| The product run under a tap for the whole ad | Let 1 unbroken demonstration do the arguing, and say less |

Put this sentence first in the notes.

### 2. Check the principle fits this brand

Ask what the brand has that the principle can act on: a true fact, a real customer finding, a proof it really holds, something its product visibly does. If the principle needs something the brand doesn't have (a result, a price drop, a real person), pick the next principle from what to borrow, or say in the notes that the reference doesn't transfer and write from research alone.

### 3. Decide where the principle acts

A principle shapes 1 or 2 things, usually the hook device and the way the mechanism or proof is delivered. It does not set the sequence. Where a script type is loaded, the type sets the order and the principle acts inside it. Say in the notes which beats it shapes.

### 4. Write the script with the from-research method

Follow the steps of `script-from-research` in full, from taking stock to the test. Apply the principle at the beats you chose in step 3.

### 5. Check distance from the reference

- [ ] The order of ideas is the brand's, not the reference's
- [ ] No claim, number, offer term, story, person or shot comes from the reference
- [ ] Nothing listed under "do not borrow" is in the script
- [ ] Someone shown both ads would say they share an idea, not a template

## What this reads from the brand brain

What `script-from-research` reads, from the same places, with the same answer when a field is blank. From the reference it reads only how it persuades, what to borrow and what not to. Nothing about the reference's brand reaches the script.
