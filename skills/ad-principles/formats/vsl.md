Format: long narrated video (VSL, explainer).
- It carries teaching: narration over pictures that change every 2 to 4 seconds. The viewer stays for as long as they are still learning something that leads to what they get.
- Length is earned by what happens in it: for a buyer who does not know the answer yet, the scenes of the problem, what they tried and why it happens; for one who does, separate reasons and real proof. Never fill time with adjectives, a repeated point or proof the brand does not have. If there is no more to tell before the time runs out, write shorter and say so in the notes.
- Open cold: a scene or an observation. Leave a question open and make them wait for the answer.
- It may go round more than once (a problem, why it happens, what answers it, proof), each pass with something new. A light ask can follow each pass.
- 2nd or 3rd person narration. 1st person only for a real person's true account.
- Each visual note starts with the kind of picture: on file, product photo, diagram, text card or generated scene. A diagram shows only what the line claims.
