Format: 1 person talking to a phone camera (UGC).
- It carries a face and a voice the viewer believes. Strong for a problem told from inside, a doubt answered, the product in a real hand. Weak for anything that needs a diagram.
- It is talk: 1 person telling a friend. A half-finished thought is fine. A list of features is not.
- A generated presenter is not a named customer, the founder or an expert. They may tell a story as a person like the buyer, built only from what real customers report: the before, what was tried, the ordinary days after. No figure, medical result or review is put in their mouth.
- Shots: where they are, what is in their hand, what they do. The product is opened and used the real way (see the lessons).
