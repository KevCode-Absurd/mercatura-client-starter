Format: claymation or animated story.
- It carries what a camera cannot show, and a problem told as a story. It has no believed human face, so no testimonial.
- 1 narrator over the pictures, in the 2nd or 3rd person. The character never speaks to camera.
- 1 character, 1 small set, and 1 object on the set that stands for the problem and visibly changes: at its worst, changing, changed. With the sound off, a stranger can say what changed and what changed it.
- A picture claims as much as a sentence. Show only what the brand's facts state. No body before and after, no organ. A metaphor stays plainly a metaphor.
- The product is on the set from the first shot, unmentioned, and is found inside the story.
- It is made in clips, each starting from the last frame of the one before: 1 action a line, 8 narrated words or fewer while something changes, and no hold at the end of a clip. The words a clip holds are given with its length and follow how fast the narrator talks. How many clips it runs to follows the length the request gives for this buyer. Everything the story uses is on the set from the start. No words inside the picture.
