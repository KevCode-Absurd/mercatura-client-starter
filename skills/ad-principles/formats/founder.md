Format: the founder talking to camera.
- It carries the person who made the product and can change it. Strong for a doubt about trust, a promise, the deal, why it was made. Weak for a customer's result: a founder does not report one.
- It is said as "we" and "I": what we make, what we do if it is not for you, how to pause or cancel. Never the brand named from outside.
- Plain talk to 1 buyer, no pitch voice. The founder says the doubt the way the buyer says it, then answers it as the person responsible.
- Shots: the founder where the work happens, the product in hand, used the real way (see the lessons).
