# Anime as a still image: 1 key pose and 1 balloon

Judged on 1 thing: at the size of a thumb, a stranger sees 1 drawn hero in 1 strong pose with the product, and reads the headline once.

## The frame

- **Choose the peak of 1 device.** Transformation: 2 panels, the old way muted, the changed scene in full colour. Dramatic reveal: the hero holds the product out, a burst behind it. Versus: a diagonal split, the hero against the old way. Power-up: the hero mid-pose with the product, a charge of light around them. Talking hero: the hero faces the viewer with 1 balloon. Explainer: 3 panels at most.
- **By stage:** problem aware, the old way drawn large; solution aware, the versus or the power-up; product aware, the reveal.
- **The pose is the story.** Filled in black, the hero's silhouette still reads. 1 hero, a big simple expression.
- **1 kind of effect,** behind the hero, pointing at the product.
- **Composition:** in a tall frame the hero stands on a diagonal, the product at the hero's hand in the lower half, the top third quiet.
- **The product:** where the ad shows it, it is drawn at its true shape and colours, front to the viewer, large enough for its label to read. Where the ad leaves it out, the frame holds the hero and the old way alone.

## Words on the picture

- 1 headline of 8 words or fewer, as clean type in the brand's own typefaces, on a flat quiet area: the top third or a plain colour band. It says what the pose cannot.
- 1 speech balloon or 1 caption box is allowed, never both: 8 words or fewer. A balloon is the hero speaking as a guide, its tail to the hero's mouth; a caption box is the narrator. Neither states a result of the hero's own.
- 1 smaller line or a row of 2 or 3 chips at most, and a button only where there is an offer. 20 words in all, the balloon or caption included.
- A versus may label its 2 sides in 3 words in all, counted in the 20. The rival is "the old way", never a competitor's name.
- Every word is given exactly, in quotes. No sound-effect words, no other lettering. The product's own label is not counted.

## For the image prompt

- Say what it is: an original key frame from a hand-drawn animated ad, the drawing filling the frame.
- Then the look. `cel` (the default): clean confident outlines of 1 even weight, flat colour fills, 1 hard-edged shadow tone and 1 small highlight to a surface, a simple painted background. `inked`: bold black brush outlines that swell and taper, solid black shadows, flat colour. `manga`: fine black pen line, screentone dots in 2 greys, solid blacks, white paper, and 1 accent colour on the product only. `graphic-novel`: textured ink line, muted colour in a narrow palette, deep shadow shapes. `pop`: thick black outlines, saturated flat colour, large halftone dots, a 1-colour background.
- `retro-ova`: painted cels and subtle grain. `cyberpunk`: urban depth and controlled neon. `deadpan-comedy`: calm face against one exaggerated event. No paper/VHS/panel artifacts unless selected.
- Describe the hero in full: an original adult, 7 to 8 heads tall, build, hair as 1 clear shape and its colour, outfit in 2 or 3 flat colours, 1 mark. Adult facial proportions.
- Light is flat: 1 shadow tone, falling from 1 named side. Name the angle (low for power, level for talk), three-quarter view.
- The product is drawn from its photo in the same line and flat colour, at its true geometry and colours. State every line of its label exactly, in quotes, with its colour. No effect line crosses it.
- A balloon is white with a thin black rule.
- Not a 3D render, not a photograph, not soft airbrushed shading. No existing character, series, artist or studio.

## What fails in a still

- Fine detail everywhere: the silhouette is lost at the size of a thumb.
- A hero who reads as a known character, or as a teenager.
- 2 balloons: a conversation nobody reads.
- A powered-up body: a result the brand does not claim.
