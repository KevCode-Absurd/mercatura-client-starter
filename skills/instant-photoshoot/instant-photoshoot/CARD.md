# 1 shoot in 1 color world: rules for planning the frames

A set of photographs of 1 product that look made in 1 afternoon: they share 1 color world,
1 light and 1 small kit of props. No words on any of them. The set is a kit for a product
page, a carousel, social posts, an email and base pictures for ads; cut together fast it is
a montage.

A set is judged on 3 things: the product is the real product and identical in every frame;
laid side by side the frames read as 1 shoot; every frame has a job.

## Decide the direction once, for the whole set

- **The color world.** 1 of 4, with 1 to 3 colors by hex from the brand's palette:
  - Monochrome flood: ground, surface, props and clothes in 1 signature color and its tones. The default.
  - Multicolor pop: a different flat saturated ground per frame. For a product whose variety is the message (flavors, shades).
  - Muted editorial: quiet tones taken from the product's own materials. For clothes, bags, tools, hard goods.
  - Earthy organic: clay, olive, cream, stone, warm daylight. For a product sold on what it is made of.
- **The kit of props:** 3 to 6 real things this product is made of or used with, repeated across the set. Never another brand's product, never an ingredient it does not hold.
- **The places:** 3 to 6 where this buyer really uses or keeps it, plus plain surfaces tinted to the color world.
- **The light:** 1 recipe for every frame, with 1 frame on purpose in contrast.

Say the direction in every scene, in the same words, so no frame leaves it.

## The frames, each with its job

1. **Clean hero:** the product alone on a plain ground in the main color (a soft or large thing folded, hung or laid flat). Always first. For a product page and catalogue ads.
2. **Held:** an adult hand holding it up, label to the camera. Scale.
3. **On a body:** a worn product, front, back, cropped detail, in motion. Fit.
4. **At the skin:** an applied product, the real amount, a swatch. In use, texture.
5. **In action:** the task, the pour, the bite or sip at the moment that matters. In use, for an ad.
6. **Very close:** the texture, material or finish. For a carousel card.
7. **With what it is made of:** beside its real ingredient or raw material.
8. **On a surface:** stone, oak, linen or plaster in the set's light. A calm second hero.
9. **In its place:** where it is used or kept. For an email header or a banner.
10. **From above:** laid flat with a few props, or everything in 1 order.
11. **Color ground:** 1 bold flat color and clear empty space. A base for an ad.
12. **Moody:** directional light, deep shadow. The contrast frame.
13. **Group:** 2 or 3 of the same product, or its real variants. For a bundle ad.
14. **Small in space:** the product small, a great deal of clean space. Room for words set later.
15. **In motion:** a pour, a drop, steam, cloth in wind. Only motions the product really makes.

## Which frames, by kind of product

- **Worn** (a jacket, a ring): about half the frames on a body; the cloth and a seam or clasp very close; laid flat from above; 1 in its place.
- **Applied** (a serum, a lipstick): at the skin, the texture very close, a drop in motion, the shelf it lives on, the routine from above.
- **Eaten or drunk** (a snack, a coffee): the food itself very close, the pour, served, the bite or sip, with what it is made of.
- **Used with the hands** (a pan, a suitcase): in action 3 times (the key moment, the result, another use), the grip, a joint very close, open or stored.
- **Placed in a room** (a candle, bedding): in its place 3 times (the room wide, closer, in its state of use), the material very close, held once for size. Few people or none.

Every set has the hero, 1 scale frame, 1 very close frame and 1 frame with clear space. No 2 frames share a composition.

## What must be true of the product

- Identical in every frame: the same label, shape, colors and finish.
- **The color world floods the set, never the product.** The product keeps the colors of its photo; colored light and ground stop at its edge.
- It stands off the ground. If it is the color of the flood, the ground is a lighter or darker tone.
- Only what its photos show. A side, an inside or a use with no photo is not drawn.
- 1 use is 1 real amount, from the real opening. It is worn the way it is worn.

## People

- Fictional adults, from the brand's buyers, dressed in the color world. Say what each hand does; no finger over the label.
- No child, and no part of one: a product for children is shown without one. Nobody is a customer, a founder or an expert.
- Some frames always have nobody in them.

## Camera

- Photographed, not rendered: a real lens, shallow focus, true textures, fine grain.
- Vary distance and angle from frame to frame: front on, from above, very close, wide.
- In frames meant for a montage the product sits at about the same height and size, so it holds still while the world changes.

## Avoid

- A frame outside the color world; a prop from the category in general.
- The product tinted by the flood, or lost against it.
- A result the brand does not claim; a scene it avoids.
- Words, logos, borders. A glossy rendered look.

## After the render, look at

- All frames side by side: 1 color, 1 light, 1 kit of props? Any 2 alike?
- The product in each beside its real photo: label words, shape, colors, closure.
- The product's own color, untinted, edge clear.
- Hands whole; no child; no second brand.
- Each frame doing its job: the hero at thumb size, the scale frame, the space left for words.
