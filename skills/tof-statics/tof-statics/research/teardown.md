# Corpus teardown — the knowledge behind this skill

Distilled from a studied corpus of 263 shipped, high-spend DTC static ads (2022–2026, across
supplements, apparel, jewelry, EDC, beverages, games, B2B) plus the production notes published
alongside them. This file carries the evidence and reasoning; SKILL.md carries the procedure.

## The load-bearing findings

**Message beats visual — tested.** Across an 800-ad month: the same headline kept winning
across dozens of different visuals; the best-performing visual collapsed under different
headlines. Copy is the variable to spend quality on. Visual is the delivery vehicle.

**One master file.** 173 of the 260 corpus stills are exactly 1080×1920. There is no 1:1
variant, no 4:5 recompose. The discipline: the image bleeds full 9:16; every element that must
be READ sits inside the centred 4:5; the lower ~25% carries scenery only. One file, all
placements, zero recompose drift.

**Type is never generated.** Every corpus ad has flawless type because the base image and the
type are separate layers made in separate steps. The base is fully AI; the composition is set
type. This deletes the mangled-glyph failure class instead of QA-ing it, and makes headline
iteration free (twenty headline variants against one approved base = text edits).

**Two-layer model.** Base Image = visual idea × visual style. Composition = format/layout.
Four production handoffs: research/angles → messaging brief → visual concepts + generation →
copy written TO the generated visual → final composition. The copy-after-visual order matters:
copy written blind produces caption-copy, not integrated copy.

**Offer statics capped under 10% of shipped concepts** — anyone can put a discount on a
product shot; it works but teaches nothing and doesn't crack cold traffic. The valuable
problem is top-of-funnel statics, and cracking a TOF format opens a whole lane.

## Strategy machinery

**Persona doc** (one core pain point per persona): awareness, sophistication, sub-pains,
trigger events, failed alternatives, desired outcomes, common phrases, questions they ask.
Two-source minimum per insight (TikTok, Twitter, Reddit, reviews, competitor reviews). Every
bullet becomes messaging. The finished doc is the QA gate all copy gets checked back against.

**Winning insights carry 5 elements:** specific avatar · clear pain point · failed
alternatives · mechanism · result. "Identify those and the ideas are infinite."

**Angle validation, 4 questions:** what pain/desire? what argument? what justifies it?
why does it matter to this avatar?

**Differentiation test:** only two ways to win — a pain few are selling a solution for, or a
common pain solved in a way nobody else is.

**Angle multiplication:** hold a proven pain point, swap the persona — new vocabulary, new
trigger events, new contexts. Cheapest source of new winners.

**Messaging diversity ≠ creative diversity.** Visually distinct ads making the same argument
are one message with several coats of paint. Count diversity at the argument level; it unlocks
new pockets of spend that visual variety alone cannot.

**Planning loop:** analysis → hypothesis table + visual strategy → creative forecast (how many
ads get X messaging × Y format, planned ahead) → production → launch → analysis, repeat.

**Post-mortems are Pareto:** a winner has 100 explainable variables; name the top 1–3 and
build the next hypothesis from those alone.

**Video→static transfer is messaging-only.** Pain/desire, failed alternatives, problem
mechanism, solution mechanism, awareness, sophistication. Format observations transfer
nothing. The message that earned the attention lives in the first 15–20 seconds.

## Copy law

Headline rules: plain english (no jargon unless sophistication demands) · fewest words
possible · written to one person · comprehensible with zero context. Plus: simplicity >
cleverness, specificity > ambiguity, and the headline should deliberately exclude non-target
readers. Hard budget: headline + subhead ≤ 90 characters combined, trending shorter — the
shorter the headline, the easier it is to consume. Sound human; avoid vague, clever, or
AI-sounding copy.

## Where the AI line sits

AI for data collection and organization; humans for data consumption and prioritization.
Designers who depended on AI completely produced average work; designers who brought their own
ideas and used AI to enhance them were night-and-day better. The real skill in an
everyone-has-the-data world is prioritization: looking at 100 insights and knowing which 5 to
test. And the reason gates exist: statics made end-to-end with AI look like statics made
end-to-end with AI.

## Market reads worth keeping (as of mid-2026)

Single-CBO era over; more involved media buying back. Multiple simultaneous funnels working.
Raw UGC ripping again. Degraded AI output visibly failing. Few have TOF statics figured out.
Brands getting the most from creative run several structures at once: a CBO scaling campaign,
a cost-cap holding the top winners, a separate ABO for another offer.
