# Research & angle worksheets

## Persona doc template

One doc per persona. A persona is DEFINED by its overarching pain point — if two "personas"
share the same core problem, they are one persona with two contexts.

```markdown
# Persona: [name — e.g. "The Reactive Problem-Solver, allergy edition"]

**Overarching pain point:** [one sentence, one problem]
**Awareness:** [unaware | problem | solution | product | most]  **Sophistication:** [1–5 + why]

## Sub pain points          (each: bullet + 2 sources)
## Trigger events           (what happened this week that makes them buy today)
## Failed alternatives      (what they tried, why it disappointed — their words)
## Desired outcomes         (the after-state, concrete and visible)
## Common phrases           (verbatim quotes — future headlines live here)
## Questions they ask       (verbatim — subheads and objection copy live here)
```

**Two-source rule:** every bullet cites ≥2 independent sources (review + Reddit thread;
TikTok comment + competitor review; survey answer + support ticket). One source = parked in a
"candidates" section, not usable for copy.

**The doc is the copy QA gate.** In P7 every headline/subhead must trace to a specific bullet.
If copy can't name its bullet, the copy is invented, not researched.

## Angle worksheet

```
Angle: [name]
Persona:            [which doc]
Pain/desire:        [Q1 — what are we addressing?]
Argument:           [Q2 — the claim, one sentence]
Justification:      [Q3 — proof: mechanism, study, review count, comparison]
Why it matters:     [Q4 — connect to trigger event / desired outcome]
Insight elements:   avatar ☐  pain ☐  failed-alts ☐  mechanism ☐  result ☐   (want ≥4/5)
Differentiation:    [rare pain? OR common pain, uncommon solution? — name which]
Awareness level:    [determines how much education the ad must carry]
```

## Messaging-diversity manifest (batch-level)

| # | Persona | Argument (one sentence) | Proof | Mode |
|---|---------|------------------------|-------|------|

Rules: two rows with the same argument = one angle, merge or cut. Distinct arguments ≥ ~60% of
batch size. Offer/promo rows <10% of batch (unless assigned strategy is PROMO).

## Multiplying a winning angle

Hold the pain point. Swap the persona. Rewrite:
- their **vocabulary** for the pain (a new mom vs. a lifter describe "no energy" differently)
- the **trigger event** (baby's first birthday vs. failed PR attempt)
- the **context** the visual stages (nursery at 3am vs. gym floor)

One proven pain × N personas = N genuinely different ads. This is the cheapest source of
new winners — always check it before inventing a cold new angle.

## Video → static transfer

From any winning video (client's or competitor's), capture ONLY messaging:
pain/desire · failed alternatives · problem mechanism · solution mechanism · awareness ·
sophistication. Format observations (pacing, cuts, creator style) transfer NOTHING to statics.
80/20: the first 15–20 seconds hold the message that earned the attention.

## Post-mortem (Pareto)

A winner has 100 explainable variables; naming them all tests nothing. Name the **top 1–3**,
build the next hypothesis table from those alone. Log in the client's brain.md.
