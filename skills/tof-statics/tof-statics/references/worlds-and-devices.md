# Worlds & devices: the full art-direction library

> In the app the 23 worlds and the devices are data in `src/lib/studio/worlds.ts`, in the app's
> own words; the planner never sees this file. This file is the studied corpus behind them.
> Brand names and `(idx NN)` numbers below are citations of that corpus (most of it supplements
> and apparel), never things to copy. Read each world for the product in hand: a world written
> around the body becomes, for a pan, a jacket or a suitcase, the thing the problem lives in
> (a flaking coating, a leaking seam, a jammed wheel). Every world is held to "The product in
> the picture" and "An honest picture" in SKILL.md.

Built from a complete visual scan of the full corpus (2026-08-05). Contact sheets ship inside
this skill at `research/contact-sheets/`, **grouped by style** — `premium-*.jpg`,
`creative-photoreal-*.jpg`, `communicative-*.jpg`, `characters-mascots-*.jpg`,
`illustration-comics-*.jpg`, `native-*.jpg`, `mixed-*.jpg`. Tile labels are stable corpus
indexes — every `(idx NN)` cited below resolves via `mapping.txt` (index → category → sheet →
file). 43 curated full-size exemplars at `research/exemplars/`. When concepting, open the
sheet(s) matching the modes in your forecast — premium batches start at `premium-01`,
mechanism ads at `communicative-01`, and so on. Use this in P3: pick ONE world per ad, 1–3 devices, never reuse a
world within a batch.

## Devices (composition layer — add 1–3, all set in HTML)

Already in the skill: highlight bar · two-tone headline · leader-line callout · circular
product inset · price/offer flag · lower-third · iMessage bubbles. ADD:

- **CTA chip** — small solid pill ("SHOP NOW", "GET RELIEF NOW", "APPLY NOW", "TRY OUTTY",
  "START LOSING WEIGHT"). Present on a large share of shipped DR ads, usually bottom of the
  read-zone, brand accent or white. Brand-world ads often omit it. One per ad, max.
- **Offer bar** — "20% OFF + FREE SHIPPING" / "BUY 2 GET 2 FREE" chip next to CTA or product.
  Only when an offer angle is assigned (<10% of batch), and only the brand's real offer in its
  own words.
- **Benefit-chip row** — 2–4 tiny rounded chips under the headline ("speak your mind",
  "no brain fog or side effects") — Outty idx 80–82.
- **Speech bubble** — character/mascot speaks one line ("THOSE AIN'T GONNA WORK, BOSS."
  idx 134; "NOT TODAY BABY" idx 114; "Will Work for Nutrients" idx 84).
- **VS layout** — two labelled columns/panels fighting (surgery-vs-product health bars,
  cleaning-products checklist, nylon-vs-alpaca).
- **Strikethrough** — headline word struck through ("BUILT TO BEAT ~~MAN BOOBS~~" idx 154).
- **Big-number pair** — two huge stats linked by an arrow (idx 156). Both numbers come from
  the brand's facts. A body-change number is a result claim: only if the brand can support it.
- **Stage flags** — WEEK 0/2/4 chips under a sequence (idx 122, 131, 261). Only for a sequence
  the brand's facts state (a seasoning schedule, a study's timeline); never an invented
  timeline of results.
- **Quote-card proof** — a real review as the believability block (idx 157, 145), word for
  word, in a card of your own design.
- **Warning frame** — "WARNING:" alarm framing for pattern-interrupt (idx 124, 183).
- **Torn-paper reveal** — photo torn open to show the real scene behind (Graza olive grove
  idx 248; TONE bark idx 221).

## Copy registers seen (all obey the 4 rules + ≤90 chars)

DR symptom-calling · mechanism-factual · absurd-humor one-liner ("LIQUID DROPPINGS? THAT'S
THE GUT TALKING.") · provocation/tribal ("SOY BOYS LOVE WEAK TEES.") · warning/fear ("WARNING:
MEN IN THEIR 40S…") · identity-premium ("ONLY THE WORTHY CARRY IT.") · benefit triplet
("TRAIN harder. RECOVER faster. FOCUS longer." — deliberate staccato, premium display only) ·
B2B DR ("$250K–$5M. APPROVED IN 24 HOURS. FUNDED FAST").

## Premium is a register with FLAVORS, not one formula

Constant across all premium work: ONE committed world derived from brand identity; product
monumental or fetishized; shorter, identity-led copy; elegant type (serif mixes allowed);
proof shrunk to one spec or one line; CTA chip optional.

Flavors observed: **brand-color pop** (Magna yellow, Hormbles lime) · **dark luxury**
(CRAFTD, TONE) · **mythic-epic** (Ridge) · **dreamy pastel dissolve** (BRĒZ) · **storybook**
(Tales) · **vintage-gourmet** (Graza). Pick the flavor from the brand's own identity, then
commit the whole batch's premium ads to it.

## Verticals proven

Supplements (human + dog + chicken), apparel, jewelry, everyday-carry goods, beverages, card
games, fitness hardware, B2B lending, law, agencies. The system carries; only the persona work
changes, and with it which worlds fit: body worlds for products about the body, object worlds
(diorama, cutaway, comparison, monument) for products that are made, worn or cooked with.
