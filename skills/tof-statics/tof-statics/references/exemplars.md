# Exemplar map — open these images to calibrate

All files in `research/exemplars/` inside this skill (43 shipped, high-spend DTC ads,
downscaled to 1080w). They ARE the bar — before concepting a batch, open the ones matching
your intended modes. For breadth, the full corpus is in `research/contact-sheets/` grouped by
style (premium / creative-photoreal / communicative / characters-mascots / illustration-comics
/ native); tile labels are stable corpus indexes, resolved by `mapping.txt`.

## The canonical pair (Creative vs Communicative)

- **Creative** — `creative-sciatic-nerve.jpg` — photoreal studio black; red/violet nerve root
  system erupting from a man's lower back. "PAIN SHOOTING FROM YOUR LOWER BACK TO YOUR LEG?"
  in the quiet area; subhead names the mechanism; product bottom-left with price flag.
  The visual makes the pain FELT.
- **Communicative** — `communicative-clay-rebound-chart.jpg` — soft 3D claymation line chart:
  drop on the drug, rebound without the product, sustained loss with it. In-world 3D label
  bubbles; highlight bar on the pivot. The chart IS the argument.

## What the whole corpus obeys

173/260 stills are exactly 1080×1920. Readable content inside the centred 4:5. Type always
crisp because it was never generated. No layout reused within a client's batch. Product always
in-scene or small circular inset — never a floating cutout.
