# Shared rules (production-principles)

1. **Product from photos.** Tag roles; bind labels. Repeat 2-3 facts (`looks_like`, `pack`) verbatim; `on_camera` wins. Resolve identity/wardrobe conflicts. Known size + visible anchor; no guesses.
2. **The colour of a thing is its own colour,** not the colour of the background it was photographed on.
3. **It is opened, held, worn and used the way the brand says,** step by step. An action nobody described is not shown.
4. **Frame 1:** 1 subject, motion underway, thumb-readable, vital content clear of top/bottom bands.
5. **Camera:** physical place/distance, compatible crop/focus, one perspective cue. Alternatives vary more than height; locked views stay. Real speed; 1 action/move, timed. Materials behave; needed objects visible at start.
6. **Sound:** approved voice; local delivery outside words; cues/room below speech; music or none. Listen on phone.
7. **People:** fictional generated adults; no personal result or children. Real people use their own photos. Keep complexion/undertone/age; texture fits zone/crop/light/coverage, not uniform pores.
8. **Words:** a clip carries none; they are added afterwards. A picture carries only the words it was given, exactly.
9. **Brand world:** purposeful permitted props. Match scene light, perspective, focus, reflections and contact shadows. Phone surroundings stay visible; wear modest. Studio heroes stay clean.
10. **Check:** true product/action, subject in frame 1, anatomy/contact, intended crops, no stray letters. Upscaling preserves shot/ratio. Test hardest motion first; review the middle, not just endpoints.
11. **References:** scene role/exclusion, owner, fixed/open. Reuse structure; rebind sources. Repair one axis. Shots add evidence; live holds clarify; carry end states.
