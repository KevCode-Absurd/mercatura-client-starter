# UGC production gates, editing and delivery

Use this when taking an approved UGC script through production and final handoff. It complements [UGC script craft](../../script-format-ugc/video.md) and [Realistic AI UGC](../../realistic-ai-ugc/realistic-ai-ugc/SKILL.md). The supplied five-step workflow adds useful editing and delivery gates; it does not authorize a new shoot, batch of renders or publication. Use the same decisions from any assistant through [Harness portability](../../README.md#harness-portability).

## Agree on the delivery before making it

Record the brand's actual destination, aspect ratio, duration, supported resolution/file requirements, caption/overlay choice, sound/BGM choices and number of approved hook versions. Use existing brief/job fields or an explicit manual handoff, without inventing application fields. Reuse settled choices; clarify only an essential missing requirement. Vertical 9:16, clean/captioned pairs and multiple hooks are common possibilities, not a compulsory package. Plan framing and safe zones for the requested destination before generating a shot; a last-minute crop may cut off the demonstration or pack.

Separate three choices: subtitles transcribe approved speech; editorial brand/CTA text adds only approved messaging; audio controls voice, scene sound and optional music. A clean version here has no added captions or editorial text baked into the picture. It still shows the real product's printed packaging/logo, and keeps the selected audio. Clean does not mean muted. Honor the app's captions-off behavior and sound switches; do not add branding or subtitles against those choices.

## 1. Establish the creator and scene

Match the buyer's moment, plausible adult age, styling and environment using the approved persona and brand world. Reuse an approved presenter. A new generated adult remains fictional; a real creator or digital likeness uses their authorized references. Natural skin and room light support the selected phone look without imposing blemishes, heavy grain or beauty smoothing. Use [Identity photo libraries](identity-photo-library.md) only when reusable coverage helps the shot, not as a compulsory photo-pack stage.

Review the creator, camera support and opening scene before animation. A direct selfie leaves only one hand free; support the camera when the product operation needs both. Test the visible physical action, not only a face portrait. Separate creator approval and product checks in the review, but one sound combined still can satisfy both; this workflow does not require two paid still generations.

## 2. Integrate and verify the product

Bind the tagged original product photos by role. Preserve packaging, readable printing/logo, proportions, colors, shape, material and true scale. Verify a feasible grip, occlusion, scene light/contact and the correct sealed/open/used state. Demonstration follows the recorded handling; missing instructions mean display rather than invented use. No fabricated post-use improvement or personal testimonial. An attractive still with an incorrect pack fails before video is paid for.

## 3. Animate the approved argument

Hook → problem/benefit → product → CTA is a useful summary, not a replacement for the existing hook/setup/demonstration/turn/close method. Product arrival follows awareness, the approved script and worn-product exceptions. Keep exact words and the real evidence; a claimed benefit cannot replace a visible demonstration. Allocate coherent phrases and one principal action per shot, with approved starting states and explicit handoffs at cuts. Native video audio remains current; respect sound/BGM choices and keep voice identity stable.

Review the whole actual clip: likeness, hands, product artwork/state, contact, readable action, voice, complete words and lip timing. An opening still or a few frames cannot validate movement. Correct the smallest supported failure within existing retry limits before assembling the final edit.

## 4. Edit without changing the claim

Trim unusable starts/ends and repeated actions while retaining complete speech, useful pauses and readable product holds. Keep coherent action/voice joins and the approved CTA. Cuts add information; avoid transitions or speed changes that hide a handling failure. Preserve the clean accepted picture before overlays where supported.

Subtitles, when enabled, use the approved words and actual spoken timing; verify names, spelling and readability. Editorial text supports the argument rather than duplicating every word. Add only approved brand/CTA text, with genuine artwork or an editable layer where supported. Keep text within destination safe zones and clear of faces, hands, labels and controls. Do not silently rewrite approved words, offers or claims to fix a timing problem.

Keep speech clear, action sounds synchronized and optional music subordinate. Audio off includes speech; BGM off excludes music rather than all sound. Listen through the final edit and inspect actual mouth timing after voice repairs. If the route cannot provide separate clean/captioned outputs or editable audio, state the needed finishing handoff; do not claim that requesting them in a prompt creates those assets.

## 5. Verify each requested deliverable

Export only the agreed versions using available controls. Check actual dimensions/aspect, duration, file playback and any required encoding; a filename or prompt is not proof of output properties. Reframe another ratio deliberately and review it again rather than stretching or blindly cropping the master.

For hook versions, use approved variant IDs and check each opening with its body entry. Reuse the body only when the speaker, product state, words and visual/audio bridge still fit. Multiple hooks are distinct approved ways into the same argument, not automatic extra generations or permission to invent new claims.

Review every final file at normal speed and phone viewing size: opening reads with sound off; captions/text match that version's choice; product and action remain readable; identity and handling remain coherent; speech/mix/joins pass when sound is enabled; the approved CTA lands. Label hook, ratio, caption/overlay and audio state clearly in the handoff, with actual filenames and any unresolved limitations. Keep source/master files when the available workflow supports them. Rendering, editing, delivery and publication are separate completion states; delivery does not publish an ad.
