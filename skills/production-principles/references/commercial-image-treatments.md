# Commercial image treatments

Adapted from the supplied “Use case 1 → UGC ad content” lesson and its six commercial use cases. Choose the treatment that serves the approved argument, buyer moment and format. UGC/luxury describe capture and styling; seasonal describes context; vertical describes layout; before/after describes a comparison that needs evidence. They can overlap without becoming six mandatory new formats.

## Casual UGC and influencer-style moments

Use a plausible buyer routine, available light, recognisable surroundings and one or two incidental traces of use. Imperfect framing or exposure can help; product damage, skin flaws, blur and clutter are not required. Keep the product, exact copy and selling detail legible. A named expensive phone, camera badge or elaborate lens recipe does not establish authenticity. Generated adults remain fictional presenters, never real customers or influencers giving invented testimony.

Choose the physical capture before the pose:

- **Mirror selfie:** establish the mirror, reflected phone and free product hand. The rear camera faces the mirror. Gaze can meet the screen/reflection. Product lettering and room geometry follow real mirror reversal; do not change label spelling to make the reflection read like a direct pack shot.
- **Direct selfie, including a parked-car moment:** one hand operates the camera outside its own image and one is free. Do not draw a second visible filming phone. Car location, glare and contrast are optional context, with the car parked for the posed selfie. Preserve sufficient highlight/shadow detail to read the product.
- **Hands-only unboxing:** name the camera support that frees both hands, actual opening mechanism, what has been removed and where it rests. A still shows one coherent state; a video needs the opening/contact/state handoff. Keep real packaging and count; no assumed ribbon, gift wrap or impossible digits.
- **Table or restaurant moment:** use props relevant to the real product and routine. Signs of use can explain the moment without staging random food, adding ingredients or implying unverified use. Cup size is a scale reference only when supported by measurements or a reliable shared photo.

For each, [Environment realism](environment-realism.md), [Hands and accessories](hands-and-accessories.md) and [Product placement](product-placement.md) carry the camera/contact checks. A face-and-pack pose or tight product-adjacent portrait may belong to a lifestyle format instead of the existing caught-moment UGC format.

## Controlled luxury and editorial lifestyle

Communicate value through purposeful composition, material response, light and quiet space. Keep the brand's own palette and world; luxury does not require beige marble, silk, a particular film stock or a particular gender/age. Choose whether the person or product leads. With a product hero and background person, focus and hierarchy can differ deliberately; both inherit the scene's light and perspective.

A pinch grip works only for an object its weight and geometry permit. A surface hero needs credible contact, shadows and surface-appropriate reflection. If both face and product must be sharp, their depth and focus must support that request rather than blindly combining it with extremely shallow blur. Product prominence comes from view and layout, not invented dimensions.

## Before/after and post-use results

A realistic-looking improvement is still a product claim. Use only approved result evidence, with its actual scope and conditions. A single existing “after” image does not establish the earlier state or causation. Do not fabricate dryness, redness, aging, changed complexion or a flawless “after” to illustrate efficacy, even subtly. The same rule covers implied results in a bathroom routine without comparison labels.

For an evidenced comparison, preserve the same identity, pose, crop, expression, lighting, exposure and relevant styling across panels. Keep product position and exact approved labels coherent. Do not use makeup, retouching, white balance or different camera distances to manufacture the apparent improvement. Verify that the visible change matches the evidence rather than making it stronger. Without suitable evidence, return the result concept for revision into a supported use/process or product-detail demonstration; do not invent a transformation.

## Seasonal context

Build season through the approved location's light, weather, clothing, materials and routine. Summer can mean bright outdoor light; winter can mean cool window light plus a warm practical source. Both sources affect skin, fabric and product consistently. Snow, sand, candles, steam and beachwear are optional direction choices, not an automatic seasonal kit.

Preserve product, identity and offer across the set. A new location requires new light/reflections; a season change must not silently tan or lighten an approved person, change the packaging, invent a seasonal discount or imply an unsupported product benefit. An actual promo or deadline comes from the supplied offer.

## Vertical Story and candid movement

Design the tall composition from the start and keep approved text/logo areas within the integration's existing safe zones. Recompose for other ratios rather than clipping away hands or selling detail. Keep one immediate focal point and the product's true size. A close-to-face setup names feasible grip/contact and preserves unobstructed expression; contact with skin is not permission to imply an unverified use.

A walking moment has supported hand positions, weight and scene perspective. Limited motion blur belongs to moving areas and should not erase required product detail. A still's blur is not evidence that a video interaction succeeds. For optional video, carry the approved state into timed action and check the full movement, product rigidity, label stability and audio choices.

## Review at the delivery view

Check whether the treatment is visible without a caption naming it, whether capture physics work, and whether identity, product, scale and claims stay supported. Compare at phone size, then inspect hands, printing and visible face details enlarged. Correct one mismatch without swapping the treatment, camera, lighting and styling together. Existing approval and retry limits apply; this reference adds no required generation or new provider integration.
