# Product placement techniques

Adapted from the supplied “Place Any Product Into an AI Image” lesson. Use the relevant technique for an approved placement, product shot or local repair. These are operations within the chosen format, not new format defaults. Apply the shared [Product placement](product-placement.md) method for reference authority, size evidence, contact and integration.

## Choose the operation

An existing-scene edit preserves its camera, lighting, identity, pose and unrelated styling. A new hero, flat lay or product-first scene uses the approved brief's composition and capture look. A macro changes framing deliberately while preserving the product. Specify that distinction before writing the placement clause; “everything unchanged” cannot also authorize a new environment or outfit.

For each operation, bind references by role and include only the product-specific decisions below. The source's sample capacities, dimensions, hand comparisons, camera names, ratios and renderer choices do not establish facts about another product.

## 1. Bottle in hand

Use the product master for bottle geometry, closure, label, glass and contents; the base scene controls the person and light. Describe supporting fingers, thumb, bottle orientation and any second support such as a thigh. Keep the bottle rigid while fingers wrap around it. Inspect skin overlap and shadows at both support points. Transparent glass transmits/refracts the scene and any visible liquid; opaque packaging does not. Reflective caps pick up the actual surroundings rather than a separate studio highlight.

## 2. Surface hero

Set the upright or resting product on a named surface with a credible footprint. Distinguish its cast shadow from contact at the base and any reflection permitted by the surface finish. A matte surface needs no mirror image. Use verified dimensions or a grounded nearby scale reference; an unknown decorative stone proves no measurement. Product prominence can come from framing and focus. Marble, silk, golden light and luxury styling apply only when chosen in the brief.

## 3. Macro detail

Name the visible boundary and focus target, such as the cap/neck junction and upper label. A tight crop magnifies the view without changing component proportions or stretching the object. Keep the source's material finish and light direction; narrow focus can leave other areas soft. Show only supported details at that scale. Do not invent brushed metal, scratches, embossing, a liquid meniscus or damage to make the macro feel realistic. An intentionally cropped label is different from misspelled printing.

## 4. Specific garment on a character

Replace only the named garment. Lock identity, body, pose, other clothing and scene. Preserve the garment's cut, seams, pattern, colour and material; shoulder/arm/torso fit, sleeve and hem placement follow the actual design and available sizing evidence. Do not turn a cropped, oversized or short-sleeved design into a generic hip-length sweater. Keep knit/weave and pattern scale consistent with the garment. Describe weight, drape, compression and fold shadows around the pose, rather than laying a flat texture over the body. A convincing render alone does not verify sizing or fit.

## 5. Watch or wearable

Locate the case on the approved wrist and follow its angle. The strap wraps behind the wrist with plausible closure, tension and overlap; retain its material and design. Inspect contact shadows, strap continuity, glass reflections and case dimensions against supplied scale evidence. Neither a guessed wrist width nor the source's example case diameter sets the product size. Do not switch wrist, add jewelry or alter sleeves to accommodate the shot without direction.

## 6. Multi-product flat lay

Assign a reference to each bottle, jar or tube and preserve its identity and count. Establish relative exterior dimensions from measurements or a shared scale photograph; different capacities do not determine relative height. Describe the overhead plane, arrangement and intentional negative space. Every item receives the same coherent light, while its orientation, height and material govern its own shadow/reflection. Check that labels belong to the correct products and that no container is duplicated or merged. Props and palette follow the brand plan.

## 7. Replace a held beverage can

Replace the existing can using the approved packaging reference, preserving the person, grip and scene where physically feasible. The cylinder stays rigid; fingers occlude the correct parts. Printing follows the cylindrical surface and camera view, with natural foreshortening but unchanged spelling, artwork and placement. Do not flatten the label into a floating rectangle or force hidden text onto the visible side. Inspect rim, pull tab and opening state against references. Condensation is optional styling, not an automatic placement correction.

## 8. Food hero

Preserve the actual food's form, portion, layers, surface and ingredients. Establish scale against a supported plate/cup relationship and describe contact with the plate. Light can reveal texture without altering the recipe or inventing toppings, filling, extra food or a larger portion. Crumbs, coffee and napkins are brief-dependent supporting elements. Add steam only to an approved hot item whose state supports it, not by default to the product. Do not imply a preparation or serving result the product evidence does not support.

## 9. Tech product beside a hand

Place the supported device on the named surface beside the hand at a comparable depth. Use recorded size or an observed scale reference, not a universal “palm-sized” rule. Preserve lid/opening state, ports, parts and visible accessories from evidence; otherwise keep it closed and at rest under the existing product-use rule. Matte and glossy components receive different reflections, with contact at the actual footprint. A hand nearby does not authorize interaction, extra components or unsupported device functions.

## Product-first scene planning

When only the product reference exists, propose a setting, presentation and optional fictional adult that serve the approved ad idea. Keep the product fixed while designing its surroundings. Mark casting, styling and composition suggestions as direction choices before rendering; do not treat them as approved identity or customer testimony. Use the selected format's look, dimensions and supported tool controls. Multiple directions are useful only when requested or within the existing bounded exploration scope; this technique does not require extra paid generations.

## Review the selected operation

Compare the output with its references at delivery size and enlarged where needed: identity/printing, measured or observed scale, support/fit, material response, perspective and focus. Name the specific mismatch and use a localized correction that preserves the successful choices. If an accurate label or grip remains unachievable, follow the existing repair limits and supported editing/compositing path rather than claiming the prompt guarantees preservation.
