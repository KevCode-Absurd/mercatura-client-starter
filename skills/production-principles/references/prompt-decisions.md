# Prompt decisions, open choices and wrong-result diagnosis

Adapted from the user's *4. Why you keep getting the wrong result* lesson (2026-10-09). Use the framework without treating “most failures are the prompt” as an established diagnosis. Explicit direction can reduce ambiguity; it does not guarantee obedience, identity continuity or identical outputs from repeated runs.

## Keep direction separate from its model prompt

Use **idea → direction brief → route-compatible prompt → authorized generation → observation/refinement**. The brief holds creative intent; the prompt translates it for execution. A helper organizes decisions and proposes alternatives without silently selecting a new cast, selling argument, wardrobe, environment or capture style. User direction, approved script/plan, brand facts and format remain authoritative. “Make it less like an ad” is a capture/performance adjustment unless a message change is also requested; it does not remove required product evidence or invent personal experience.

Develop an initial idea into a short reusable record before adding technical vocabulary:

| Direction field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Purpose and feeling | What the viewer must notice/understand; express the intended feeling through a few visible cues such as relaxed posture, ordinary surroundings and restrained expression rather than style labels alone. |
| Visible scene | Subject/count, approved appearance/wardrobe, location, action or still pose, expression and gaze. Preserve the existing plan's argument and words; missing product/claim facts require evidence. |
| View and light | Crop, physical camera position/support/focus, actual sources and capture language. Rain, haze or reflections belong only when motivated and must not obscure product/text/action. |
| Reference authority | Bound input and job: product truth, approved identity/wardrobe, composition, environment/light or style inspiration. Say what carries over and changes; resolve conflicting roles. |
| Fixed / open / proposed | Preserve user/brand/format choices; bound deliberately open ones. Label a suggested change as a proposal, not an approved fact. Record routine choices made within the authorized brief without creating an unnecessary approval step. |
| Execution and review | Still state or timed event, supported route/settings, visible success criteria and the smallest useful diagnostic test. Keep existing generation authorization, budget and retry limits. |

For a still, describe one coherent photographable moment: composition, pose, contact, light and material appearance. A written walking/opening pose does not prove completed motion. For a clip, state what changes and stays: feasible endpoints, one main event, one camera behavior, timing and supported sound. A locked shot with subtle live behavior is valid; every scene does not need a traveling camera or dramatic effect. Preserve approved dialogue and the format's pace/gaze rules.

An approved reference can carry appearance and scene information more efficiently than exhaustive prose. Bind its supported role, then describe the intended change and essential preservation cues. Style inspiration cannot replace authoritative product/identity assets; a general image input is not automatically a native starting frame. Review the artifact rather than assuming that fewer words plus a reference guarantee preservation.

Keep the direction record stable when adapting to a model. Use actual documented input roles, syntax, supported settings and the format's vocabulary/order from [Models](../models.md) and the execution workflow; adjust wording/length without adding or dropping the goal, product facts, words or required constraints. There is no universal rule that one provider needs long cinematic prompts and another short ones. Do not import film/lens-maker language into UGC or resolution claims into unsupported controls. A route change is a named comparison variable, not an invisible implementation swap.

At refinement, compare **what worked → observed mismatch → proposed direction change → what stays**. Keep successful choices, change one diagnosed part within limits and update the record when direction intentionally changes. Remove or revise suggestions that conflict with user intent before execution. A first result may pass without a compulsory refinement render; stop when the requested outcome and checks are met. See [Scene direction](scene-direction.md) for audience evidence and [Visual taste](visual-taste.md) for reference transfer.

## Decide what must hold and what may vary

Start with the asset's approved job and the one thing the viewer must see or understand. Record **fixed decisions with their source → deliberately open choices and bounds → the next comparison's single variable**. A decision may already be fixed by the brief, format, approved image, product facts or reference role; it need not be redescribed with new adjectives. Resolve competing sources instead of layering both into the prompt.

Approved appearance, exact product geometry/printing/handling, words, claims and required framing are not exploratory blanks. Unknown product facts need evidence or clarification, not invention. For a new concept, a nonessential background arrangement might remain open within the brand's world while subject, action, camera and light stay fixed. Once a direction is chosen, approve the anchor and carry its relevant decisions forward. Repeated text-only results are candidates, not one established reusable identity.

## Check six visual layers

Use this as a planning checklist, not six mandatory headings or a demand to specify every pixel. Omit inapplicable person/skin fields for a product-only, note or illustration concept.

| Layer | Make the required decision concrete |
|---|---|
| Subject | Approved person/object identity, count, relevant appearance or product form, wardrobe/styling. New casting describes an adult appropriate to the brief without copying the sample woman or inventing customer/expert status. |
| Action / expression | Observable pose, grip, gaze target and expression cues. Translate “confident” into visible behavior only when it serves the brief. A still depicts a feasible state; a clip needs one readable event. |
| Environment | Purposeful place, surface and a few anchors with real scale/contact. Replace an ambiguous “luxury bathroom” with selected materials/layout; keep the brand's world and forbidden props. |
| Camera | Visible crop/boundaries, distance, height, angle, viewpoint, support and focus. Lens numbers are optional compatible direction, not facts inferred from an image or a substitute for position. Keep phone/propped/podcast rules. |
| Light | Physical source, direction, quality, relative warmth and shadow/reflection behavior. Multiple sources have a stated relationship; do not stack flash/window/overhead looks without a coherent scene. |
| Style + texture | Chosen capture/render language and material detail at viewing scale. Skin follows zone, coverage, distance and source light. “Hyper-realistic,” “professional” or “beautiful” alone leaves these unclear; capture flaws never hide anatomy, product printing or planned text. |

For video, add **subject movement and one compatible camera behavior**, feasible start/end states and timing within the supported duration. Define **sound when supported**: exact approved dialogue/voice, quieter room ambience, action sounds on their beat, music or none according to the format. Do not import electronic music into no-music UGC or replace podcast gaze with lens contact. Prose does not create an unavailable audio capability. See [Video starting points and motion review](video-generation.md) and [Dialogue performance](dialogue-performance.md).

## Write positive, prioritized and compatible direction

Lead with the main required subject/action and preservation priority, then supporting scene, camera, light and restrained style. Product-reference roles, exact words and the format's prompt/schema order still win; do not impose a universal subject-first template. Keep optional decoration last; remove redundant or conflicting synonyms.

Describe the desired construction as well as the failure to avoid: “recognizable lived-in room under the actual window light” gives direction beyond “no studio”; “subtle local relief at this distance” goes beyond “no plastic skin.” Positive skin wording does not authorize new blemishes/freckles. Keep essential exclusions for extra products, people, invented text, claims and forbidden styling. Positive direction complements those constraints rather than deleting them.

“4K,” “masterpiece” or “cinematic” is not an output-dimension control. Use supported ratio/duration/resolution settings and verify the returned artifact. A longer prompt does not guarantee quality; a numerical lens or editorial blur must not contradict a phone/podcast format. Consult [Models](../models.md) for route controls and [Skin detail craft](skin-detail-craft.md) for proportionate appearance.

## Diagnose before rerolling

Record **requested behavior → observed result → governing source/layer → fixed decisions → one proposed correction**. Compare the artifact against the plan, not the feeling that it should be “more realistic.”

- Required decision left open: define it concretely, retaining the sound approved source.
- Text/reference/format conflict: resolve authority and role first; more descriptors preserve the conflict.
- Unavailable input/control or physically infeasible action: simplify or choose a supported workflow as an explicit change. Do not keep rewriting adjectives.
- Clear compatible instruction still failed: treat it as observed execution/preservation failure. Use the approved reference and one targeted change, or stop/change the plan at the existing retry limit. Do not assume user vagueness or guarantee the next run will comply.

Keep prompt, reference roles, actual settings and result for each bounded attempt. A controlled comparison changes one decision while the rest stay fixed; deliberate discovery may leave a named nonessential choice open, but several changes cannot isolate a cause. Preserve exact dialogue and product truth during simplification. Review stills at delivery size/matched enlargement and motion/audio throughout the clip. Stop after checks pass rather than generating an unbounded exploration batch.

## Reuse a successful structure with current evidence

The AI Video Bootcamp Foundations prompting lessons distinguish a reusable prompt library from starting again each time. Retain an accepted prompt with its direction brief, bound assets/roles, actual route/settings, returned shape and the observation that made it useful. A visually accepted generation is not evidence of ad performance; record measured performance separately when it exists.

For another scene or brand, reuse the useful structure, then replace every scene-specific dependency: identity, product photos/facts, exact copy, offer, location, wardrobe and audio. Check the current route before carrying over settings or input tags. The previous output's props, celebrity, claims, logos or composition are not automatically part of the new brief. Remove unused fields; unresolved placeholders are not a finished prompt. Preserve a proven choice when it still serves the task, rather than adding every bank option to the prompt.

When refining an existing series from supplied retention data, identify the actual drop point and one change to test, such as the opening frame, delayed explanation or an unhelpful hold. Keep the message/evidence and other comparison variables stable. Different posting conditions are a confound, not proof of an algorithm rule. Save the result and its limits; no fixed posting quota, automatic publishing or guaranteed lift follows from this method.
