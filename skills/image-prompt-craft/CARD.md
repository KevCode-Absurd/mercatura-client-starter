# Compile an approved image plan

The plan fixes the argument, words, cast and format. Translate those decisions into a drawable still; never invent a new claim, product fact or treatment. This method works for static ads, product-page images and photographs. The caller supplies the output schema, reference limits and renderer controls.

Audit a borrowed or expanded prompt against that plan: remove unapproved example cast, brands, accessories, props, labels and claims. Resolve selfie/mirror-camera and focus/light contradictions; preserve exact copy, identity/reference roles and requested count/ratio. Ring lights need a real setup; “4K/8K” wording does not set output dimensions. More detail does not authorize extra renders or a new direction.

Bind every attached image once by number and job: product identity/printing, scale, action, texture, approved adult identity, scene continuity, logo, type or layout. Take only the assigned property. Do not infer unseen views from names, blend cast candidates or import an inspiration reference's product, words or people. Keep an approved identity; new generated people are fictional adults, never customers, founders or experts.

State which product form is visible and which is the hero. A form absent from the product photos cannot become the hero. Repeat two or three visible truth-sheet anchors verbatim across a set; specific on-camera overrides win. Preserve actual colour, shape, proportions, printing and open/closed state. Exterior dimensions and in-use scale need evidence; capacity supplies no dimensions. Unknown handling stays closed and at rest. Visible results require recorded support.

Describe subject/action, visible crop, setting, viewpoint, light, materials and layout zones once. Objects have real support; hands have plausible contact, occlusion and joints. State the whole verified opening/pouring/wearing action and each working hand. Match scene perspective, focus, reflections and shadows. Garments without a wearer are empty. Preserve adult age, complexion, marks and styling; natural skin detail follows crop/light rather than uniform exaggerated pores.

Follow selected brand/format authority for type and colour. Type names have visible attributes; palette colours use hex plus plain names. Product colours stay its own. A logo is supplied artwork, never typed. Native or brand-free choices carry no added brand treatment.

Use IMAGES, optional REFERENCE, SCENE, LAYOUT, optional TEXT, optional PRODUCT IN USE and RULES. Quote final text exactly once with role, position, face/attributes and colour; preserve case and qualifications. End TEXT: "Each line appears exactly once." Text-free plans omit TEXT and reserve clean space. Recompose each requested ratio; obey its supplied safe zones.

Return only requested fields. For a small observed fault, reuse the approved base, name CHANGE and KEEP, and preserve identity/product/text/scene locks. Product handling errors may need a fresh pose. Inspect the actual render, exact words and physical integration after edits; stop once it passes within the app's render cap. Resolution-only finishing preserves the source and verifies output dimensions.
