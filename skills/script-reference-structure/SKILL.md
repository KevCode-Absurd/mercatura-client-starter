# Script on a reference's structure

The job: the reference is the skeleton and the brand is the flesh. Someone who knows the reference should recognise its shape at once, and nobody should find a sentence of it. It is judged on that, and on every line being true of this brand.

Your freedom is over words only. The reference has chosen the beat order, the number of lines, the pace and the device: its rows are the beat table, and the 9-beat order and the length presets don't apply. The rules of the app, sent with the job, win over this file.

## What never changes

1. Every word is new. Share no phrase of 6 or more words with the reference, and don't closely paraphrase its distinctive lines.
2. Take none of the reference's claims, numbers, offer terms or stories. A claim that was true of their product is an invented claim for this one.
3. No invented customer, quote, review, statistic, study or expert. A speaker who is not a real customer reports no result of their own. A missing figure is a placeholder in square brackets, listed.
4. The rules sent with the job say whether mechanism and proof lines are held to approved claims.
5. Never carry over a real person, a celebrity, a child, a third party's character, or the reference's footage and sound.

## Steps

### 1. Read the breakdown

You are sent the reference row by row. For each row note its job (its beat), its device (a repeated phrase, a list, a reveal, a contrast) and how long it takes to say. Name the device of the whole ad in 1 sentence. Start the notes with a short table: row, beat, what you kept.

### 2. Map the device to something true

The device usually rests on a fact: a price drop, a new version, a result, a confession. Find the brand's fact that does the same job.

- If the brand has no such fact, keep the shape of the device and rest it on a different fact that is true. Say what you changed in the notes.
- If nothing true can carry the device, say so in the notes and write the closest honest version. Don't fake the fact.

The reference may sell something bought the other way (used up and reordered, or bought once and kept). Map each row's job, not its content: a first-box price becomes the price against what it replaces; "by day 30" becomes "by the 30th wash"; people who reordered become people who still own theirs. And the reverse.

### 3. Write 1 new line per row

Same order. Each new line does the same job as its row, uses the same device and takes about as long to say. A repeated phrase in the reference is a repeated phrase in yours (your own). A list of 3 stays a list of 3.

- Fill each line from the brand: its customers' words for the problem, its mechanism, its proof, its real offer.
- A row the brand has nothing true for stays in place as a placeholder line, listed. A dropped row breaks the structure.
- A row with no spoken words is a silent shot. Write no line for it; describe it at the end of the previous line's visual note. Never write a stage direction as spoken text.
- Person, tense and register follow the reference. Total spoken words match its count, within about 10%.

### 4. Rebuild the visual track

For each line, restate that row's shot with the brand's product in it: same framing, same kind of action, same pace. On-screen text follows the reference's pattern in the brand's words. Every shot can be made with this product as it really looks and is used. Where the reference's shot can't be (a real person, a clip from elsewhere, a child, words inside the picture), write the nearest shot that does the same job and say so in the notes.

### 5. Check what still binds you

- [ ] Promise before mechanism, if the reference has both (if it doesn't, don't add one)
- [ ] The promise is no bigger than the brand's proof
- [ ] Each hook variant leads into line 2 as written
- [ ] The product arrives where it does in the reference
- [ ] No phrase of 6 or more words is shared with the reference

### 6. Voice

The words are the brand's: its voice, sent with the job, decides them. The pace is the reference's: if its lines are short and fast, yours are.

## What you don't do

- Don't improve the structure, add beats the reference lacks, or reorder for the 9-beat method.
- Don't soften the device because it is unusual. The device is why the reference was chosen.

## What this reads from the brand brain

The buyer's words and what nearly stops them (`customer-avatars.md`), the mechanism (`products-and-positioning.md`), the proof (`brand.json`) and the offer (`offers.json`). A blank one is a placeholder line in its row, never a dropped row: start the notes with `Assumed:` and each one that was blank.
