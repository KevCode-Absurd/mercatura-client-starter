# Realistic AI UGC

**What it is.** 1 fictional adult films their day on a phone, in short hard-cut shots. The real product appears in use when the words reach it.

**Judged on:** phone look in frame 1; real product throughout; complete natural speech; demonstration readable with sound off.

Lock cast; bind appearance/motion roles. Test hardest beat; resolve conflicts.

## Structure (the brief gives each clip's length)

- Each clip contains hard-cut shots under the director's rules. Shot 1 opens mid-sentence/action.
- Each shot: 1 line to lens or 1 permitted product step.
- The voice runs on as 1 track. The presenter is seen saying part of it and heard over the hands and the cutaways.
- Each shot adds visible evidence. A brief live hold lets it register; no freeze or padding.
- New clips advance the same arc, without restarting hook/reveal/CTA. Spare time: finish the action or a live reaction; never stretch/add words.

Once words reach it: 1 product step a shot (open, use, true result, show). Plan what viewers learn; preserve gaze, count and state across cuts.

## Shot choices

- Face to lens at arm's length, mid-sentence.
- Low counter camera on the key phrase.
- Propped top-down camera for hand work.
- Over-shoulder or their view of their hands.
- Supported side view for walking/reaching.
- Second place for the "before"; close product detail.

## Camera support (every shot)

Name crop, height, position and gaze; choose scale for what viewers need to see.

- **Arm's length:** 1 hand films at/just below eye level, face close and a little wide, slight movement. Only 1 hand free.
- **Propped:** still, chest up or down at hands; both hands free. Use for 2-hand work (opening, pouring, putting on, folding).

Name support/grip. In shot prompts say "camera"/"lens", not "phone" (it can draw another). Cuts may change crop/position. Handheld moves follow the grip: closer, back, following a hand, walking. One behaviour or locked; time action/move within duration, keeping light sources fixed.

## Phone video look

- Phone video, lived-in room, its own light from a named source (left window, ceiling lamp), slightly off-centre; everything in focus.
- Leave these out of every field: cinematic, film, any camera or lens maker, film grain, dramatic lighting, bokeh, shallow depth of field, lens flare, colour grade, crane, dolly, gimbal, slow motion, epic, stunning, flawless, perfect.
- Main room: where the buyer uses it, with 2 or 3 ordinary things. Other shots name their place. No studio/backdrop or ring light in the eyes.
- Purposeful props, modest wear. Match light, reflections and contact shadows across person, room and product. Separate action, expression and gaze; capture flaws never hide the product.
- No filming device in its own selfie; establish a mirror for reflection shots.
- Same light and time of day across shots.

## The presenter

- Fictional adult, the buyer's peer: persona's age band/world, not a model.
- Scene/crop/light first, then skin/details; review full/zoom before motion. Soft forehead/cheeks, nose pores, fine eye/lip lines, varied brow hairs/jaw texture. Crop/light: subtle selfie, clearer close, 1 macro feature. Keep tone/undertone/age/marks and accessory count/placement in motion. No smoothing/etched dots/dusty fuzz.
- Makeup: choose coverage/finish/placement; keep plausible relief, not all marks equally. Keep approved look.
- Hands: pose/grip, joints/nail beds/cuticles, scale/contact. Respect occlusion. Details need attachment/overlap/material response and stable nail shape/color/finish, accessory count/design/position; no floating/fusion.
- Same plain everyday clothes in every clip; no words/logos.
- Never dressed or introduced as an expert, a founder or a customer.
- Phrase-motivated gesture/expression, then settle. No constant smiles/nods/pointing or over-articulation; hands may rest.

## The product on screen

- Approved `handling` wins: display stays sealed on support; nobody holds/opens/uses.
- Appearance from pack photo/facts; scale from actual in-hand/dimension evidence.
- Hidden contents need an actual photo, not artwork; absent evidence, display.
- If permitted, lift front to lens beside face, never over mouth.
- Until words reach it, out of frame. It arrives at a cut, in hand/use. Later shots retain its state (sealed/torn, full/empty).
- A product that is worn is worn from the first frame: its photos define what the presenter has on, and the demonstration is something done with it (zipped, a pocket used, a sleeve pushed up).
- A product that is applied goes onto the back of a hand or a forearm unless the lessons say more. No before and after that the brand's facts do not have.

## Sound

- Voice: age, pace, traits, accent; 1 clear voice throughout. Allow breaths, pauses and reaction.
- Room: tile echo, soft bedroom, open kitchen, muffled car or outdoor air. Ambience stays below speech; background voices indistinct.
- The product's own sound on the beat it happens (a cap clicking, powder falling, a zip), written into that scene's action. Not otherwise.
- No music.

## First and last frame

- First frame: presenter mid-thought/action, real room behind; the eyes on the lens or on what the hands do, as the shot's face says; vital content outside top/bottom sixth.
- Each clip lands its point without freezing.
- Last frame: product in view with its front readable (in use, set down, or held up beside the face), held still briefly. The verified front; in a hand only if permitted.

## Avoid

- A second person or active screen. Animals only for their product: in frame at shot opening, 1 action.
- Light, clothes or time of day that change across a cut.
- Filler words the script does not have ("like", "honestly") added to sound casual.
- Telling without showing the demonstration.
- A hand hiding the product name.

## Review

1. Phone look? Waxy skin/dotted pores/clipped shine: fix 1 cue, keep look; match scale.
2. Face/texture attached in speech/blinks/turns; same hair/light/clothes, no flicker/pumping?
3. Phone/headphones: exact words, tone, clear voice. Check lips/gaze and repaired-line joins.
4. Does the product match its photo, and its size against the hand?
5. Can the demonstration be followed with the sound off?
6. New evidence, no repeated cut actions; live holds clarify? Glitch trims preserve whole lines, readable use and state; otherwise repair the shot.
7. Listen to full mix: 1 voice, consistent room bed, synced action sounds, no music.

Set requested ratio/duration, captions, audio and hook IDs first. Approve creator/product still before motion; check edited exports. Clean = no added text, not muted or erased pack. Check subtitle timing, safe zones, hook/body joins; deliver requested versions only.
