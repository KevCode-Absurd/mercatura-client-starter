# Claymation as a still image: 1 frame of the story

Judged on 1 thing: at the size of a thumb, a stranger sees a small clay world with 1 thing in it plainly wrong or plainly put right, and reads the headline once.

## The frame

- **Choose 1 state of the thing that changes.** For an unaware or problem-aware buyer: the thing at its worst, the character caught mid-pose with it. For a solution-aware buyer: the change half done, at the product. For a buyer who knows the product: the changed set, the character upright beside the product. A still shows 1 state, or 2 as a split set (the left greyed, the right in the brand's colours). Never all 3.
- **The pose is the story.** A still has no narrator, so the feeling is in the body: shoulders dropped, head tilted, arms lifted. 1 character, 1 set, 3 or 4 pieces on it.
- **Composition:** the set fills the frame like a small model, seen at the character's eye level. The thing that changes is the largest or the brightest thing in the picture. In a tall frame it sits above or behind the character, and the product at the character's side.
- **The product:** where the ad shows it, the real pack stands on the set with its front to the lens, within the character's reach. Where the ad leaves it out, the set holds only the problem.

## Words on the picture

- 1 headline of 8 words or fewer, as clean type on a quiet part of the backdrop, in the top third. It says what the picture cannot: whose problem this is, or what just changed.
- 1 smaller line or a row of 2 or 3 chips at most, and a button only where there is an offer. 20 words in all.
- Type is laid over the picture. Nothing is written in clay, and no sign or label on the set carries words.
- The character says nothing: no speech bubble.

## For the image prompt

- Say what it is: a photograph of a handmade stop-motion clay set on a tabletop. Matte plasticine with faint fingerprints and tool marks, even proportions, bead eyes and a pressed-line mouth, soft even studio light, shallow focus, a plain backdrop.
- Everything except the product is clay: water, steam, light and heat are clay shapes.
- 2 palettes: the problem is greyed and cool with 1 dull colour; the changed state takes the brand's colours. Until the change, the product is the only thing in the brand's colours.
- The product is the real one from its photo, lit by the set, at a size that reads against the character (a pack waist to shoulder high). No letter of it is redrawn.
- Not a glossy 3D render, not a flat cartoon drawing, no motion blur.

## What fails in a still

- A whole story squeezed into 1 frame: several moments, several characters, arrows between them.
- A clay figure standing next to a pack with nothing changing: that is a mascot, not an ad.
- A change the brand's claims do not state, drawn because it looked good.
