## This page is a reasons-why page

It is a trailer for the product, read by someone comparing options. Every reason is a reason to choose this product over what they use now.

- **The hero headline** is 8 to 14 words. On a page for an ad it keeps the ad's main words, and the count ("5 reasons...") goes in the heading above the reasons. On a page for no ad it takes 1 of 4 shapes; pick the 1 the buyer points to:
  - The switch: the old way against the new way ("5 reasons [buyers] are swapping [old way] for [product]").
  - The gift: an occasion and who it is for.
  - The revelation: a buyer with a specific problem, and what they found out.
  - The better alternative: leaving a commodity category behind.
- **The line under it** is 1 sentence that says who is talking or why now. No figure the facts do not state.
- **Each reason** argues 1 thing, and no 2 reasons argue the same thing. Order them by what this buyer cares about most, strongest first; the last reason is about risk or the offer.
- **What the reasons are about** follows the kind of product. Bought again: what is in it, how easy it is to keep using, what it replaces, what it costs a day, what happens if they stop liking it. Bought once: what it is made of, how long it lasts, how it fits or what it works with, what the cheaper kind costs over time (only with figures the facts state), the returns and the warranty.
- **A reason's title** is 6 to 10 words and can be read alone: it is the claim, not a label. "Benefits" is a label; "1 scoop replaces the 6 bottles in your cupboard" and "No coating, so there is nothing to wear off" are claims.
- **A reason's body** is 1 to 3 short paragraphs of 2 sentences at most: what the buyer does or feels now, what changes, and the fact that makes it true.
- **Proof runs through the page.** A customer's words sit with the reason they prove. A reason with no proof to its name is the weakest: put it in the middle.
- **The offer comes after the reasons**, once, and is the offer the ad named. Under about 100 in the brand's currency, a flat price or a bundle price reads stronger than a percentage. State only the offers given. What it includes is listed item by item.
- **The FAQ** is 6 to 8 questions, each a reason this buyer still would not buy.
- **Buttons** say what the buyer gets. The same wording on every button of the page.
- Never a review score, a review count, a badge or a "bought today" figure that is not in the facts.
