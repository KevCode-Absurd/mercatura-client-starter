## This page is a product landing page

It is read by someone who knows the product, or has just seen it in an ad, and is deciding whether to buy now. Everything they need to decide is in the first screen; the rest of the page answers what still holds them back.

- **The first screen**, in this order: the headline (the ad's words when there is an ad), 1 sub-line on why this one, proof, the price, the reason to buy now, the button, the line that takes the risk away.
- **The eyebrow** is the product's name. The headline is the outcome, 6 to 12 words, never the name alone.
- **The 3 bullets** under the sub-line are the 3 things this buyer most needs to know before paying, not the 3 nicest features. For a product bought again: what is in it, how it is taken or used, how long a pack lasts. For a product bought once: what it is made of, the size or fit, what comes in the box.
- **The price line** states the price as the offers give it. A price per day only if the facts give one. A product bought once has no price per day.
- **The reason to buy now** is 1 line from the facts: the gift, the saving in money, when it arrives, free returns. If the facts give none, leave it empty.
- **The line under the button**: for a product bought again, the guarantee and "pause or cancel" as the facts word it; for a product bought once, the returns window and the warranty as the facts word them.
- **Each value prop** answers 1 doubt, in the order this buyer has them. Its points carry the facts behind it: an ingredient and what it does, a material and why it lasts, a dimension and what it fits.
- **The comparison**, when the page has one, is against the thing this buyer uses now (the cheaper kind, the old way), row by row, with only what the facts state for both sides.
- **The FAQ** is 5 to 8 questions. Bought again: taste or feel, whether it suits them, how long until they notice a change (only as the facts state), how to pause or cancel, what happens if they do not like it. Bought once: which size, whether it works with what they own, how to care for it, how a return works, what the warranty covers, when it arrives.
- **Buttons** say what the buyer gets, and say the same thing down the page.
- Never a review score, a review count, a badge, a stock line or a "bought today" figure that is not in the facts.
