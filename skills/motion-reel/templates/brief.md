# Brief

Fill every field before building, from the brand brain first. `?` means "ask". A default is used only if the person says "your call".

| Input | Value | Where it comes from, or the default if "your call" |
|---|---|---|
| Product | ? | Required. The product's entry in the brand brain |
| What the reel is for | ? | Required: a launch, an offer, "3 reasons", a bundle, a closing card |
| Product photos | ? | Required: the pack front on a clean ground. Also, where they exist: out of its pack, in a hand, in use. Never drawn or generated |
| The words, in order | ? | An approved script, or the brand's key phrases, offers and claims with sources |
| Offer and price | ? | Only as the brand's offers state them. None: the reel ends on the action alone |
| Audience and placement | ? | A social feed, read with the sound off |
| Duration (s) | ? | 10 to 20 |
| Formats | ? | 9x16 and 4x5 (also available: 1x1, 16x9) |
| Brand colours | ? | The brand's palette: one accent |
| Fonts | ? | The brand's own faces, as files (capture.mjs fetches the ones its site serves) |
| Logo | ? | The brand's logo file |
| Reference film | ? | None: rhythm from the house rules |
| Music | ? | `synth` (scripts/music.py), or a track the brand has the right to use |
| Voiceover | ? | None, unless the brand has a voice it has the right to use |
| Action / end card | ? | The action in the brand's words, with the pack and the logo |
| Must avoid | ? | The brand's guardrails and the product's "never show" |
