# Motion reel

A short film made in code, not by a video model: the brand's real product photos, its type, its colours and a few approved words, moving on a beat. A film here is a pure function of time. `window.seek(t)` paints frame t in a headless browser and ffmpeg encodes it. Picture and sound share one `timeline.json`, in beats on a measured grid.

Because nothing is generated, the pack, the label and the colour are exactly the brand's photos, and a render costs nothing. That is what this is for: **a launch, an offer or a sale, "3 reasons", a bundle, the closing card of another ad.** It is the wrong tool for a demonstration (that needs footage) and for a story (that needs a person).

## What a reel is judged on

1. Sound off, by 2 seconds, a stranger knows what the product is.
2. Every word that must be read is read at phone size, and the action is the most legible thing in the film.
3. The product looks exactly as in its photos: never redrawn, stretched or recoloured.
4. Every word, number, price and offer is one the brand approved.

The rules for the product shown true, words in a picture and spending are in `skills/production-principles`. The house rules of the build are in [reference/RULES.md](reference/RULES.md): read them before building. Also here: [reference/ENGINE.md](reference/ENGINE.md) (the engine and its commands), [reference/AUDIO.md](reference/AUDIO.md) (music, grid, sound effects, voice, mix), [reference/CRITIQUE.md](reference/CRITIQUE.md) (the reviewer's prompt).

Needs on the machine: node with playwright (chromium), ffmpeg, python3 with numpy, scipy, soundfile, librosa and pillow. `init.sh` checks them.

## 0. Intake: from the brand brain first

Fill `brief.md` (template in `templates/`) from the brain, and ask only for what is blank, in 1 round. Use a default only where the person says "your call".

- **The product and its photos:** the product's entry and its `Reference photos`: pack front, product out of its pack, in a hand, in use. A reel needs at least the pack front on a clean ground. A photo that is missing is asked for; it is never generated or drawn.
- **Colours and type:** the brand's palette and typefaces. The font files themselves must be on disk; `capture.mjs` fetches the ones the brand's site serves.
- **The words, in order:** from an approved script, or the brand's key phrases, offers and claims with sources. Nothing is written fresh during the build.
- **Length and formats; music** (made in code, or a track the brand has the right to use); **voice** (only a voice the brand has the right to use, otherwise none).

A preset is a folder with `preset.jsonc` and the brand's font files; it fills colours, fonts and defaults (`scripts/preset.mjs` lists the fields). None ships with the skill: the first reel for a brand is filled by hand and can be saved as that brand's preset.

## 1. Scaffold
```
sh <skill>/scripts/init.sh videos/<distinctive-slug> [--preset presets/<name>]
```
`init.sh` refuses a folder that exists, so 2 sessions never write into the same one. Work from the project root from here on.

## 2. Assets
Copy the product photos into `assets/product/`, each named for its job (`pack-front`, `out-of-pack`, `in-hand`, `in-use`). Cut-outs need clean edges and their own soft contact shadow is added in the film, not baked in.
```
node scripts/capture.mjs <the brand's site>
```
This gathers the site's font files, colours, logos and share image into `assets/`. Point `film/index.html` `@font-face` at the real display and text faces, and set the `:root` colour tokens.
If a reference film is given, pull frames and cut times from it (`ffmpeg`, 2 frames a second and scene changes). Take its rhythm, never its content.

## 3. Style guide
Write `docs/style_guide.md` from the template: palette (hex and where each was read), type, rhythm, transitions, camera, texture, how text enters and leaves, sound.

## 4. Beat grid
Set the bpm, duration and marks in `timeline.json`. Then:
- **Music made in code:** `python3 scripts/music.py`, then `python3 scripts/beats.py audio/music.wav --stem audio/drums.wav`.
- **A supplied track:** copy it to `audio/music.wav`, then `python3 scripts/beats.py audio/music.wav`.

Then run `node scripts/sync.mjs`. Details: AUDIO.md section 1.

## 5. Shotlist, then STOP for the OK
Write `docs/shotlist.md` from the template. The order for a physical product:

1. **Hook, by 2 seconds:** the product already in frame on frame 0 and the first word landing with it.
2. **What it is:** 1 line.
3. **1 to 3 reasons:** each a fact from the brand's facts, each with the photo that shows it (the product out of its pack for what it is made of, in use for what it does).
4. **Proof,** if the brand has it: a number with its source, or a customer's words exactly as given.
5. **The offer and the action:** an end card of 2 seconds or less with the pack, the logo and the action, never still.

Each row needs beats and time, the exact on-screen words, the photo used, motion and transition, the sound mark, the exact voice line if any, and notes for 9:16. Something new every 2 to 4 seconds.

Send the person a short summary: the shotlist table, palette, fonts, music plan, and the voice and its cost if any. **Wait for an explicit OK.** Build nothing until then. Mark the shotlist APPROVED, and reflect every change asked for.

## 6. Voice (only if asked for)
A reel must work with the sound off, so voice is extra. Use only a voice the brand has the right to use: its own recorded lines, or a voice from a voice tool under that tool's licence. Never a voice copied from another ad or from a known person. 1 take per line into `audio/vo/`, then `vo.py --scan`, write `vo.json`, run `vo.py`, then `sync.mjs`. Details: AUDIO.md section 3.

## 7. Build
Replace the starter scenes in `film/film.js` (they are placeholders that show the engine's patterns, not a layout to keep). One `C.scene()` per shot; marks and cues live in `timeline.json`. Use:
- `C.spHit` for anything with a sound
- `TYPE.rise` for type
- `C.pick` for per-format layout
- `Motion.indicator` / `track` for motion with several targets

A product photo is placed with its own proportions and scaled as a whole. Declare every sound in `timeline.sfx` as you build it. After each edit, check with:
```
node scripts/render.mjs --sheet --all        # LOOK at review/sheets/*.jpg
node scripts/render.mjs --at 3.2,3.25 [--fmt 9x16]
node scripts/render.mjs --range 3,5
```
Verify once per project that the same time always paints the same frame: `node scripts/render.mjs --verify --all`.

## 8. Review loop: at least 3 rounds, until every score is 8 or more
Each round:
1. `node scripts/sfx.mjs && python3 scripts/mix.py` (draft sound, so sync can be scored)
2. `node scripts/render.mjs --sheet --all && node scripts/render.mjs --draft --all`
3. `python3 scripts/review.py <N> --draft`
4. **Reviewer:** a fresh agent that did not write the film, given `reference/CRITIQUE.md`, the project path and N. It looks at every sheet and strip, scores the 8 criteria with evidence, and appends the round to `docs/review_log.md`.
5. Fix the 3 worst problems, check each fix with stills or clips, and log what changed.

Stop only when every score is 8 or more and at least 3 rounds are done. Do not show the person a film before that.

## 9. Final render, sound, all formats
1. **Render** the primary format: `node scripts/render.mjs`, then `python3 scripts/review.py final`.
2. **Sound effects:** re-check `metrics.sync` on the final. Nudge marks or gains in `timeline.sfx`, then run `sync.mjs` and `sfx.mjs`.
3. **Mix:** `python3 scripts/mix.py`. It must reach -14 LUFS with true peak at or under -1 dBTP and print no WARNING.
4. **All formats:** `node scripts/render.mjs --all` (or `--mux --all` if only the sound changed). Run `review.py final` again and look at every `phone_*.jpg` and `safe_9x16.jpg`. A person listens to the final mix.

## 10. Deliver
Report the `renders/<fmt>.mp4` paths, the duration and loudness, the final scores, the contact sheet, and anything knowingly left imperfect. Changes go through `timeline.json` and `film.js`, then a draft and a review before a new final.

## What this reads from the brand brain

- `products-and-positioning.md`, the product: `What it is`, `Key features`, `Core benefit`, `Price`, `Pack`, `Looks like`, `Never show`, `Reference photos` (the pictures in the film); `Bundles and offers`.
- `brand-guidelines.md`: `Color palette` (name, hex, role), `Typography`, `Key phrases and taglines`, `Unique selling propositions`, `Brand guardrails`, `Motion and sound` (`Pace`, `Sound`, `On-screen text`); and `Logo files` (proposed in this pass: the logo for light and for dark grounds, as vector files).
- `brand-look.json` (fonts and colours read off the site), `offers.json`, `brand.json` (claims with sources, the guarantee), `assets/` (the photos).
- For voice: `Motion and sound`, `Voice` (proposed in this pass). Blank means no voice.

A blank field is asked for. A photo, a claim, a price or an offer is never made up to fill a shot; the shot is dropped.
