# Strategic audit: what the brand has not tried

It maps what a brand's ads have covered against what they have not, then turns the 5 biggest gaps into concepts.

It is judged on 3 things: every gap is a true gap (the thing has in fact not run); every concept aims at 1; and nothing is called a winner that was not measured.

## In the app

The Coverage screen already counts, by rule, 6 lines of an account: awareness, market sophistication, formats, buyers, age and gender, and offers. Read it; do not count those again. This skill adds the 4 lines Coverage does not have (desires, opening types, products, competitors) and the 5 concepts.

## 1. What "tested" means, and what evidence there is

Say in the first line of the audit which of these it rests on.

| What is to hand | What it shows | What the audit may say |
|---|---|---|
| **The ad account**, by ad: what the ad says (opening line, angle), its format and product, spend, purchases, revenue, hook rate, hold rate, days running, on or off | What ran, what it cost, what returned | "Tested and won", "tested and lost", "ran without enough spend to tell" (under about 3 times the cost of 1 sale), "never ran" |
| **The founder's report** (questionnaire F8.1 to F8.6) | What the brand remembers trying, before and outside the account | "Reported as tried", "reported as a winner, not measured" |
| **An ad library only** (the brand's own page) | What is running now, since when, in how many versions, in which formats | "Running" or "not running now". Nothing about results, and nothing about ads that were switched off |

A gap needs only the first column of knowledge: that a thing has not run. So most of this audit can be done from an ad library plus the founder's report. What cannot be done without an account is the "what worked" half of section 1, and any ranking by return. Write "not measured" there and move on.

"Not known" is not "not tested". If there is no record of whether a desire was ever led with, the cell says "not known: ask (F8.2, F8.3)", not "No".

## 2. Read first

- `winning-ads.md` and `brain.md` (What we know works, What to avoid): what ran and what came of it.
- `brand-guidelines.md`: the voice, the selling points, the guardrails.
- `products-and-positioning.md`: every product, its price, the bundles.
- `customer-avatars.md`: the buyers, their quotes, What almost stops them, The moment.
- `competitors.md`: what competitors run, and the gaps. If it is empty, section 7 is skipped and the audit says so.
- `offers.json`, `recent-changes.md`: what is live.
- The reference files here, as each section names them. They are other brands' patterns, to be tested, not this brand's results.

## 3. Used up, or bought once

Decide this before looking for gaps. The gaps are in different places.

| | A product that is used up (a cream, a snack, a powder) | A product bought once (a pan, a jacket, a bag) |
|---|---|---|
| Ads usually over-cover | The first try: taste, feel, an opening offer | The product as an object: studio pictures, a list of features |
| Gaps usually found | The daily moment it fits into; the buyer who tried it and stopped; what is in it and why; the returning buyer | The moment the old one fails; proof it lasts (a year on, 5 years on); care and how it is used; fit; returns and warranty; the comparison with keeping what they own; the gift |
| Desires most often unspoken | Control, Ease, Relief | Status, Identity, Safety (of the money spent) |
| Products line (section 8) | Sizes, flavours, the bundle, the subscription | The second purchase: an accessory, a part, a matching piece |

## 4. The 10 sections

Sections 1 to 8 find gaps. Section 9 turns them into concepts. Section 10 ranks them.

### Section 1: what has run

2 tables.

**Angles**: every angle that has run: the product, the format, the buyer it spoke to, its awareness level, and the result with its basis (measured, with the numbers and dates; reported; or not measured).

**Formats**: every kind of ad the brand has used, each marked proven (measured), running and not measured, planned, or never run. Use the app's list of formats (the Formats screen) so the names match.

Then 3 or 4 lines each under "What worked" and "What did not", stating the pattern, not the ad. "A named expert on camera returns more than a customer on camera for this brand" is a finding. "Ad 3 was good" is not. With nothing measured, write "Not measured" under both and list what the founder reports, marked as reported.

### Section 2: desires not yet led with

`references/desire-map.md`. The 10: Status, Ease, Belonging, Control, Transformation, Identity, Safety, Novelty, Connection, Relief.

For each: has any ad led with it? Yes, partly, no, or not known. For "partly", say what was done and what is left. Close with the 3 or 4 that are the biggest open lanes, and why for this product.

### Section 3: opening types not yet used

`references/hook-library.md`. Against everything that has run:

- The 9 archetypes: Shock, Controversy, Break belief, Curiosity, Authority, Positioning, Emotion or story, Product, Negativity.
- The 6 strategies: Emotional driver, Curiosity-led, Myth-busting, Call-out, Problem or symptom list, Testimonial.

For each: used or not; if used, the example; if not, 1 sentence on why it could suit this brand. Pick out 4 or 5 that are both unused and a fit. An opening type that needs something the brand does not have (an expert for Authority, real reviews for Testimonial) is marked "needs: ...", not recommended as it stands.

### Section 4: awareness levels

`references/awareness-levels.md`. In the app, copy this line from Coverage. By hand, a table: level, what is running, and whether that is enough, thin or nothing. The app plans 70 to 90% of new ads for buyers who are unaware or problem-aware; the source's budget split is a rule of thumb beside that.

### Section 5: buyers not yet spoken to

`references/desire-map.md`, the micro-persona part. 6 to 8 buyers narrower than the avatars on file. Each has:

- A name in quotes that describes a situation ("The one who ruined a non-stick pan a year").
- 2 or 3 sentences: who, and the moment they are in.
- **Its evidence**: the review, ticket, survey answer or founder's remark it comes from, word for word with its source. One with no evidence is marked "inferred: check before briefing".
- The opening that would stop them.
- The product they would buy first.

### Section 6: formats and vehicles

`references/vertical-playbooks.md` and `references/universal-winners.md`. A table: format, status (as in section 1), and for each one not proven, what exactly to test and why. Close with the single biggest format gap.

A format that needs a real person (the founder, a customer, an expert, a creator posting from their own account) is only an opportunity if the brand has that person and the right to show them (`creative-kit.json`, founder questions F9.1 to F9.5).

### Section 7: competitors

Needs `competitors.md`. 2 tables:

- **What competitors lean on, that this brand has not tried**: the competitor, their angle, how long and in how many versions it has run, and this brand's own version to test. This is inferred from what is running, not measured: say so in the table's heading.
- **What no competitor is doing**: the open ground this brand is placed to own, and what in the brand's facts makes it so.

### Section 8: products

From `products-and-positioning.md`: each product (or group, for a large catalogue), how many ads it has had, and 1 line: over-covered, well covered, under-covered, none. Close with the product that most deserves ads and has none, and the reason from the brain (it sells without ads, its margin, the founder's priority in F4.1). Do not cite search volume or revenue that is not in the brain.

### Section 9: 5 new concepts

Each concept takes a gap nothing has touched. Each opens with the same thinking block as a brief (`skills/brief-v2`, `CARD.md`):

```
### Concept {letter}: "{Name}"

> **Thinking block**
> - Stage and awareness: {Trigger / Exploration / Evaluation / Purchase}, {level}
> - The belief in the way: "{in the buyer's words}" ({source, or "inferred"})
> - The belief to install: "{...}" (held up by: {the fact})
> - The moment: {where, when, doing what} ({source, or "inferred"})
> - Evidence tier: {5 to 8, or 4 if the buyer is measured}, {basis}

**Gap it fills:** {section and line}
**Desire:** {from section 2}   **Opening type:** {from section 3}
**Buyer:** {from section 5}    **Format:** {from section 6}
**Product:** {1 product}       **Call to action:** {Shop now / Learn more / ...}

**Why it is new here:** 2 or 3 sentences: what has never run, and why this mix reaches someone the ads have not.

**Headlines:** 3, each 10 words or fewer.
**Opening:** 2 to 4 sentences of the first words (for a video, the first 5 seconds, and what is seen).
```

The 5 differ from each other in desire, awareness level and buyer. Every claim, price and offer in them is the brand's own. Urgency only where it is real: a product is called new, limited or nearly gone only when `recent-changes.md` says so.

### Section 10: the gaps, ranked

The 10 that matter most: rank, gap, and why it matters (the buyers it would reach, the risk it removes, or what the account cannot grow without). Say for each whether it is known from measurement or from what is running.

## Output

- By hand: `strategic-audit-YYYY-MM-DD.md` in the brand's folder. The title, the line saying what evidence it rests on, then the 10 sections. No summary before section 1.
- In the app: the gaps join the ones on the Coverage screen, and the 5 concepts go to the Concepts screen as drafts.
- Afterwards, tell the person the biggest gap and the first concept in 2 or 3 sentences.
- Do not write to `brain.md`. The person reads the audit and chooses what to record.

| Document and field | Used for | When it is blank |
|---|---|---|
| `winning-ads.md`: each ad's format, opening, what was shown, why it worked; and, where known, its product, buyer, awareness level and result | Sections 1 to 4, 6, 8 | Use the ad library and the founder's report; mark results "not measured" |
| `brain.md` What we know works, What to avoid, Ideas to explore, Brand diagnosis | Sections 1 and 10 | Say "no record of past tests" and ask (F8.1 to F8.3) |
| `customer-avatars.md` per avatar: Grounded in, What they care about, What almost stops them, The moment; Customer language bank | Sections 2 and 5, the thinking blocks | Buyers are marked "inferred" |
| `products-and-positioning.md` Product catalog, Best sellers, Bundles and offers | Section 8, the product of each concept | Ask for the catalogue |
| `competitors.md` Competitor profiles, Competitor ads and messaging, Gaps worth owning | Section 7 | Skip section 7 and say so |
| `brand-guidelines.md` Unique selling propositions, Brand guardrails, Motion and sound: Formats in use | What a concept may claim; section 6 | Ask (F8.5) |
| `creative-kit.json` people and rights | Whether a format that needs a real person is open | The format is marked "needs: a person and the right to show them" |
| `offers.json`, `recent-changes.md` | Offers and real urgency | None is written |

Needed and not in the brain today: on each ad in `winning-ads.md`, its product, the buyer it spoke to, its awareness level, the desire it led with and its opening type, and whether its result is measured (with spend, sales and dates), reported, or not known. Without these the audit re-reads every ad each time.
