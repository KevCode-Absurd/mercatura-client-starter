# Store data request (G1: asked for once; the audit runs page-only without it)

An audit sized in money must be built on the brand's real numbers. Send the message below, then wait once. If the numbers do not come, the audit is done from the pages alone (page-only mode, G1) and says so; nothing is invented or estimated to stand in for them.

The message names Shopify's reports because most D2C stores are on Shopify. On another platform ask for the same figures from its own analytics.

## The paste-ready message (copy exactly; no long dashes)

> To size the leaks in real money, I need your actual store numbers. From **Shopify Admin > Analytics > Reports**, pull these for the **last 30, 60, or 90 days** (tell me which window), and the same window **a year ago** if you have it:
>
> **Store-wide (the period)**
> - Online store sessions
> - Conversion rate (overall)
> - Average order value (AOV)
> - Total orders and total revenue
>
> **The funnel (Analytics > "Conversion over time" / funnel)**
> - Added to cart rate (sessions that added to cart)
> - Reached checkout rate
> - Checkout completion / sessions converted rate
>
> **Device split (Reports > Sessions by device)**
> - Sessions and conversion rate for **mobile vs desktop** separately (this is where most leaks hide)
>
> **Products and pages**
> - Top 5 products by units and by revenue
> - Top 5 landing pages by sessions, with the conversion rate of each
>
> **Customers**
> - New against returning customers: share of orders
> - Return or refund rate, and the top reasons if you record them
> - If you sell a subscription: the share of orders on subscription
>
> **Context**
> - The date window, and how traffic and conversion rate are trending against last year (up or down, roughly)
>
> Paste whatever you have. If a number is unavailable, say so and I will mark it as a gap rather than guess.

## On receipt

1. Save the raw figures word for word to `{brand-brain}/shopify-funnel-data.md`, with the date window.
2. Compute and append the derived figures: revenue per session, the mobile against desktop completion gap, where the largest drop sits in the funnel, and recoverable revenue per month under a stated assumption (e.g. "if mobile checkout completion rose from 28% to 38%"). Label it a model, not a forecast.
3. Mark any uncertain or missing figure clearly. Never silently fill a gap.

## What each number adds (say this in a page-only audit)

| Number | What the audit can say with it, and cannot without it |
|---|---|
| Sessions, conversion rate, AOV | The size of any leak in money. Without them: a ranking, no money |
| Mobile against desktop | Whether the phone is where the loss is. Without it: phone first on principle |
| Funnel rates | Which step loses most (product page, cart or checkout). Without them: each surface is judged on its own |
| Landing pages by sessions | Which page to fix first. Without it: the pages the ads point to |
| New against returning | Whether a low rate is a page problem or a traffic mix. Without it: not said |
| Return rate | Whether a page that converts is over-promising. Without it: not said |

## Minimum for sized mode

At least **sessions, overall conversion rate, AOV, and the mobile against desktop split.** With a subset, take what there is, mark the rest as gaps, and say which conclusions are therefore directional. With none of the 4, the audit is page-only.
