# Creator brief: the template

The brief a real creator films from. Keep the section order. Write from the approved script and delivery requirements: selected hooks, duration, aspect ratios, extra shots, raw-file needs, deadline and agreed usage. Confirm missing requirements; 9:16 is a suggestion until selected. Do not import another brief's clip count, deadline or testimonial claims.

A brief never asks a creator to say a result they did not have, and never asks them to copy another channel's video. A reference, if one is given, is the brand's own earlier ad or a description in words.

---

## Concept

3 to 5 sentences: who is talking, where, what happens, what the viewer is left thinking. Then 1 line on why it should read as a person's post and not an advert.

## 1) Hooks

- Film the approved, selected hooks. Each must join naturally to this body; omit unselected drafts.
- Each hook is a different lever, not the same line reworded.
- Start at the approved opening. No greeting. Show the product when the concept calls for it; keep an indirect opening intact, and never exaggerate a worn product's scale to force it into view.
- A hook in the first person is filmed only if it is true of you. If it is not, skip it and tell us.

| Hook | Say | Also do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "[line]" | [where you are, what your hands are doing, the on-screen text the editor will add] |
| 2 | "[line]" | |
| 3 | "[line]" | |

## 2) Body

- Say it your way. The beats are the rail; the words are yours. If a line is not something you would say, change it and keep the point.
- Where you see square brackets, put in your own true detail. If you have none, leave that beat out and tell us.
- Do not say: [the brand's Never say list], or any result, health or money claim that is not written here.
- Length: about [N] seconds.

| Beat | Points |
|---|---|
| Setup | [the doubt or the problem, in the buyer's words] / [what you tried before] |
| Demonstration | [the product in use, step by step, as it is really used] / [the 1 thing to show closely] |
| The number | [the 1 specific number, exactly as written] |
| Turn | [your honest result, said smaller than you feel it] |
| Close | [what to do and what happens next]. Say it like you are telling a friend. Do not say "link below" unless asked |

## 3) Extra shots

No talking. Clean, well lit, no seasonal decorations.

- [ ] The product on the surface where you use it
- [ ] Picking it up
- [ ] Opening it, the way it really opens: [from the lessons]
- [ ] Using it, close: [the 1 action]
- [ ] The result, if there is one to see
- [ ] The product held to the lens, name readable
- [ ] [1 or 2 shots particular to this concept]

## 4) Filming

| | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| Framing | Agreed aspect ratio(s), with space for the planned crop and captions. Eye level when appropriate | Cut off your head or the product |
| Light | Face a window or a lamp | Film with a window behind you |
| Sound | A quiet room. Speak clearly | Music, a fan, a television |
| Phone | Clean the lens. Use agreed capture settings; check the editor's color workflow before choosing HDR | Filters, beauty mode |
| Product | Name to the lens, fingers off the name | Other brands' logos in the frame |
| Clothes | Plain, no words or logos. A different top for the extra shots | Anything with another brand on it |
| Editing | Send raw files | Captions, cuts, transitions, music |

## 5) Sending files

- Raw files only, in 3 folders: Hooks, Body, Extra shots.
- Names: "Hook 1", "Hook 2"; "Body"; "Extra 1 opening the jar".
- With the files: 1 line saying which hook felt most like you, and anything in the brief you would not say.
- Leave a little stillness before and after each take for the editor. List missing takes and unresolved factual lines; never fill them with invented experience.
- State agreed paid/organic use, duration, channels and partnership-ad permissions. Filming or public posting alone does not establish those rights. The brief sends and publishes nothing.

## Filled-in example: a consumable (made-up brand)

Concept: she is at the bathroom shelf at night, mid-routine. She says she has 4 half-used moisturisers and shows them. She uses the new one. The turn is small: it is the first jar she has finished. Close: where she got it.

| Hook | Say | Also do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I counted. There are 4 half-used moisturisers on this shelf." | Phone propped. 4 jars lined up. The product at the end of the row |
| 2 | "Why do I keep buying a new one before I finish the old one?" | Holding 2 old jars, looking at them, not at the lens |
| 3 | "This is the first jar I have ever finished." | The empty jar tipped to the lens |

Body: Setup, [which ones you gave up on, and why]. Demonstration, [the actual opening and instructed amount/application]. The number, [how many weeks the jar lasted you, if known]. Turn, [what is different, said plainly]. Close, "It's on their site. I'd start with the small one."

## Filled-in example: a durable (made-up brand)

Concept: he is packing in a hallway, running late. The case shuts on the first try. The turn is small: nothing happened at the airport, and that is the point. Close: the guarantee, said in passing.

| Hook | Say | Also do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "My last case burst open on the belt. This is why I changed." | Kneeling by the open case, 1 hand on the lid |
| 2 | "Watch this latch." | Close on the latch, your hand already on it |
| 3 | "If you sit on your suitcase to close it, this is for you." | Standing, the case beside you, shut |

Body: Setup, [what happened with your old case]. Demonstration, [the real closing mechanism], the case stood up and rolled. The number, [how many trips so far, if known]. Turn, [what did not go wrong]. Close, [actual warranty terms, if approved], said in passing.
