**Words on a picture.**

- **Each line is a plain sentence, or a phrase a person really says,** with its small words left in ("the", "you", "so"). A customer's own words, word for word, are the best line there is.
- **It makes sense alone.** A stranger who reads only this line and sees the picture knows what is meant. No made-up term, no label from the plan, nothing to decode.
- **It agrees with the picture** and says what the picture cannot. It never labels what is already shown and never needs a different picture.
- **1 idea on the whole picture.** The first line says what the buyer gets, or names their moment in their words. Any other line answers 1 doubt about it, as a sentence.
- **The picture is a moment in a story.** The words say what is happening to this person, or what just changed for them. A line that could sit on any picture belongs on none.
- **When the space is small, say less, not the same thing squeezed.** Pick a smaller idea that fits as a sentence. Never drop the small words, never a row of 1 or 2 word sentences.
- A price, an amount, a guarantee term or a button is a label: a phrase is fine.
- **Test:** hold up the picture and say the line to a friend. If they would ask "what do you mean?", write it again.
