# Arc: the reluctant believer

The narrator had hoped before and been let down, and didn't want to hope again.

- **Stance:** guarded. They almost dismissed the answer because it sounded like every other one.
- **The weight of it leads with:** the thing tried or bought that hurt the most. Not the most expensive: the one they had the most hope for.
- **The reframe is framed as:** the thing they nearly dismissed. They read it, closed it, and came back.
- **How they found the product:** someone they trust mentioned it, or they kept the page open for days. They ordered while telling themselves it probably wouldn't work.
- **Tone:** wary, then slowly warmer. The narrator argues with themselves on the page.
- **First experience matters most:** waiting for it to fail, and it not failing.
- **Closing line leans on:** being glad they were wrong.
- **Feel:** fear of being let down into relief.
- **Care:** this arc leans on the guarantee or the returns terms if the brand has them, said exactly as the brand's facts give them: they are what let the narrator try without having to believe. With none on record, the narrator simply risked it.
