# Arc: quietly angry

The narrator was sold things that promised a lot and delivered little, and is done being polite about it.

- **Stance:** controlled anger. Not ranting: precise.
- **The weight of it leads with:** the thing tried or bought that made the biggest promise and delivered the least. The weight is the specific expectation that was betrayed, and what it cost.
- **The reframe is framed as:** the thing nobody selling in this category mentions, because saying it would undermine everything else on the shelf.
- **How they found the product:** sceptically, checking the claims against the reframe before believing any of it.
- **Tone:** dry, exact, a little sharp. Numbers and specifics instead of adjectives.
- **Closing line leans on:** "I'm not making you a promise. I'm telling you a true thing that happened."
- **Feel:** anger into matter-of-fact.
- **Care:** anger at kinds of product and at being oversold, never at a named brand or person. Every criticism of the category is one the brand can support.
