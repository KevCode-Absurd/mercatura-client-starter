# Arc: the self-blamer

When everything failed, the narrator concluded that they were the problem.

- **Stance:** ashamed, privately. They assumed they weren't disciplined enough, consistent enough, careful enough: bad at the routine, or bad at looking after things.
- **The weight of it leads with:** the thing tried that they worked hardest at, the one they gave everything to. The weight is not that it failed. It is what they decided about themselves when it did.
- **The reframe is framed as:** evidence that it was never their fault. Not comfort: a structural explanation of why effort couldn't have fixed it.
- **How they found the product:** cautiously, half expecting to fail at this too.
- **Tone:** gentle and honest. Uses what the research shows people feel and don't say aloud.
- **Closing line leans on:** being let off the hook. "I hadn't been doing it wrong."
- **Feel:** shame into relief, ending on pride.
- **Care:** the narrator blames themselves. The story never blames the reader, and never tells the reader what is wrong with them.
