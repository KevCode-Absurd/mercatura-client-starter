# Arc: exhausted surrender

The narrator had given up. They weren't looking any more when the explanation reached them.

- **Stance:** tired, resigned. They had accepted this as how things are now.
- **The opening is:** the day they stopped: stopped the routine, stopped looking, stopped replacing it and made do.
- **The weight of it leads with:** the thing tried that cost the most time, the one they stuck with longest before they finally quit.
- **The reframe is framed as:** the explanation that found them after they had stopped looking: something overheard, something a friend sent, something they nearly scrolled past.
- **How they found the product:** without hope. They tried it expecting nothing.
- **Tone:** low and flat at the start, lifting slowly. No excitement even at the end; surprise instead.
- **Closing line leans on:** not having believed that ordinary was still available to them.
- **Feel:** resignation into relief.
