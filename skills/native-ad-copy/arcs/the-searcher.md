# Arc: the searcher

The lead arc. The narrator kept looking after everything failed, and found the answer through their own research.

- **Stance:** determined, a little worn. They did everything right and it still didn't work, so they went looking for why.
- **The weight of it leads with:** the full run of things tried or bought, in order, each done properly.
- **The reframe is framed as:** the thing they found late at night, the kind of reading you do when you've stopped expecting an answer and can't stop looking.
- **How they found the product:** by searching for something built on the reframe, and reading the page several times before ordering.
- **Tone:** even and factual. The relief is quiet.
- **Closing line leans on:** "It turns out the problem was never me."
- **Feel:** frustration into relief.
