# Form: personal journey

The narrator's own history with the problem, from when it started to the day it changed. The standard form.

- **Opens with:** How long they've had it, stated flatly. "My skin has stung after every wash since I was 14. That was 24 years ago." "I've bought 6 frying pans since 2015. I counted them last week."
- **Order:** Opening, the weight of it, the breaking point, the reframe, the bridge, first experience, what's in it and why (optional), what changed, "I know what you're thinking", the close.
- **Carried by:** The length of the struggle. Years, attempts and money, counted.
- **Lengths it suits:** standard, long
- **A product bought once:** the history is the ones bought before, each with what it cost and how it ended.
- **Closes with:** "The link is below. Go read the reviews from people who were where you are. Then decide."

## Care

- With a composite narrator the history is built from the research: the durations and the things tried are ones the quotes mention.
- What was tried is named as a kind of thing, not a brand, and nothing says it is unsafe.
- "Go read the reviews" is written only when the page it leads to has real reviews.
