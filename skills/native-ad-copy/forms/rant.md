# Form: a rant

Anger at the advice everyone gives, said fast and plainly, on behalf of everyone who has followed it.

- **Opens with:** "I'm so sick of being told to [the usual advice]." Then: "It doesn't work, not for people like us." "I'm so sick of being told to drink more water." "I'm so sick of being told to just season it properly."
- **Order:** Opening, everything they did, told quickly (1 sentence each, with what it cost and the one bitter detail), the moment they stopped believing the advice, the reframe as the thing nobody mentioned, a short bridge, the close.
- **Carried by:** Heat and recognition. Whole sentences the way an angry person talks to a friend, quick and joined by "and" and "but", never a row of fragments. Humour with an edge.
- **Lengths it suits:** short, standard
- **A product bought once:** the list is the ones they bought, each with what it cost and how it died.
- **Closes with:** "Read it before one more person tells you to [the usual advice]." Usually leads to an article.

## Care

- The anger is at generic advice and at being oversold, never at named people, professions or brands.
- Every item in the list is something the research mentions. A price is said only as the quotes say it.
- Swearing only if the brand's voice allows it; otherwise the same heat without it.
- Pairs with the quietly angry arc.
