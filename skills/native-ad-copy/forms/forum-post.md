# Form: a forum post

Written as a long post to people with the same problem, with an update added at the end.

- **Opens with:** A correction to what they believed. "For 8 years I thought I just had sensitive skin. Turns out that was never the real problem." "For 10 years I thought I was bad at frying eggs. It was never me."
- **Order:** Opening, what they believed and did for years (the weight of it, with costs added up), the breaking point, the reframe in detail, the bridge, first experience week by week, what changed, the close, then an EDIT.
- **Carried by:** Detail. It reads as someone reporting back to people like them, costs and dates included.
- **Lengths it suits:** long
- **A product bought once:** week by week becomes use by use: the first meal, the tenth, what it looks like now.
- **Closes with:** "EDIT, because someone will ask where I got it:" and the answer, which points to the brand's own page.

## Care

- It is addressed to "anyone else who...", never set on a named forum, group or site, and it claims no replies, votes or messages that did not happen. "A lot of you asked" is not written.
- Costs and durations are plausible totals of things the research mentions, and are the narrator's own, never a figure about the reader.
- Never says a diagnosis, a doctor or another professional was wrong. "Nobody had explained this to me" is as far as it goes.
- No claim that products sold elsewhere are fake or failed tests unless the brand's material documents it.
