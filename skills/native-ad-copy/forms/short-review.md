# Form: a short review

Reads like a detailed review: what the problem was, what they nearly did, what happened.

- **Opens with:** The problem and what they were about to do about it. "My face stung every time I washed it. I was about to pay for a fourth cream I didn't believe in." "I'd thrown out 4 pans. I had the same cheap one in my basket again."
- **Order:** Opening, first use, what changed over the first weeks, a small proof (a second one bought, a friend who ordered), 1 honest limit ("it isn't perfect"), the close.
- **Carried by:** Plain testimony. No reframe passage: the mechanism is 1 sentence at most.
- **Lengths it suits:** short
- **A product bought once:** the proof is the first use and what it replaced, not weeks of results.
- **Closes with:** "If you're on the fence, I was too." Then the link.

## Care

- A review is 1 real person's account. This form is written in the 1st person only from a real customer's review or story in the research: their words kept, their details, their honest limit. If the brand has not recorded that customer's say, no name is used and the notes flag it.
- With no such review, do not write a made-up one. Write it as the observer: what people who bought it say, each real quote word for word with where it came from, and the honest limit as customers put it. Say in the notes that the form was changed and why.
- It is never laid out as a review on a named site, and the copy carries no star rating.
