# Founder as a still image: 1 decision, signed

Judged on 1 thing: a stranger reads 1 decision in the founder's own words and believes a person stands behind it.

## The frame

Pick the 1 that the brand's material allows. Never generate the founder's face, body or hands.

- **A real photo is on file or uploaded:** the photo is the picture, used as it is: the founder where the product is made, packed or tested. The words are laid beside the face, never over it. It is not retouched, restaged or extended.
- **No photo:** a note from the founder. Plain paper or a card on the bench where the product is made, the product beside it, with the thing the decision is about in view (the rejected samples, the raw material, the part that costs more). No person in the frame.
- **The decision as the picture:** the product cut open or taken apart to show the 1 thing that was chosen, with the founder's line beside it.

Composition: 1 subject, 1 block of words, the product real and sharp. It should look like something the brand's owner put out, not a campaign.

## Words on the picture

- **1 decision, in the 1st person,** 25 words or fewer, in the founder's own phrasing where it exists: what was chosen or turned down, and what that means for the buyer ("We turned down 11 samples. The 12th is the one that didn't pill.").
- Under it, the founder's real name and real role, small. No name on file: "[founder's name and role]" stays as a placeholder and is listed in the notes.
- A guarantee in its real terms may stand as a second, smaller line. A button only where there is an offer. 35 words in all.
- A fact that is not on file is a prompt in square brackets, never a guess.

## For the image prompt

- With a real photo: say that the attached photo of the founder is used exactly as it is, as the picture, and that the person in it is not redrawn, relit or altered. Describe only the type layer: where the words sit, their size and colour.
- With no photo: a real photograph of a work surface in daylight: the paper's grain, a pen beside it, the product from its photo with its printing sharp. The note's words are given exactly; a handwritten signature is not invented.
- **Type, the look's own:** on a note, the decision and the name under it are typed on the paper: a plain typewriter face or a plain book serif, in dark ink. A guarantee line, a button, and every word laid beside a real photo are set in the brand's typefaces.
- **Type, the brand's:** on a note, the decision and the name under it are the brand's body typeface printed on the paper in dark ink: its weight, width and letter shapes kept. Never the brand's small tag face for the note. Every other word is set in the brand's typefaces as they are.
- No person is generated. No hands, no silhouette, no figure out of focus in the background.
- Plain, unstyled, slightly imperfect: no campaign gloss, no gradient, no badge.

## What fails in a still

- A generated founder, or a stock-looking person captioned as one.
- A mission statement ("We believe in better mornings") where a decision should be.
- A quote the founder never said.
- 3 decisions on 1 image: 1, told with its reason.
