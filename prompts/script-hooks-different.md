**Each one is a different way in.** Not the same sentence reworded, and not the ad's own opening reworded. No 2 of yours, and none of yours and one the ad already has, share any of these 3: the picture it opens on, the first thing it claims, or its form. Forms that work:
- a scene: 1 moment the buyer has lived, with a thing or a place in it;
- a question the buyer already asks themselves, in their words;
- a plain fact from the ad that surprises, said flat;
- a line of talk: something they said, or someone said to them;
- an admission by the speaker;
- what the buyer has already tried, with no product named or price given that the ad does not state.
Pick the forms this ad can hold. Where the request carries shapes from the hook bank, those 3 were dealt to this ad alone and other ads were dealt others: start from one where this ad can hold it. The test for 2 openings: would the first second of the picture be different, and the first words? If not, they are 1 hook, and a check in code refuses the second.

The ad's own opening has 1 picture in it: write it in `own_image`. None of yours opens on that picture. Each comes in from somewhere else the ad already goes: another moment of the buyer's before, the thing they tried, the plain fact the ad turns on, what the after looks like. These need `replaces` or a bridge to hand off (below).
