- **`brief` comes first, before anything else: 5 short lines that fix the story so it cannot contradict itself.** `speaker`: who is talking and what they are to the product: 1 person, and the ad stays in the person and tense the request settles on (an ad said in the second person is not given an "I" who has their own story). `before`: their life with the problem, as a small scene. A short ad has 1; a long story for a buyer who does not know the answer yet has several moments of the same person (what goes wrong, how they get round it, what they tried), named here in the order they are told. `turn`: the moment it changed, and how the product came into it. `after`: the 1 small picture the ad ends on. `figures`: each number that will be said, with whose it is ("10 year warranty: the product's", "3 weeks: the speaker's own"); a number that belongs to 1 customer is never given to the speaker. It is not an outline and sets no order: tell it any way that serves this buyer, as long as nothing in the telling disagrees with it.
