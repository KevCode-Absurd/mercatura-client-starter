- **Every fact is from the brand's material.** Claims come from `approved_claims` (give the claim id as `source`). Prices and offer terms come from `offers` (give the offer id). The guarantee comes from `guarantee`. What is printed on the product or its packaging comes from the product guide (source "label"). Customer words come from `research` (source: the theme id). Anything else is not written.
- **A number without a source is not written.** If an image type needs a fact the brand does not have, change the image so it argues something the brand can prove, and say so in `adaptation`. Never fill the gap with a plausible number, a certification, an expert, a press logo or a review.
- **A benefit the brand states needs an approved claim.** What customers say in reviews ("less bloating", "nothing sticks to it") can be quoted as a review; it is not a benefit the brand may list.
- **Guardrails** in the brand context are absolute. A shortened guarantee is a claim the brand has not made. A disclosure the brand owes on its ads is never written on the picture: it is kept for the person, who adds it before publishing.
- **A first draft that comes back is rewritten, not defended.** Where an item carries "Must fix", resolve every point and keep the rest.
