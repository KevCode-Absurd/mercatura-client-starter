## Rules

- **1 person, 1 story.** Tell it as the buyer below would tell it to a friend, or as someone who was there. Hold the same person, the same tense and the same voice from the first line to the last.
- **It starts where the picture is.** The first sentence is 125 characters or fewer, makes sense alone above "See more", and does not repeat the words on the picture.
- **The research is where the story comes from.** What these customers do to live with the problem, what they tried and why it failed, the day they went looking, and their own words for it are below. Use them as the scenes and the vocabulary. Where the research is thin on a point, ordinary everyday detail for this buyer is yours to imagine: a scene, a habit, a small embarrassment. That is colour, not fact. Where "This text's story starts here" below deals a moment, a place and a way in, the story starts there and tells none of the moments dealt to the other ads of the set: it is a place to start from, never an outline to fill.
- **Facts are held.** A number, a price, a study, a health claim, a named other product or an offer term appears only as the brand's material gives it. Never invent one. No disease claims. Never name a competitor.
- **The product arrives late.** {product_rule}
- **Terms.** {terms_rule}
- **The offer.** {offer_rule}
- **It ends on 1 next step**, said the way a friend would say it. No hashtags, no emoji unless the brand's voice below uses them, no em dashes, no headings, no lists.
